Quick Jump to Position:

ST-01 General Manager (GM)

Objective & Scope

Serves as the single internal authority for the entire resort. Holds final approval over hiring, promotion, discipline, emergency authorization, and inter-department grievances. Owns the master view of all eight SOP modules and is the top node of the organization chart.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: Owner / Management Contract (external authority).
  • Directly supervises: every Department Head and Duty Supervisor (ST-02 through ST-09).
  • Final-appeal authority: unresolved grievances escalate to the GM after the immediate supervisor and Department Head steps (SOP-HR grievance chain).

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Human Resources & Hiring:
    • Conducts the final interview for supervisory and head roles and approves all hiring offers (SOP-HR-01).
    • Approves regularization, merit increases, and promotion proposals (SOP-HR evaluation cycle).
  2. Emergency & Operations:
    • Grants final approval for high-volume or emergency overtime authorization.
    • Delivers the welcome address and articulates the resort vision/mission to new staff.
  3. Discipline & Review:
    • Reviews panel findings and approves major disciplinary actions (suspension, termination).
    • Acts as final internal appeal authority for unresolved grievances.

Localized Context

At Gamay's coastal provincial site, the GM keeps a physical emergency binder (arrivals, departures, in-house lists, typhoon response) so decisions remain possible during grid-power or internet outages. The GM is the designated point of contact for SAMELCO load-shedding and Starlink/4G network failures.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Every supervisory hire carries a GM-signed approval memo.
  • Grievance chain never bypasses the GM for a final decision.
  • Emergency binder current and reachable within arm's reach of the office.
  • Headcount achievable in under 15 minutes during a crisis (SOP-FO-24).
ST-02 Duty Manager / Front Office Supervisor

Objective & Scope

Runs the front desk shift and owns guest-facing operations end to end: reservations intake, check-in/out, cashiering, night audit, and emergency communication. The operational bridge between the GM and the Front Office associates.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: FO Associates, Reservations Clerk, Night Auditor.
  • Peer heads: HK Head (ST-03), F&B Head (ST-04) — coordinate on lost & found and guest transfers.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Guest Cycle:
    • Owns reservations intake, confirmation, guest check-in, walk-in settlement, and check-out (SOP-FO-02 to FO-14).
    • Manages room assignment with zero double-allocations and access-control key handling (SOP-FO-07, FO-08).
  2. Finance & Audit:
    • Oversees cashiering, credit accounts, deposits/refunds, and no-show re-release (SOP-FO-15 to FO-18).
    • Signs off the shift cash float and the Night Audit reconciliation (SOP-FO-21, FO-22).
  3. Service Recovery & Emergency:
    • Resolves guest complaints within the 30-minute SLA (SOP-FO-11).
    • Runs emergency communication and headcount with the GM (SOP-FO-24).

Localized Context

Confirms grid-power (SAMELCO I) and ATS/generator standby each shift, and keeps printed arrival/departure lists in the emergency binder for provincial load-shedding or internet lag. Verifies primary and backup satellite/LTE connections before opening the desk.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Check-in under 5 minutes; check-out under 4 minutes.
  • Zero cash-drawer variance at shift close.
  • EOD night-audit report sent by 05:00 AM.
  • Front desk and lobby neatness score 100%.
ST-03 Housekeeping Department Head

Objective & Scope

Owns room cleaning standards, linen and laundry operations, public-area upkeep, and pest control across the 30-room resort. Coordinates lost & found handoff with the Front Office.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Room Attendants, Laundry crew, Public-Area Attendants.
  • Coordinates with: Duty Manager (ST-02) on lost & found and room-transfer packing.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Rooms & Linen:
    • Enforces room cleaning standards and bed-making drill for new hires (SOP-HK practical test).
    • Manages linen par levels, towel rotation, and minibar restock.
  2. Laundry & Public Areas:
    • Runs laundry operations and public-area cleaning schedules.
    • Owns pest-control protocols for the coastal site.
  3. Lost & Found:
    • Logs found items within 1 hour and hands off to Front Office (SOP-FO-19).

Localized Context

Gamay's coastal humidity and salt air accelerate linen wear and corrosion; the Head schedules extra towel rotation and rust checks on fixtures. Humidity also affects electronic terminals used for housekeeping status reports.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Housekeeping status report current for every occupied room.
  • Linen par levels verified at shift start.
  • Found items logged within 1 hour.
  • Pest-control sweep completed per schedule.
ST-04 Food & Beverage Department Head

Objective & Scope

Owns restaurant and bar operations, food safety, and kitchen hygiene for the resort's restaurant and function-hall catering. The executive authority over the kitchen line and service crews.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Executive Chef, Service Captains, Bar Crew.
  • Coordinates with: Events Coordinator (ST-05) for banquet catering volume.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Kitchen & Safety:
    • Enforces food safety and kitchen hygiene standards across all service.
    • Runs the practical cooking exam for new kitchen hires (SOP-HR test).
  2. Service & Bar:
    • Owns dining service flow and bar operations.
    • Coordinates POS order entry and billing generation with FO cashiering.

Localized Context

Provincial sourcing at Gamay means seafood and produce arrive by local supply runs; the Head enforces cold-chain and holding-time checks on delivery and keeps printed safety standards for power-outage service continuity.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cold-chain and holding-time checks passed on every delivery.
  • Kitchen hygiene inspection logged daily.
  • Banquet volume confirmed with Events Coordinator before service.
ST-05 Function Hall / Events Coordinator

Objective & Scope

Owns event reservations, client consultations, audio-visual staging, and banquet catering for weddings and functions in the hall. Bridges F&B volume with FO billing.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Banquet Captain, AV Technician, Hall Crew.
  • Coordinates with: F&B Head (ST-04) for catering volume and FO (ST-02) for billing.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Booking & Consultation:
    • Runs event reservations and client consultations (SOP-FH-01 to FH-06).
  2. Staging & Catering:
    • Owns audio-visual staging and banquet catering setup (SOP-FH-10).

Localized Context

Coastal wind and typhoon season affect outdoor hall staging; the Coordinator keeps a typhoon-day event cancellation protocol aligned with Module 06 and confirms AV power draw against the standby generator.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Every booking carries a completed client consultation record.
  • AV power draw confirmed within generator capacity.
  • Typhoon-day cancellation protocol reviewed before each event.
ST-06 Facilities & Engineering Lead

Objective & Scope

Owns resort maintenance, swimming-pool water treatment, and engineering repairs. The authority over physical plant, water systems, and the standby generator.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Pool Attendant, Maintenance Technician, Grounds Crew.
  • Coordinates with: Safety Officer (ST-07) on typhoon preparedness of the plant.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Pool & Water:
    • Runs pool water treatment and circulation (SOP-FM).
  2. Plant & Repairs:
    • Owns engineering repairs and pending maintenance tickets reviewed at FO opening.
    • Confirms ATS and standby generator auto-start mode for load-shedding.

Localized Context

SAMELCO grid power and provincial load-shedding make the generator/ATS check the critical daily duty; salt air accelerates corrosion on pool pumps and fixtures, so the Lead schedules rust checks.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Pool water chemistry within range daily.
  • ATS and generator confirmed in auto-start mode.
  • Open maintenance tickets current and reachable by FO.
ST-07 Safety & Security Officer

Objective & Scope

Owns typhoon preparedness, emergency evacuation, and security protocols (Module 06). The authority over disaster response and the guard shift.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Guard Shift Crew, Typhoon Response Team.
  • Coordinates with: Engineering Lead (ST-06) on securing the physical plant.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Typhoon Response:
    • Runs signal-based typhoon preparedness and evacuation (SOP-SS series).
  2. Security & Access:
    • Owns the guard shift and access-control support for FO key handling.

Localized Context

Gamay sits on the coast of Northern Samar and is typhoon-exposed; the Officer maintains the always-cached Module 06 emergency page and the physical emergency binder for offline use during storms.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Typhoon signal protocol reviewed each storm season.
  • Headcount achievable in under 15 minutes in a crisis.
  • Guard shift log complete at shift close.
ST-08 HR & Administration Officer

Objective & Scope

Owns the staff code of conduct, personnel scheduling, job descriptions, and the grievance/leave systems (Module 07). The compliance authority for every position on this page.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01).
  • Directly supervises: Personnel Desk, Compliance Staff.
  • Supports: the GM on hiring screening and job-description audits.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Job Descriptions:
    • Maintains the master job-description database and audits role alignment (SOP-HR JD).
  2. Compliance & Scheduling:
    • Runs time-register clock-in, shift-swap forms, and leave protocols.
    • Files incident reports within 24 hours of an offense.

Localized Context

Biometric clock-in can fail from wet hands or outage at the coastal site; the Officer enforces the Manual Time Register co-signed by the Duty Supervisor within 1 hour as the offline fallback.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Every position on this page has a current job description.
  • Pre-shift grooming lineup logged by shift supervisor.
  • Incident reports filed within 24 hours.
ST-09 IT Administrator / Digital Systems

Objective & Scope

Owns PMS/POS systems, database management, digital records, cybersecurity, and network backup (Module 08). The authority over system administration and access levels.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (ST-01) — Level 4 privilege authority.
  • Directly supervises: PMS Operator, Records & Network Staff.
  • Supports: FO cashiering (POS terminals) and Engineering Lead (network backup).

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Access Control:
    • Enforces role-based access levels (Level 1 staff to Level 4 GM/IT admin).
    • Provisions new user accounts from Department Head request forms.
  2. Network & Records:
    • Owns Starlink primary and 4G LTE backup connectivity management.
    • Maintains digital records and audit-log access.

Localized Context

At Gamay the network runs Starlink primary with 4G LTE backup; the IT Administrator keeps printed SOPs and offline-cached emergency pages so operations survive satellite/LTE lag during storms.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • No Level 1 account has void/discount access.
  • Backup LTE connection verified alongside Starlink.
  • Audit-log access limited to Level 4.
