Module 01: Front Office & Guest Services

SOPs for front desk operations, guest reservations, check-in/out procedures, and guest services.

Quick Jump to Procedure:

FO-01 Front Desk Opening Procedure

Objective & Scope

Establish operational readiness for the Front Desk prior to peak guest traffic, ensuring accounting accuracy, physical cleanliness, system synchronization, and local infrastructure preparedness for the day.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate (Morning Shift)
  • Duty Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Physical Readiness & Security:
    • Unlock the main Front Office drawer/counter and inspect the lobby, reception desk, and guest seating areas for cleanliness and safety.
    • Turn on lobby lights, air conditioning, and guest display screens.
  2. System & Terminal Startup:
    • Power on the Property Management System (PMS) terminal, point-of-sale (POS) card terminals, and receipt printers.
    • Log into the PMS using personal staff credentials.
  3. Cash Float Verification:
    • Retrieve the shift cash drawer/float from the Front Office safe in the presence of the outgoing Night Auditor or Duty Supervisor.
    • Count and verify the standard opening float (₱10,000 in mixed denominations).
    • Sign the Cash Float Logbook detailing denomination counts and variance notes (if any).
  4. Information Review & Handover Audit:
    • Review the Night Audit Handover Log, unhandled guest requests from the previous night, and pending maintenance tickets.
    • Print or generate digital daily reports: Arrivals List, Departures List, In-House Guest List, and Housekeeping Status Report.
  5. Local Infrastructure & Power Check:
    • Verify grid power status (SAMELCO I). Confirm that the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) and standby generator unit are set to auto-start mode in case of provincial load shedding.
    • Check local internet connectivity (primary and backup satellite/LTE connections). Test local landline/mobile reception numbers.

Localized Context

Given Gamay’s coastal provincial location, morning high humidity can affect electronic terminals. Ensure counter surfaces and card reader terminals are wiped dry. Keep physical backup printouts of daily arrival lists in the emergency binder to handle potential local grid power interruptions or internet lag.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cash float counted, verified, and logged by 06:00 AM.
  • Daily arrival/departure/in-house reports printed/saved.
  • Generator status and internet connectivity verified.
  • Front desk and lobby neatness score: 100%.
FO-02 Guest Reservation Procedure

Objective & Scope

Ensure standard and efficient handling of room reservation inquiries via phone, email, messaging channels, or walk-in, maximizing occupancy while providing accurate resort details.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Reservations Clerk
  • Front Office Associate

Execution Procedure

  1. Inquiry Intake & Greetings:
    • Answer incoming calls within three (3) rings: "Warm greetings from Marcela's Resort Hotel Gamay! This is [Name], how may I assist your stay today?"
    • Capture caller details: full name, contact number, email, and preferred channel of communication (Viber/WhatsApp/Facebook Messenger).
  2. Needs Analysis & Availability Verification:
    • Identify travel dates, number of adults/children, room preference, and special requirements (e.g., ground floor for elderly guests, sea view, extra beds).
    • Check room availability on the PMS calendar for requested dates.
  3. Rate Quote & Inventory Management:
    • Quote Published Rates, Seasonal Rates, or Corporate Rates following property rate structures.
    • Detail inclusions clearly (breakfast allocation, pool access, environmental/local taxes if applicable).
    • Offer room upgrades or package add-ons (e.g., island tour arrangements, airport transfers from Catarman/Calbayog).
  4. Booking Capture in PMS:
    • Create a new reservation record in the PMS.
    • Input complete data: Guest Name, Contact Number, Email Address, Arrival Date/Time, Departure Date, Room Type, Rate Code, Deposit Terms, and Special Requests.
    • Apply appropriate booking source tags (Direct Call, OTA, Walk-in, Local Government/Corporate).
  5. Payment Guarantee & Deposit Terms:
    • Inform the guest of the reservation deposit policy (50% initial deposit required within 48 hours for regular season; 100% advance payment for peak holidays).
    • Provide accepted payment options: Bank Transfer (BDO/Landbank), GCash, Maya, or Credit Card link.

Localized Context

Guests traveling to Gamay often face travel delays due to weather conditions along the Pacific board, ferry crossing delays, or provincial road conditions. Always ask for expected modes of arrival (e.g., private car via Tacloban/Catarman, public van) to estimate realistic arrival times.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% accurate data entry into PMS (zero misspelled names or mismatched contact numbers).
  • Payment terms clearly communicated and logged in reservation notes.
  • Call answered within 3 rings.
FO-03 Reservation Confirmation & Modification

Objective & Scope

Verify deposit receipts, issue prompt booking confirmation vouchers, and handle reservation changes accurately while upholding resort cancellation and modification policies.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Reservations Clerk
  • Front Office Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Confirmation Issuance:
    • Upon receipt of valid deposit proof (bank receipt/GCash screenshot), verify funds with accounting or payment portal.
    • Post the payment advance into the PMS reservation folio.
    • Generate a formal Booking Confirmation Voucher from the PMS and dispatch via email/messaging app within two (2) hours of payment verification.
  2. Confirmation Content Verification:
    • Ensure voucher explicitly lists: Confirmation Code, Guest Name, Stay Dates, Room Category, Total Booking Amount, Deposit Paid, Balance Due at Check-in, Cancellation Policy, and Resort House Rules.
  3. Processing Reservation Modifications:
    • Receive written or verbal modification requests. Check inventory availability for new dates.
    • Re-calculate total room cost based on new dates or upgraded categories.
    • Issue an updated Revised Confirmation Voucher reflecting date changes, rate adjustments, or additional room charges.
    • Apply modification fees if the request falls within the restricted penalty window (e.g., within 7 days of peak season arrival) as approved by Management.
  4. System Log Maintenance:
    • Log every change in the PMS reservation history, recording staff ID, date, time, and specific reason for alteration.

Localized Context

Typhoon signals or severe ocean swells around Northern Samar frequently disrupt regional travel plans. In cases of official tropical storm warnings (PAGASA Signal No. 1 or higher affecting Samar routes), rebooking fees are waived, and reservation credits are extended for up to 6 months.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Confirmation sent within 2 hours of payment receipt verification.
  • Updated voucher generated for all modifications.
  • Adverse weather exception policies correctly applied.
FO-04 Guest Check-in Procedure

Objective & Scope

Deliver a seamless, welcoming, and efficient check-in experience while completing identity verification, payment settlement, and room orientation within standard SLAs.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Bellman / Resort Host

Execution Procedure

  1. Guest Arrival & Welcome:
    • Greet arriving guests warmly at the entrance or counter: "Welcome to Marcela's Resort Hotel! Good morning/afternoon, [Guest Name]."
    • Offer welcome refreshment (e.g., local chilled calamansi juice) and cold towels.
  2. Registration & Verification:
    • Request reservation name or confirmation code.
    • Pull up the PMS profile and verify stay details (dates, room category, rate plan).
    • Request valid government-issued ID or passport for all adult occupants (refer to SOP-FO-06).
    • Present the pre-printed Guest Registration Form (GRF) for verification and signature.
  3. Payment & Security Deposit Settlement:
    • Review room payment status. Collect remaining stay balance if unpaid.
    • Collect a standard incidentals security deposit (₱1,000 per room night) via cash or credit card pre-authorization.
    • Issue official receipt / acknowledgement slip for cash incidentals.
  4. Room Allocation & Key Delivery:
    • Verify room status in PMS is Inspected & Ready.
    • Key card / physical key programming (refer to SOP-FO-08).
    • Hand over key card packet containing: key cards, breakfast coupons, Wi-Fi access details, and resort map.
  5. Orientation & Escort:
    • Explain key resort amenities, breakfast timings (06:30 AM – 10:00 AM), swimming pool rules, and desk contact numbers.
    • Signal the Bellman/Host to assist guest with luggage and escort them to their assigned room.

FO-05 Walk-in Guest Procedure

Objective & Scope

Convert walk-in inquiries into resort bookings professionally while verifying room readiness and securing 100% upfront payment.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Greeting & Needs Assessment:
    • Welcome the guest warmly and inquire about room requirements (number of guests, preferred room style, duration of stay).
    • Check real-time PMS inventory for immediate room availability.
  2. Room Presentation & Rate Disclosure:
    • If rooms are available, show room photos/brochures on the front desk display, or offer a room viewing if requested and staff level permits.
    • Quote the official Rack Rate or approved daily Walk-In Rate.
  3. Room Readiness Verification:
    • Check PMS room status. If room is Dirty or Vacant Clean (Uninspected), contact Housekeeping immediately via radio/intercom to prioritize inspect/clean cycle.
    • Inform guest of estimated wait time if room is undergoing final inspection (offer complimentary lounge seating and beverage).
  4. Registration & Full Payment Collection:
    • Walk-in guests must pay 100% of room charges plus security deposit upfront prior to key issuance.
    • Complete standard registration procedure (SOP-FO-04 & SOP-FO-06).
  5. No Availability Handling:
    • If resort is fully booked, express sincere regret.
    • Offer assistance in contacting nearby partner accommodations in Gamay or neighboring towns if requested.

Localized Context

Walk-ins in Samar often include provincial government officials, field engineers, or travelers forced to stop due to late-night road closures or heavy downpours. Maintain updated contact details for reputable local guesthouses to support travelers when Marcela's is at 100% occupancy.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% room charge and security deposit collected prior to key release.
  • Housekeeping notified immediately for expedited room preparation.
  • Walk-in conversion logged for daily occupancy statistics.
FO-06 Guest Identification & Registration

Objective & Scope

Ensure compliance with national security, law enforcement, and Data Privacy Act regulations by accurately recording guest identity and maintaining secure registration files.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Duty Manager

Execution Procedure

  1. Identification Requirements:
    • Request valid photo identification from all adult guests (18 years and older) upon check-in.
    • Accepted Documents: Passport, Driver's License, UMID, SSS/GSIS ID, PRC ID, Postal ID, Philippine National ID (PhilID), or Voters ID.
    • For foreign guests: Passport with valid entry visa/stamp is mandatory.
  2. Document Inspection & Scanning:
    • Check ID validity, expiration date, photo match, and name spelling.
    • Scan or photocopy ID (front and back for national IDs; photo page and entry stamp page for passports).
    • Attach electronic copy directly to the guest profile in PMS.
  3. Registration Form Completion:
    • Full legal name matching ID, residential address, nationality.
    • Contact phone number and email address.
    • Departure date and onward destination.
    • Vehicle plate number / model (if parked on property).
    • Emergency contact details.
  4. Data Privacy Compliance:
    • Ensure guest reviews the Data Privacy Clause on the GRF regarding Philippine Data Privacy Act (R.A. 10173) compliance.
    • Obtain signature confirming accuracy and privacy consent.
    • Store physical GRF securely in locked filing drawers; ensure scanned files are restricted to authorized PMS roles.

Localized Context

Local Municipal Tourism Offices in Samar occasionally require demographic guest reports for municipal tourism statistics. Ensure address/province and nationality details are recorded accurately to streamline monthly municipal reporting.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Valid photo ID scanned and attached for 100% of check-ins.
  • Foreign passport entry stamps checked for valid stay duration.
  • Signed privacy consent captured on every GRF.
FO-07 Room Assignment Procedure

Objective & Scope

Optimize room allocations based on guest preferences, VIP designations, accessibility needs, and operational maintenance schedules while avoiding double-booking errors.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Supervisor
  • Front Office Associate

Execution Procedure

  1. Pre-Assignment Routine (Morning Audit):
    • Review daily arrival manifest by 07:00 AM.
    • Identify VIP arrivals, return guests, group bookings, extended stays, and specific guest request tags (e.g., quiet zone, ground floor access, adjoining rooms).
  2. Assignment Rules & Prioritization:
    • VIPs & Long-stay Guests: Assign highest-tier rooms or best-view locations first.
    • Elderly Guests / Persons with Disability (PWD): Assign ground floor rooms or rooms nearest to elevator/main amenities.
    • Group Bookings: Block contiguous rooms on the same floor/wing to simplify service.
    • Maintenance Rotation: Avoid assigning same rooms repeatedly; distribute wear evenly across room inventory.
  3. Status Verification:
    • Cross-check PMS status with Housekeeping physical inspection reports.
    • Rule: Never assign a room marked Dirty or Out of Order (OOO). Assign only Inspected Clean inventory.
  4. Blocking & Locking in PMS:
    • Lock room assignments in PMS by 11:00 AM for same-day arrivals.
    • Place operational blocks for rooms requiring scheduled maintenance or deep cleaning.
  5. Handling Pre-assignment Changes:
    • If an assigned room suffers an unexpected maintenance issue (e.g., AC malfunction), re-assign to an equivalent or upgraded category immediately. Log the issue in maintenance ticketing.

Localized Context

Resort layouts in Samar may involve garden paths or outdoor corridors exposed to rain. Assign elderly guests or families with infants to main building rooms or covered-walkway sections during rainy monsoon seasons (November to February).

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero room assignment conflicts or double-bookings.
  • Special requests fulfilled for at least 95% of reservations.
  • PWD and elderly access preferences 100% honored.
FO-08 Key/Card/Room Access Control

Objective & Scope

Control key card encoding, storage, and issuance to protect guest safety, maintain physical room security, and track master key custody strictly.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Duty Manager / Security Chief

Execution Procedure

  1. Key Card Encoding at Check-In:
    • Encode RFID key cards using the hotel key encoder linked to the PMS.
    • Encode precise check-in and check-out date/time limits.
    • Program key card permissions: Room Access, Main Gate Access, Pool Area, Night Entrance.
    • Never write the room number directly on the physical key card. Write room number only on the secure key card holder booklet.
  2. Replacement & Duplicate Keys:
    • Re-verify guest ID before issuing replacement or duplicate key cards.
    • Verify guest name against PMS profile.
    • Invalidate previously issued lost key cards in system before issuing new card.
  3. Master Key Management:
    • Master Keys (Grand Master, Emergency Master, Floor Master) must be stored in a secured key safe at the Front Office.
    • Master keys are issued only to authorized staff (Duty Manager, Head Housekeeper, Security Chief) via signed Key Logbook.
    • Audit master key custody at every shift change.
  4. Emergency / Manual Key Access:
    • For physical key cylinder overrides during power/encoder failures, obtain Duty Manager written clearance.
    • Record every physical key extraction with timestamp, reason, staff ID, and guest room affected.

Localized Context

During extended regional power outages where encoder backup battery fails, switch to manual lock override kits stored in the Front Office safe under double-custody locks. Always verify guest identity manually against physical GRFs.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero room numbers written directly on key card surfaces.
  • 100% ID verification required for key card re-issuance.
  • Master key logbook signed and reconciled at every shift change.

FO-11 Guest Complaint & Service Recovery

Objective & Scope

Resolve guest dissatisfaction promptly using the LAST framework (Listen, Apologize, Solve, Thank) and authorized empowerment matrices to retain guest goodwill.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Duty Supervisor
  • General Manager

Execution Procedure

  1. Active Listening (LAST Step 1):
    • Listen without interruption. Maintain professional eye contact and open body language.
    • Take notes on specific details. Move conversation to a private office if guest is vocal in the lobby.
  2. Sincere Apology (LAST Step 2):
    • Offer an empathetic, non-defensive apology on behalf of the resort: "I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience this has caused you, [Guest Name]. This is certainly not the level of experience we aim to provide."
  3. Problem Solving & Empowerment Matrix (LAST Step 3):
    • Level 1 (Front Office Associate - up to ₱1,000 value): Free beverage vouchers, late check-out extension (up to 2 hours), complimentary laundry item.
    • Level 2 (Duty Supervisor - up to ₱3,500 value): Complimentary meal voucher, room category upgrade for remaining stay, 1-night room charge discount.
    • Level 3 (General Manager - above ₱3,500 value): Full night room waiver, future stay voucher, complete bill adjustment.
  4. Execution & Thanking (LAST Step 4):
    • Implement agreed solution immediately.
    • Thank guest for bringing the issue to light: "Thank you for pointing this out to us, as it helps us improve our service."
  5. Documentation & Root Cause Analysis:
    • File an official Incident & Service Recovery Report before end of shift. Log details in guest profile for future stay alerts.

Localized Context

Local issues like brief power glitches or insect presence near tropical vegetation require proactive explanation rather than defensive excuses. Offer insect repellant sprays, immediate room fogging coordination, or prompt room transfers.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Guest complaints resolved within 30 minutes of notification.
  • Incident report filed for 100% of complaints involving compensation.
  • Zero unresolved complaints escalated past Duty Manager without GM notice.
FO-12 Room Transfer Procedure

Objective & Scope

Execute guest room changes smoothly, ensuring guest comfort, item security, key updates, and clear departmental notifications.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Duty Manager / Front Office Associate
  • Housekeeping & Luggage Host

Execution Procedure

  1. Transfer Request Approval & System Update:
    • Identify reason for transfer (maintenance issue, noise complaint, category upgrade request).
    • Check PMS availability for suitable clean room of equal or higher category.
    • Update PMS with new room assignment details, maintaining original billing rates (unless paid upgrade).
  2. Guest Coordination:
    • Inform guest of transfer arrangements and agreed timing.
    • If guest is present: Bellman assists in packing (if requested) and moving luggage directly to new room.
    • If guest is absent (Emergency Transfer only): Duty Manager and Security Officer must enter room, pack guest belongings under dual-witness supervision, complete item inventory sheet, move items to new room, and re-lock room securely.
  3. Key Card Re-issuance & System Handover:
    • Issue new encoded key cards for new room. Retrieve and clear old key cards.
    • Inform Housekeeping and In-Room Dining/F&B immediately of room change to redirect pending deliveries or room charge posts.
  4. Post-Transfer Verification:
    • Inspect old room to ensure no guest belongings were left behind.
    • Set old room PMS status to Dirty / Touch-up required or Out of Order.

Localized Context

In resort environments, transferring between garden cabins and beachfront suites involves outdoor cart or foot transport. Ensure luggage trolley covers are used during overcast or rainy conditions in Gamay.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Dual-witness supervision and inventory form completed for all absent guest transfers.
  • Key cards updated and old cards deactivated immediately.
  • F&B and Housekeeping notified of new room assignment within 10 minutes.
FO-13 Early Check-in / Late Check-out

Objective & Scope

Manage requests for arrivals before standard check-in or departures after standard check-out while maintaining inventory control and billing integrity.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Duty Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Standard Operational Hours:
    • Check-in Time: 02:00 PM (14:00).
    • Check-out Time: 12:00 PM (12:00 noon).
  2. Early Check-in Handling:
    • Arrival before 06:00 AM: Charge 100% of previous night rate (subject to availability).
    • Arrival between 06:00 AM and 10:00 AM: Charge 50% of published day rate (subject to clean room availability).
    • Arrival between 10:00 AM and 02:00 PM: Complimentary early check-in if room is Inspected Clean and approved by Duty Supervisor.
    • If room is not ready: Tag luggage for secure storage at Front Office, issue bag claim tags, offer guest resort amenity access (pool, changing rooms, restaurant).
  3. Late Check-out Handling:
    • Request must be evaluated based on same-day incoming arrivals for that specific room.
    • Departure between 12:00 PM and 02:00 PM: Complimentary extension subject to Supervisor approval.
    • Departure between 02:00 PM and 06:00 PM: Charge 50% of published room rate.
    • Departure after 06:00 PM: Charge 100% of daily room rate.
  4. System Billing Input:
    • Post early check-in or late check-out fees directly to guest PMS folio before key extension/issuance.

Localized Context

Guests dependent on fixed provincial van schedules or long distance road trips often request flexible departure times. Always offer bag storage and changing room usage as a hospitable alternative when late room extension is impossible due to 100% full house occupancy.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Fees correctly billed in PMS according to hour brackets.
  • Housekeeping alerted immediately to adjust room priority list.
  • Luggage storage receipts issued for early/late arrivals waiting in public areas.

FO-15 Cashiering & Payment Collection

Objective & Scope

Maintain financial accountability, process cash, card, and digital payment transactions accurately, and perform tamper-evident shift cash drops.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate / Cashier
  • Duty Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Payment Acceptance Protocol:
    • Cash: Count cash in clear view of guest. Verify note authenticity under UV cash detector. State amount received aloud: "I have received ₱3,000." Hand over change and official receipt immediately.
    • Credit/Debit Card: Insert/Tap card on POS terminal. Ensure currency charged is PHP. Ensure authorization slip prints successfully and is signed by guest (for swipe/chip signature transactions). Compare signature against back of card/ID.
    • Digital Wallets (GCash/Maya): Display official Marcela's QR code stand. Guest scans and executes transaction. Associate must verify payment confirmation screen on guest device AND verify SMS/merchant app notification on hotel monitoring device before finalizing in PMS.
  2. Posting Transactions in PMS:
    • Post every payment immediately under appropriate payment methods (Cash, Card-VISA, Card-MC, GCash, Bank Transfer).
    • Never leave payments unposted or stored in pending pads.
  3. Paid-Outs & Foreign Exchange (If applicable):
    • Petty cash paid-outs require signed Paid-Out Voucher and Duty Manager signature.
    • Foreign currency exchanges must use official resort daily exchange rates approved by Finance.
  4. Shift Cash Drop:
    • At shift end, reconcile collected cash against PMS Cashier Summary Report.
    • Place drop cash inside numbered Tamper-Evident Cash Drop Envelope with signed balance breakdown sheet.
    • Drop envelope into Front Office Drop Safe witnessed by Supervisor.

Localized Context

Due to occasional unstable cellular coverage affecting standalone wireless POS card terminals in Samar, always keep wired POS backup lines connected and functional, and maintain official offline manual credit card charge slips for contingency use.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cash drawer balanced with zero short/over variance.
  • All GCash/Maya transactions verified on hotel merchant receiver phone before guest departure.
  • Shift cash drop witnessed and logged dual-signature.
FO-16 Credit/Charge Account Procedure

Objective & Scope

Monitor in-house guest credit limits, manage room charge privileges, and administer direct corporate billing accounts accurately.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Credit Manager
  • Duty Manager / Front Office Associate

Execution Procedure

  1. In-House Room Charge Privileges:
    • Room charge privileges (charging F&B, spa, tours to room folio) are granted only if guest has provided valid credit card pre-authorization or cash incidentals deposit exceeding total pending charges.
    • Set credit ceiling in PMS (Standard Limit: ₱5,000 per room).
    • When guest folio charges reach 80% of deposit ceiling, system triggers High-Balance Alert.
  2. High-Balance Alert Routine:
    • Contact guest politely via phone or letter: "Dear [Guest Name], your room charges have reached ₱4,000. We kindly request an interim settlement or deposit top-up at the Front Desk at your convenience."
  3. Direct Billing / Corporate Credit Accounts:
    • Direct billing is permitted strictly for corporate, travel agency, or LGU/Government accounts with an approved, active Credit Line Agreement on file.
    • Require official Letter of Authorization (LOA) or Purchase Order (PO) at or before check-in specifying coverages.
    • Verify billing terms in PMS profile before routing folio balances to City Ledger.
  4. Check-out Routing:
    • At check-out, guest must review and sign all detailed vouchers. Transfer approved LOA balances to City Ledger account; collect cash/card for non-covered personal incidentals.

Localized Context

Government entities or provincial project teams frequenting Gamay require strict adherence to PO details. Ensure signed itemized meal receipts are attached to corporate invoices to avoid payment disputes during agency audits.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero unverified room charges permitted without prior deposit guarantee.
  • High balance notifications issued within 1 hour of limit breach.
  • Corporate LOA verified prior to City Ledger transfer.
FO-17 Deposit & Refund Procedure

Objective & Scope

Regulate reservation deposits, process cash/card security deposit returns at check-out, and handle official refund requests in compliance with resort finance controls.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Finance Department / General Manager

Execution Procedure

  1. Advance Deposit Intake:
    • Collect advance deposits per reservation cancellation policy.
    • Record transaction under PMS Advance Deposit Ledger.
    • Send official payment receipt voucher to guest within 2 hours.
  2. Incidentals Deposit Refund at Check-Out:
    • Cash Incidentals Deposit: Inspect room clearance status from Housekeeping. If no damages or unpaid charges exist, refund remaining cash deposit in full. Obtain guest signature on Deposit Refund Voucher.
    • Credit Card Pre-authorization: Release pre-authorization lock on POS terminal. Provide release transaction receipt copy to guest. (Advise guest of 7-15 bank processing days).
  3. Cancellation Refunds:
    • Cancellation > 14 days prior to arrival: Full refund minus 5% administrative/banking fee.
    • Cancellation 7–13 days prior to arrival: 50% deposit refund.
    • Cancellation < 7 days prior to arrival: Deposit forfeited.
    • For approved refunds, submit Refund Request Form signed by General Manager to Finance Department.
    • Refunds are processed via original payment method within 5 business days. Never issue cash refunds for card or bank deposits.

Localized Context

For weather-induced travel cancellations certified by Coast Guard or PAGASA advisories affecting Northern Samar, 100% of deposits are refundable or convertible into rebookable credit vouchers valid for 6 months without fee deductions.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cash security deposits 100% reconciled and signed at check-out.
  • Approved refund requests processed by accounting within 5 business days.
  • Zero cash refunds given for non-cash deposit channels.
FO-18 No-show & Cancellation Procedure

Objective & Scope

Manage reservation cancellations systematically, process no-show billing, and release unfulfilled room inventory back into saleable availability.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Reservations Clerk
  • Night Auditor / Front Office Associate

Execution Procedure

  1. Cancellation Processing:
    • Receive cancellation via email, phone, or OTA extranet.
    • Record exact date, time, name of caller, and reason for cancellation in PMS notes.
    • Issue formal Cancellation Number to guest for tracking.
    • Update PMS status to Cancelled to immediately release room inventory back into available booking channels.
  2. No-Show Identification (Night Audit):
    • At 11:59 PM nightly, Night Auditor runs pending arrivals list.

  3. No-Show Billing & Inventory Release:
    • For guaranteed bookings: Charge No-Show Penalty (minimum 1st night stay or full deposit forfeiture per booking contract) against advance deposit or credit card guarantee.

    • Generate No-Show Invoice and email to guest/agency profile.

Localized Context

Travelers to Gamay may experience major transport delays along Samar roads or sea transit. Before marking a guest as No-Show at midnight, staff must check messaging platforms (Viber/FB Page) or SMS for emergency contact notes from stranded travelers.

  • No-show status applied only after attempt to call guest contact number.

FO-19 Lost & Found Coordination

Objective & Scope

Log, categorize, store, and return items left behind by guests while protecting privacy and maintaining chain-of-custody security.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate
  • Duty Manager / Housekeeping

Execution Procedure

  1. Discovery & Turn-In:
    • Staff finding an item must immediately notify Front Office and bring item to Duty Manager within 1 hour.
    • Never handle items without gloves. Seal item inside clear plastic Lost & Found bag.
  2. Logging & Classification:
    • Enter details into the PMS / Physical Lost & Found Register: Tag #, Date Found, Time Found, Location/Room #, Detailed Item Description (brand, color, condition, serial #), Finder Name.
    • Valuables (Category A): Cash, jewelry, passports, high-end electronics. Store in key-locked safe in Front Office.
    • Non-Valuables (Category B): Clothing, shoes, books, sunglasses. Store in locked Lost & Found Storage Closet.
    • Perishables (Category C): Food, drinks, open toiletries. Retain for 24 hours, then dispose.
  3. Guest Notification & Claim Verification:
    • Front Office checks PMS out-of-house guest details for room found. Contact guest discreetly without listing sensitive items explicitly over phone for privacy/security reasons.
    • Claimants must provide accurate detailed description before item release.
  4. Return & Retention Expiration:
    • In-Person Claim: Require valid photo ID and signed release receipt.
    • Courier Return: Guest arranges and pays for accredited courier pickup.
    • Retention Deadlines: Category A kept for 1 year; Category B kept for 90 days. Unclaimed items after expiration are disposed of or awarded to finder per GM approval.

Localized Context

Items like wet beach clothing or waterproof gear are frequently left in outdoor drying racks. Ensure Housekeeping audits patio areas thoroughly during departure inspections.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Found items logged within 1 hour of recovery.
  • Valuables placed inside double-custody safe immediately.
  • Signed release slips archived for 100% of claimed items.

FO-24 Emergency Guest Communication

Objective & Scope

Respond systematically to regional typhoons, coastal surges, power blackouts, or structural emergencies by delivering clear guest advisories and executing orderly evacuations.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • General Manager / Duty Manager
  • Front Office Associates & Security Team

Execution Procedure

  1. Emergency Classification & Activation:
    • Level 1 (Local Advisory): Moderate monsoon rain, scheduled power interruptions, minor earthquake tremor.

  2. Communication Channels Execution:
    • In-Room Phone Broadcast / Public Address (PA) System: Announce clear, calm instructions in English and Tagalog.

    • Lobby Emergency Bulletin Board: Post updated weather maps, coast guard advisories, and emergency contact lists.
  3. Evacuation Coordination (Level 3 Events):
    • Front Office Associate extracts physical In-House Guest Manifest and Emergency Master Key packet.
    • Direct guests calmly toward designated safe assembly zones (e.g., Elevated Main Building Clubhouse / Structure).
    • Conduct headcount against In-House Guest Manifest at assembly point. Report missing guests to GM and Emergency Responders immediately.
  4. External Liaison:
    • Duty Manager maintains direct phone link with Gamay Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO), local police, and coast guard stations.
