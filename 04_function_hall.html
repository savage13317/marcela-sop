Module 04: Function Hall & Events

Standard Operating Procedures for event reservations, client consultations, audio-visual technical staging, banquet catering, wedding coordination, and post-event turnaround.

Quick Jump to Procedure:

FH-01 Function Hall Reservation

Objective & Scope

Establish a structured, error-free procedure for receiving, recording, and holding function hall date reservations. This procedure applies to all event inquiries handled by Front Office and Events staff for weddings, birthdays, corporate meetings, government seminars, and private gatherings at Marcela’s Hotel & Resort.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator / Banquet Manager: Primary owner for log management, calendar verification, and tentative date blocking.
  • Front Desk Agent (FDA): Receives walk-in or telephone inquiries during off-peak/night shifts and records initial reservation details.
  • General Manager: Approves non-standard discounts, prolonged pencil bookings, or special multi-day function holds.

Execution Procedure

  1. Receive Inquiry: Greet the prospective client warmly. Gather basic details: target event date, event type, estimated guest count, preferred setup, and contact information.
  2. Check Availability: Cross-check the requested date and time slot against the Master Events Calendar (Digital PMS & Physical Events Logbook). Ensure adequate buffer time (3 hours before and 2 hours after) for cleaning, setup, and teardown.
  3. Log Pencil Booking: If the slot is available, log a "Pencil Booking" status. Note the client's name, contact details, proposed event date, and temporary holding expiration date (maximum 7 calendar days).
  4. Issue Reservation Hold Notice: Send an SMS or Email confirmation to the client stating that the date is temporarily held for 7 days pending contract signing and deposit payment.
  5. Manage Expiration: If no deposit is received by Day 7, contact the client once for confirmation. If no response or commitment is made within 24 hours, release the date hold and update the Master Events Calendar.
  6. Confirm Booking: Upon receipt of the contract and required 50% initial deposit, convert the booking status from "Pencil Booked" to "Firm Booking" in both digital and physical logs.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Pay close attention to local Gamay municipal town fiesta dates (July), Provincial Samar events, and local school graduation seasons (March–May) which create high demand for the Function Hall. Inform clients during initial booking about the resort’s 100% generator backup capability to reassure them regarding local NORSAMELCO power supply stability in Northern Samar.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Date logged accurately in both Digital PMS and Physical Events Logbook.
  • 3-hour ingress/egress buffer strictly applied between consecutive events.
  • Pencil booking release policy (7 days) strictly enforced.
  • Target Metric: 0% double-booking rate; 100% client notification before releasing tentative dates.
FH-02 Event Inquiry & Client Coordination

Objective & Scope

Provide a standardized process for conducting client walkthroughs, determining exact event requirements, and offering personalized event options tailored to provincial and corporate client preferences.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Lead contact for conducting site tours, answering inquiries, and gathering requirements.
  • F&B Supervisor: Consults on custom menu preferences and dietary needs.
  • Head Chef: Consults on custom culinary requests and Waray local specialty inclusions.

Execution Procedure

  1. Initial Engagement: Welcome the client at the Front Office or Events Office. Offer refreshments (iced tea or cold water).
  2. Site Walkthrough: Escort the client to the Function Hall, pool area, and sample guest rooms if overnight accommodations are required. Point out air conditioning units, stage area, emergency exits, and restrooms.
  3. Requirement Gathering: Complete the Event Inquiry Worksheet, recording:
    • Event Title / Occasion
    • Estimated Number of Attendees
    • Budget Range
    • Required Date & Time (Start & End)
    • Preferred Seating Arrangement (Banquet, Classroom, Theater, Cocktail)
    • Meal Requirements (Buffet, Plated, Snacks, Drinks)
    • Audio-Visual Requirements (Microphones, Sound System, Projector)
  4. Provide Standard Package Sheets: Present pre-printed function packages (Weddings, Debuts, Seminars, Birthdays) with transparent pricing.
  5. Follow-Up: Send a written summary via Email or Messenger/Viber within 24 hours after the site tour.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Greet clients with genuine local warmth ("Kamusta po/Maupay nga adlaw"). Ask if guests are traveling from neighboring towns (Lapinig, Mapanas, Palapag, Catarman) to coordinate vehicle parking spaces, shuttle coordination, or early room check-ins.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Event Inquiry Worksheet fully filled out during or immediately after the meeting.
  • Function Hall air conditioning switched on 10 minutes prior to scheduled client site tour.
  • Formal proposal sent within 24 hours of initial consultation.
  • Target Metric: 100% inquiry logged in CRM/PMS; response time < 24 hours.
FH-03 Event Proposal & Quotation

Objective & Scope

Guide staff in calculating accurate, profitable, and transparent event quotations and issuing official proposals to clients for events hosted at Marcela’s Hotel & Resort.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Prepares the itemized quotation and formal proposal document.
  • Finance / Cashier Manager: Reviews non-standard pricing, government tax exemptions, and payment terms.
  • General Manager: Signs off on proposals exceeding PHP 100,000 or custom corporate packages.

Execution Procedure

  1. Cost Calculation: Using the Event Inquiry Worksheet, calculate total charges based on:
    • Function Hall Rental Rate (Base rate includes 4 hours with aircon, basic light/sound).
    • Overtime Rate per hour (Hall + Aircon fees).
    • Food & Beverage Cost per head × minimum guaranteed headcount.
    • Special Setup / Decor / AV Equipment Rental fees.
    • Room accommodation packages (if bundled).
  2. Draft Proposal: Fill out the standardized Event Proposal Template, specifying:
    • Itemized breakdown of rates (Venue, Food, AV, Inclusions).
    • Payment milestones (50% deposit upon contract signing, 50% full balance 7 days before the event).
    • Included amenities (e.g., free use of pool for 5 VIPs, sound system, podium, parking).
  3. Internal Review: Verify prices against current F&B menu costing sheets and peak/off-peak venue rate cards.
  4. Dispatch: Send the formal proposal via Email/PDF or present an official printed copy in a folder to the client within 24 hours.
  5. Validity Notice: State clearly on the proposal: "Quotation valid for 14 calendar days from date of issue."

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

For Department of Education (DepEd), LGU Gamay, or Provincial Government events, ensure quotations include complete BIR Tax breakdown (VAT/Non-VAT exemptions where applicable) and support Purchase Order (PO) / Agency Voucher terms.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Quotation uses current pricing matrix approved by Resort Management.
  • Payment terms, deposit rules, and overtime fees explicitly listed.
  • Tax calculations verified (BIR compliance).
  • Target Metric: 0% pricing error rate in issued proposals; 100% proposals delivered within 24 hours.
FH-04 Event Contract & Deposit

Objective & Scope

Ensure legally binding agreements, formal terms of agreement, and clear financial commitments between Marcela’s Hotel & Resort and event clients prior to event execution.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Drafts contract, presents terms, and collects client signature.
  • Front Office / Finance Cashier: Receives payments, issues Official Receipts (OR).
  • General Manager: Executes signature on behalf of Marcela’s Hotel & Resort.

Execution Procedure

  1. Contract Drafting: Populate the standard Event Service Agreement template with approved quotation details, agreed menu items, exact headcount, setup times, and breakdown of fees.
  2. Reviewing Terms with Client: Walk the client through key clauses:
    • Minimum Guaranteed Headcount Policy (billing will be based on guaranteed headcount or actual attendance, whichever is higher).
    • Cancellation & Refund Policy.
    • Damage / Loss Liability clause.
    • Force Majeure clause (Typhoons, Natural Disasters, Power Disruptions).
  3. Contract Signing: Require the client (or authorized corporate signatory) to sign all pages of the contract in duplicate.
  4. Deposit Collection: Collect the 50% Non-Refundable Mobilization Deposit.
  5. Issue Receipt: Issue an Official Receipt (OR) specifying: Client Name, Event Title, Event Date, Deposit Amount, and Remaining Balance.
  6. File Documents: Place the signed contract and receipt copy into the Master Event File (Digital & Hardcopy).

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Accept cash, GCash, Bank Transfer (BDO/LBP), or Manager's Check. For regional LGU/Government clients, accept approved Purchase Orders (PO) accompanied by an official Letter of Commitment signed by the Municipal Treasurer/Mayor/Head of Agency.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Standard Event Contract signed on all pages by both parties.
  • 50% deposit collected and Official Receipt issued.
  • Force Majeure clause clearly explained regarding typhoon warnings in Northern Samar.
  • Target Metric: 100% events covered by signed contracts and verified deposits prior to event date.

Execution Procedure

    • Kitchen completes raw material procurement (seafood, meats, fresh produce).
    • Housekeeping prepares linens, chair covers, and table skirts.
    • Maintenance inspects AC, lighting, backup generator, and sound systems.
  6. Day of Event (4 Hours Before): Execute physical hall setup, test AV, align catering lines, and conduct final pre-event walkthrough.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Procurement of specialized items (decorations, sound gear, specific beverages, imported cheeses/meats) from Catarman or Tacloban requires at least 5 to 7 days advance ordering due to provincial transit schedules.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • BEO distributed to all department heads 14 days in advance.
  • Final headcount locked 7 days prior.
  • Pre-event briefing conducted 48 hours before event start.
  • Target Metric: 100% operational readiness 2 hours prior to event scheduled start time.
FH-06 Function Room Setup

Objective & Scope

Outline physical staging, environment control, and safety protocols for preparing the Function Hall before guests arrive.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Banquet Setup Team / Housekeeping: Positions furniture, stage, podium, and decorations.
  • Maintenance Staff: Controls air conditioning, primary power routing, and lighting fixtures.
  • Events Coordinator: Inspects final floor plan execution against approved BEO.

Execution Procedure

  1. Pre-Setup Clearance: Ensure Function Hall floor is thoroughly swept, mopped, and sanitized by Housekeeping before moving furniture.
  2. Floor Plan Execution: Align tables, chairs, stage, registration desk, buffet station, and gift tables according to the approved layout diagram in the BEO.
  3. Safety & Ingress/Egress Verification: Maintain a minimum main aisle width of 1.5 meters and unobstructed 1.2-meter clearance to all emergency exits and fire extinguishers.
  4. Environment & Temperature Control:
    • Switch on air conditioning units 1 hour prior to event start to pre-cool the hall to 21°C - 23°C.
    • Verify all ceiling lights, wall sconces, and accent lighting are fully functional.
  5. Restroom Verification: Ensure dedicated Function Hall restrooms are scrubbed, equipped with tissue, hand soap, hand towels, and trash bins.
  6. Final Sign-off: Events Coordinator inspects layout against BEO and signs the Pre-Event Setup Checklist.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Northern Samar’s high ambient humidity requires pre-cooling the hall well in advance. Keep all double doors tightly closed during setup to prevent condensation on tiled floors, which creates slip hazards.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Layout matches approved BEO layout diagram.
  • All emergency exits clear and unblocked.
  • Temperature locked at 21°C - 23°C 1 hour prior to guest arrival.
  • Target Metric: Hall setup completed 2 hours before client arrival time.
FH-07 Table & Chair Arrangement

Objective & Scope

Ensure uniform, visually appealing, and ergonomically correct table and chair setups for various event formats.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Banquet Captain: Oversees table spacing, linen placement, and chair alignment.
  • Banquet Housemen: Handles heavy lifting, table/chair positioning, and skirting.

Execution Procedure

  1. Layout Selections:
    • Banquet Style (Round Tables): Space 10-seater round tables 1.8 meters apart from table edge to table edge to permit comfortable server movement.
    • Classroom Style: Space long tables 1 meter apart; allow 60 cm per seated participant.
    • Theater Style: Space chair rows 80 cm front-to-back; limit to 10 chairs per row block before inserting a 1-meter aisle.
    • U-Shape / Boardroom: Align rectangular tables seamlessly, using tablecloth clips for uniform appearance.
  2. Linen Application:
    • Inspect all tablecloths and chair covers for stains, tears, or wrinkles.
    • Lay main tablecloths centered with equal drop on all sides (30 cm drop standard).
    • Fit seat covers tightly and align bows/ribbons uniformly if requested.
  3. Table Setting: Position centerpieces, table numbers, water glasses, and folded napkins according to service standards.
  4. Chair Inspection: Ensure every chair is stable, wobble-free, clean, and free from structural defects.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Check chair linens carefully for storage dampness or humidity odors prior to dressing chairs. Keep backup dry linens staged in sealed plastic totes in the linen storage room.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Table spacing verified with tape measure or standard stride check.
  • Linens freshly pressed, stain-free, and aligned.
  • Chairs checked for stability and safety.
  • Target Metric: 100% compliance with table spacing and linen visual cleanliness standards.
FH-08 Audio-Visual Equipment Setup

Objective & Scope

Establish reliable installation, testing, and operational safeguards for sound systems, wireless microphones, projectors, screens, and lighting gear.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • AV Technician / Maintenance Staff: Sets up gear, conducts sound checks, and remains on standby during events.
  • Events Coordinator: Communicates audio-visual requirements from the client's program script.

Execution Procedure

  1. Equipment Retrieval: Retrieve mixer console, amplifiers, speakers, wireless microphones, HDMI cables, projector, and projector screen from the secure IT/AV storage room.
  2. Cable Management:
    • Run power and audio cables along walls or perimeter lines.
    • Secure all floor-crossing cables with heavy-duty rubber cable ramps or gaffer tape to prevent tripping hazards.
  3. Sound Check Procedure:
    • Connect and test main speakers and monitors.
    • Test wireless microphones: check battery levels (replace if < 75%), eliminate feedback loops, adjust treble/bass/echo.
    • Test audio auxiliary connection for client laptop/mobile background music playback.
  4. Visual Check Procedure:
    • Set up projector/display screen. Align image centered without keystone distortion.
    • Test client presentation files or videos via HDMI connection 2 hours prior to start.
  5. Power Backup Routing: Plug core AV gear into designated uninterruptible power supply (UPS) units connected to the generator circuit.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Northern Samar experiences frequent grid voltage fluctuations. MANDATORY: All AV equipment MUST be plugged into automatic voltage regulators (AVR) and UPS units to prevent system reboots or damage during generator auto-transfer procedures.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • All floor cables taped down securely.
  • Fresh AA batteries loaded into wireless microphones; spare batteries staged.
  • AVR and UPS active on all AV circuits.
  • Target Metric: 0% AV downtime during live client events; sound check completed 90 minutes prior to event.

FH-10 Catering for Events

Objective & Scope

Govern buffet line staging, plated meal service, food temperature management, and hygiene standards for function hall events.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • F&B Supervisor / Banquet Captain: Manages buffet flow, server assignments, and food replenishment timing.
  • Head Chef: Ensures food quality, presentation, portion control, and food safety standards.
  • Banquet Servers: Handles service, water refilling, and clearing dirty dishes (bus-out).

Execution Procedure

  1. Buffet Line Staging:
    • Set up chafing dishes with gel fuel sternos 45 minutes prior to meal time.
    • Fill chafing dish water pans with hot water to ensure rapid heating.
    • Arrange food sequence logically: Salad/Soup → Rice → Main Courses (Pork/Beef/Poultry/Seafood/Vegetables) → Dessert Table → Beverage Station.
  2. Food Temperature Control: Keep hot foods at ≥ 60°C and cold foods/desserts at ≤ 4°C.
  3. Buffet Opening & Refilling:
    • Light sterno burners 20 minutes before meal service.
    • Serve VIP/Head tables first if plated or escorted buffet service is specified in BEO.
    • Monitor chafing dish levels; notify kitchen for refilling when a dish reaches 30% remaining capacity. Never let chafing dishes run dry.
  4. Plate Cleansing & Bus-out: Quietly clear finished plates from guest tables from the right side. Do not stack plates higher than 4 dirty dishes in front of guests.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Ensure signature dishes (e.g., fresh coastal seafood, coconut milk preparations, local pork roast) are kept properly covered between serve cycles to protect against insects and heat loss in provincial open-air or air-conditioned settings.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Chafing dish temperatures verified before service (≥ 60°C).
  • Serving spoons and tongs positioned for each dish.
  • Buffet line replenished continuously before dishes hit 30% capacity.
  • Target Metric: 0% food availability gaps during meal hours; 100% compliance with sanitation standards.
FH-11 External Supplier & Event Contractor Coordination

Objective & Scope

Manage third-party vendors (event stylists, florists, photo/video crews, external mobile sounds & lights) during ingress, execution, and egress to safeguard resort property.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Pre-approves contractor ingress passes and monitors setup timelines.
  • Duty Security Guard: Checks contractor IDs, vehicle deliveries, and inspects gear during entry and exit.
  • Maintenance Staff: Supervises electrical tapping and heavy decor mounting.

Execution Procedure

  1. Pre-Event Supplier Registration: Collect contractor list, contact details, vehicle plate numbers, and equipment lists at least 3 days prior to event.
  2. Ingress Protocol:
    • Issue Supplier Badges at the Security Gate upon presenting valid IDs.
    • Inspect vehicles for prohibited items (flammables, hazardous chemicals, open flames).
    • Direct supplier vehicles to designated loading dock area behind the Function Hall.
  3. Facility Protection Rules:
    • Strictly forbid stapling, nailing, or painting on resort walls, ceilings, or wooden pillars.
    • Require use of free-standing trusses, heavy masking tape, or zip ties for hanging decorations.
    • Require electrical technician supervision for high-wattage external sound/light power taps.
  4. Egress Protocol: Require external contractors to dismantle and remove all decor, backdrops, and gear within 2 hours post-event conclusion.
  5. Post-Egress Inspection: Inspect hall walls, floors, and electrical outlets with contractor lead before issuing Security Gate Exit Clearance.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Suppliers coming from Catarman or Tacloban often arrive early morning or late night. Coordinate shift handovers so security and maintenance staff are ready to receive contractor vehicles outside standard business hours.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • All external suppliers issued badges and signed property rules acknowledgement.
  • No nails, staples, or damaging adhesives used on Function Hall structure.
  • Electrical loading calculated and approved by Maintenance prior to connection.
  • Target Metric: 0% facility structural damage caused by third-party event contractors.

FH-14 Event Change & Cancellation Procedure

Objective & Scope

Provide clear guidelines for managing event date modifications, scope downscaling, or cancellations caused by client requests or severe weather force majeure events.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Processes client requests, issues change orders, and recalculates fees.
  • General Manager: Approves deposit transfers or fee waivers for force majeure cancellations.
  • Finance Manager: Handles credit memos or refund processing.

Execution Procedure

  1. Client Request Receipt: Require client to submit a formal written request (Email, Letter, or Signed Cancellation Form) for any date change or cancellation.

    • > 30 Days prior to event: Full deposit retained as consumable credit valid for 6 months, OR refunded minus 20% administrative fee.

    • Waive all cancellation and rebooking penalty fees.
    • Transfer 100% of paid funds as credit toward a rebooked date within 12 months.
  4. Date Modification Processing: Issue an Event Modification Notice detailing the new date, updated headcount, revised menu, and any additional fees. Both parties must sign.
  5. Master Calendar Update: Immediately update the Digital PMS and physical Master Events Logbook to release or rebook the function hall dates.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Northern Samar is situated directly on the typhoon path. Management must maintain a compassionate, flexible rebooking policy during typhoon emergencies to preserve community goodwill and resort reputation.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cancellation request received in writing.
  • Force Majeure conditions verified against official PAGASA bulletins.
  • Master Calendar updated immediately to open cancelled dates for new bookings.
  • Target Metric: 100% written documentation for cancellations/date shifts; zero client billing disputes.

  2. Physical Facility Checks:
    • Inspect walls, doors, windows, and floor tiles for scuffs, cracks, or deep stains.
    • Check rest rooms for plumbing damage or clogged fixtures.
  3. Equipment & Furniture Checks: Verify table and chair counts; check for broken furniture joints or scorched linens.
  4. Left-Behind Guest Property Search:
    • Search under tables, stage areas, registration desk, and restrooms for forgotten personal items (phones, wallets, gifts, attire).
    • Log any found items immediately in the Lost & Found Register per SOP-HK-20.
  5. Sign Inspection Clearance Form: Both Banquet Captain and Client Representative sign the Post-Event Inspection Clearance. If damage is found, document with photographs.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Events in provincial settings often end late at night. Ensure adequate outdoor perimeter lighting is switched on during post-event inspections and contractor teardowns to prevent accidents or unrecorded property removal.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Joint inspection completed within 30 minutes of event conclusion.
  • Left-behind items registered in Lost & Found immediately.
  • Clearance form signed by both client and resort representative.
  • Target Metric: 100% post-event inspections completed and documented.
FH-17 Equipment Inventory After Event

Objective & Scope

Audit, count, and secure all resort-owned equipment (AV gear, chinaware, glassware, cutlery, tables, chairs, linens) immediately following event conclusion.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Banquet Supervisor: Conducts physical count of table service items and furniture.
  • AV Technician: Disconnects, tests, counts, and secures all electronic gear in storage.
  • Storekeeper / Housekeeping: Recovers, counts, and sorts all soiled linens for laundry.

Execution Procedure

  1. AV Equipment Breakdown & Audit:
    • Turn off all amplifiers and projectors before unplugging.
    • Coil cables properly without tight bending or kinking.
    • Count microphones (verify 2 wireless, 2 wired mics, receivers, remotes, HDMI cables, adapters).
    • Lock all AV gear in the AV Storage Room.
  2. Banquet Service Equipment Count:
    • Count all chafing dishes, serving spoons, water pitchers, and tray stands.
    • Perform dishware inventory count: Plates, soup bowls, glasses, spoons, forks, knives. Compare against pre-event issue counts.
  3. Furniture & Linen Count:
    • Count round/rectangular tables and banquet chairs.
    • Count tablecloths, napkins, seat covers, and overlays. Bundle soiled linens into designated laundry bags.
  4. Log Inventory Variations: Log any missing or broken items into the Event Equipment Reconciliation Sheet.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Gamay’s coastal atmosphere causes rapid oxidation on metal audio plugs and microphone terminals. Clean all metal connectors with electronic contact cleaner before locking gear in humidity-controlled storage cabinets.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • All AV gear accounted for and locked in storage.
  • Chinaware, glassware, and cutlery counted and discrepancies logged.
  • Soiled linens counted and turned over to Laundry.
  • Target Metric: 100% inventory audit completed before end of shift.
FH-18 Event Damage & Loss Reporting

Objective & Scope

Provide a transparent, fair procedure for assessing, documenting, and charging for physical property damage or loss occurring during an event.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Banquet Captain: Identifies and documents damages immediately during or after the event.
  • Security Officer: Takes photos and files incident report for major property damage.
  • Finance Manager: Calculates replacement costs and applies charges to client billing or security deposit.

Execution Procedure

  1. Immediate Discovery: Upon discovering damaged resort property (e.g., broken glass windows, torn acoustic panels, scorched linens, smashed furniture, broken AV gear), notify the client representative immediately.
  2. Photographic & Written Documentation:
    • Take high-resolution photographs from multiple angles.
    • Complete the Event Damage & Loss Incident Report, detailing item name, description of damage, location, time, and witness statements.
  3. Cost Evaluation: Cross-reference damaged items against the resort’s standard Master Replacement Cost Sheet (approved by Management).
  4. Client Notification & Billing:
    • Present the photographic proof and Damage Report to the client representative.
    • Deduct replacement fee from the client's Security Deposit, or add as an incidental charge to the final Master Invoice before checkout.
  5. Repair Order Generation: Forward a copy of the report to Maintenance to initiate immediate repair or replacement purchasing.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Ensure replacement cost cards reflect local market replacement costs (including freight shipping to Northern Samar) without excessive markups to maintain positive local community relations.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • High-resolution photographic evidence captured for all damage claims.
  • Incident report signed by client representative where possible.
  • Charges matched strictly to approved Master Replacement Cost Sheet.
  • Target Metric: 100% resolution of damage claims prior to client departure.
FH-19 Function Hall Cleaning & Reset

Objective & Scope

Systematically clean, sanitize, restore, and reset the Function Hall to standard ready status within 3 hours after an event ends.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Housekeeping Attendants: Performs sweeping, mopping, trash removal, and restroom sanitization.
  • Banquet Housemen: Restores standard furniture layout and stores excess equipment.
  • Executive Housekeeper: Inspects final cleanliness and releases hall status to "Ready".

Execution Procedure

  1. Trash Removal: Empty all trash bins into heavy-duty garbage bags. Remove catering food scraps, decor debris, and disposable items. Transport to garbage holding area.
  2. Floor & Surface Cleaning:
    • Sweep hall floor completely to remove confetti, food particles, and dust.
    • Damp mop entire floor area using commercial floor sanitizer and neutral cleaner.
    • Spot clean walls, light switches, door handles, and glass doors.
  3. Restroom Deep Cleaning: Scrub toilets, urinals, sinks, and mirrors. Mop restroom floors with disinfectant. Restock toilet paper, hand soap, and trash bags.
  4. Standard Reset:
    • Arrange tables and chairs in standard default setup (e.g., 4 round banquet tables, standard perimeter alignment) or clear empty floor as directed by upcoming schedule.
    • Cover tables with clean dust covers.
  5. Power & AC Shutdown: Turn off all air conditioning units, stage lights, sound systems, and non-essential ambient lights. Lock all access doors.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Tropical provincial climates attract ants, flies, and rodents rapidly if food scraps remain overnight. MANDATORY: All garbage and food waste MUST be removed from the Function Hall and surrounding foyer within 1 hour post-event.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Hall floors swept, mopped, and sanitized.
  • Restrooms scrubbed, disinfected, and restocked.
  • All food waste completely removed from premises.
  • Air conditioning, lights, and AV power switched off.
  • Target Metric: Hall fully reset and locked within 3 hours post-event.
FH-20 Post-Event Client Feedback

Objective & Scope

Capture structured client feedback, evaluate guest satisfaction ratings, resolve service shortcomings, and collect online reviews to boost resort reputation.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Sends digital/printed feedback form and conducts follow-up call.
  • General Manager: Reviews low feedback scores (≤ 3/5) and conducts service recovery.
  • Marketing / IT Staff: Monitors Google Business Profile and social media reviews.

Execution Procedure

  1. Feedback Request Dispatch: Send an online Event Satisfaction Survey link via SMS/Viber/Email, or present a printed copy to the client organizer within 24 hours post-event.
  2. Survey Structure: Evaluate key service areas on a 1–5 scale:
    • Event Planning & Coordination Experience
    • Function Hall Setup & Ambiance
    • Food Quality, Taste & Presentation
    • Service Staff Courtesy & Efficiency
    • Audio-Visual & Technical Quality
    • Overall Value for Money
  3. Feedback Review:
    • Score 4–5 Stars (Highly Satisfied): Send a thank-you message inviting the client to share their experience on Google Business Profile and Facebook.
    • Score 1–3 Stars (Dissatisfied): Immediately alert the General Manager and Events Coordinator.
  4. Service Recovery Protocol: For ratings ≤ 3, the General Manager must personally call the client within 24 hours to hear concerns, apologize, and offer appropriate vouchers or discounts for future bookings.
  5. Monthly Analysis: Aggregate feedback data monthly to identify operational improvement areas for Kitchen, F&B, Housekeeping, and Engineering.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

In Gamay and Northern Samar, community word-of-mouth and personal relationships are critical to the resort's reputation. Beyond digital survey links, conduct a warm, personalized follow-up phone call or in-person greeting for local event organizers, town leaders, and wedding couples to ensure lasting patron loyalty and immediate service recovery.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Feedback survey sent within 24 hours post-event.
  • Low scores (≤ 3/5) escalated to General Manager immediately.
  • Follow-up service recovery completed within 24 hours for dissatisfied clients.
  • Target Metric: ≥ 90% client satisfaction score; 100% response rate to feedback ≤ 3/5.
