Food & Beverage Operations

Restaurant service, bar operations, banquet catering, and F&B quality control procedures.

Quick Jump to Procedure:

FB-01 Restaurant Opening Procedure

Objective & Scope

To ensure the resort restaurant opens punctually, in immaculate condition, and fully prepared for breakfast and dining operations. Applies to all F&B service staff and kitchen opening personnel.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: F&B Supervisor, Morning Waitstaff, Morning Line Cook.
  • Secondary: Duty Manager, Security Officer (key release).

Execution Procedure

  1. Punctual Arrival & Key Retrieval: Arrive 45 minutes prior to opening (e.g., 05:45 AM for 06:30 AM breakfast opening). Sign out restaurant and kitchen keys from Front Desk key log.
  2. Facility Activation & Lighting: Disarm security/alarm, turn on main dining room lights, exterior ambient lighting, ceiling fans. Set AC units to 22°C–24°C in enclosed dining areas.
  3. Environment & Pest Sweep: Inspect indoor and terrace dining areas for overnight pest activity or humidity condensation. Wipe down all dining tables, chairs, and service stations with food-safe sanitizer.
  4. Mise en Place & Station Preparation: Verify all table setups are clean, aligned, and fully stocked. Check POS terminal: power on, test receipt printing paper, verify offline buffer status. Prepare welcome station, water pitchers, and complimentary breakfast buffet/alacarte setup.
  5. Kitchen Briefing & Alignment: Conduct a 5-minute pre-shift briefing with kitchen staff to discuss daily specials, room occupancy count, expected breakfast influx, and 86'd items.

Localized Context

Coastal humidity in Gamay causes salt shakers to clump overnight. Add rice grains to salt shakers daily and wipe condiment bottles clean of moisture. Morning sea breezes can bring flying insects; verify electronic insect lights are activated away from direct dining lines.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Doors Open: Exactly at scheduled opening time (06:30 AM)
  • Dining Cleanliness: 100% dust/insect-free tables and seating
  • POS Readiness: Power on, float logged, paper roll loaded
  • Pre-Shift Briefing: Completed daily prior to opening
FB-02 Restaurant Closing Procedure

Objective & Scope

To safely shut down the restaurant dining area and kitchen, secure perishables, reconcile daily cash/sales, and prevent fire or pest hazards overnight.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: F&B Supervisor, Closing Waitstaff, Closing Line Cook, F&B Cashier.
  • Secondary: Security Guard on duty.

Execution Procedure

  1. Last Call & Service Wind-down: Announce last call for food orders 30 minutes before closing (e.g., 09:30 PM for 10:00 PM closing). Gently inform remaining dining guests of closing hours while maintaining warm service.
  2. Dining Area Breakdown: Clear all dirty dishes, glassware, and condiments from tables. Store tabletop condiments in sealed food storage containers overnight. Wipe down all tables, chairs, and bar counter surfaces. Vacuum or damp-mop floors.
  3. POS & Cash Reconciliation: Print POS End-of-Day (Z-Reading) report. Count cash float and total daily cash receipts; reconcile against POS totals. Hand over cash pouch and daily drop report to Front Desk night cashier or deposit into safe per SOP-FB-24.
  4. Kitchen & Utility Shutdown: Verify all kitchen gas lines, griddles, ovens, and fryers are turned off. Secure all perishable food items in cold storage; log closing freezer/fridge temperatures. Ensure grease traps are cleared and dishwashing area is sanitized and drained.
  5. Security & Final Exit: Turn off AC units, dining lights, exhaust fans, and audio systems. Keep security night lights on. Lock all exterior glass doors, terrace gates, and kitchen service doors. Return keys to Front Desk key log and inform Security Guard that the F&B facility is locked.

Localized Context

Typhoon-prone rainy nights in Samar can flood exterior patios; store all loose patio seat cushions inside the main dining hall before locking up. Tropical humidity encourages ants and rodents; zero food debris or unwashed dishware may remain overnight.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Gas & Power Lines: 100% shut off (except cold storage)
  • Cash Drop: Zero discrepancy between POS and physical cash drop
  • Physical Security: All doors, windows, and gates double-locked
FB-03 Table Setup & Mise en Place

Objective & Scope

To standardize dining table arrangements, cutlery, glassware, and service station preparations to present a clean, high-standard boutique dining experience.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Waitstaff, Busser.
  • Secondary: F&B Supervisor.

Execution Procedure

  1. Table Cleanliness Check: Inspect dining table legs for stability; level table feet if wobbly. Clean table surface with sanitized cloth; ensure zero water spots or grease streaks.
  2. Standard Breakfast Cover Setup: Place clean placemat centered 1 inch from table edge. Dinner fork on the left; dinner knife on the right (cutting edge facing inward). Water goblet placed at the tip of the knife (1 o'clock position). Coffee cup and saucer placed on the right side of the knife with spoon on saucer. Centerpiece: Clean napkin caddy, salt/pepper shakers, daily menu card/table tent.
  3. Lunch & Dinner Cover Setup: Replace coffee cup setup with polished wine glass or additional water goblet. Add folded cloth napkin (or high-grade folded paper napkin) centered on placemat.
  4. Service Station Stocking (Mise en Place): Replenish service station drawers with polished cutlery wrapped in clean trays. Stock folded napkins, toothpick holders, condiments (Knorr seasoning, banana ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar), extra water pitchers, and bill folders.

Localized Context

Local resort guests frequently request native Samar dipping sauces (sinamak vinegar with calamansi and chili). Ensure service stations always have fresh sinamak cruets prepared daily. High minerals in local municipal water leave spots; all washed glassware and cutlery must be hand-polished using micro-fiber cloths and warm water vapor before setting on tables.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cutlery Alignment: Parallel, 1 inch from table edge
  • Glassware Hygiene: Zero fingerprints, water spots, or chips
  • Station Readiness: 100% stocked prior to shift start

FB-05 Order Taking Procedure

Objective & Scope

To take food and beverage orders accurately, provide knowledgeable menu suggestions, identify guest dietary restrictions, and ensure a seamless culinary experience.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Waitstaff / Servers.
  • Secondary: F&B Supervisor, Head Chef.

Execution Procedure

  1. Timing & Readiness: Approach table within 3 to 5 minutes after delivering menus or when guests close menus. Stand upright with pleasant eye contact.
  2. Suggestive Selling & Upselling: Highlight daily fresh seafood catches (fresh lapu-lapu or Gamay mud crabs) and resort specialties (Samar-style seafood in coconut cream).
  3. Recording the Order: Write down orders clearly on server pad using standard seat numbering (Seat 1 starts closest to server's left, going clockwise). Record specific preferences (spice level, meat temperatures, allergen warnings).

Localized Context

Fresh seafood availability depends on local Gamay fishing yields. Servers must know exactly what fresh catch arrived that morning. Always ask domestic and foreign tourists their preferred chili heat and sourness level.

FB-07 Food Preparation Standards

Objective & Scope

To establish uniform culinary preparation, recipe adherence, hygiene, and cooking safety for all dishes produced in Marcela's kitchen.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Head Chef, Sous Chef, Line Cooks.
  • Secondary: Kitchen Stewards.

Execution Procedure

  1. Recipe & Prep Standard Adherence: Follow standardized recipe cards strictly (exact yields, measurements, cooking times).
  2. Sanitary Prep Area Setup: Clean and sanitize stainless prep tables. Use color-coded cutting boards (Red: red meat, Yellow: poultry, Blue: seafood, Green: produce, White: ready-to-eat).
  3. Thawing & Defrosting: Thaw frozen meats in refrigerator (2°C–4°C) 24 hours in advance. Never thaw at ambient room temperature.
  4. Core Internal Temperature Cooking Standards: Poultry: 74°C (165°F); Pork/Ground Meat: 70°C (158°F); Seafood/Fish: 63°C (145°F) for 15 seconds.
  5. Holding Temperatures: Hot food held above 60°C (140°F); cold food held below 4°C (40°F).

Localized Context

Local Gamay seafood must be cleaned, descaled, and gutted immediately upon morning delivery. Store on crushed ice inside cold storage to prevent rapid decomposition.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Recipe Compliance: 100% adherence to standard taste/weight
  • Cross-Contamination: Zero misuse of color-coded cutting boards
  • Cooking Temps: 100% compliance with internal target temperatures

FB-12 Kitchen Sanitation

Objective & Scope

To maintain uncompromising clean, sanitized, hazard-free kitchen environments meeting Philippine Department of Health (DOH) sanitation standards.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Head Steward, Kitchen Stewards, Line Cooks.
  • Secondary: Head Chef, Safety Officer.

Execution Procedure

  1. Clean-As-You-Go (CAYG) Rule: Maintain continuous cleaning during shift. Wipe prep surfaces immediately after processing raw materials using food-safe sanitizer.
  2. 3-Compartment Sink Dishwashing: Sink 1 (Wash: 45°C warm water + detergent); Sink 2 (Rinse); Sink 3 (Sanitize: 100-200 ppm chlorine or thermal hot water >77°C for min 1 minute). Air-dry all utensils.
  3. Trash & Waste Disposal: Empty trash cans when 3/4 full or every 4 hours. Use pedal-operated lined trash bins.
  4. Daily Deep Sanitation Schedule: End-of-shift scrubbing of kitchen floors using degreaser. Sanitize all stainless tables, stove knobs, refrigerator handles.
  5. Grease Trap Maintenance: Clean and clear kitchen grease traps daily at end-of-night shutdown.

Localized Context

Coastal humidity in Samar accelerates mold growth on damp surfaces. Ensure all kitchen walls, silicone seals, and storage racks are completely dried and sanitized daily.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Sanitizer PPM: 100-200 ppm checked twice daily via test strips
  • Dish Air-Drying: 100% towel-free air drying
  • Grease Trap Clean: Cleared 100% daily

FB-14 Food Receiving & Inspection

Objective & Scope

To inspect, test, accept, or reject incoming food raw materials and beverages to guarantee quality, freshness, and safety before entering resort storage.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Receiving Clerk, Head Chef / Duty Cook.
  • Secondary: F&B Manager, Finance Officer.

Execution Procedure

  1. Delivery Arrival & PO Check: Match incoming items against Purchase Order (PO) invoice for quantity, brand, and unit price.
  2. Temperature & Quality Testing: Chilled foods <=4°C (40°F); Frozen foods <=-18°C (0°F) with zero thaw signs. Reject items with <50% remaining shelf life.
  3. Fresh Seafood Inspection: Fish must have clear bulging eyes, bright red gills, firm elastic flesh, and fresh ocean smell. Crabs/shellfish must be alive.
  4. Fresh Produce Inspection: Inspect fruits and vegetables for rot, excessive bruising, mold, or insect infestation.
  5. Rejection & Documentation: Reject substandard items immediately on invoice. Record reason on Receiving Log and notify Purchasing.

Localized Context

Fresh catches sourced from local Gamay fishermen must undergo immediate temperature checks. Seafood transported without adequate ice must be rejected.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Receiving Temp Checks: Logged on receiving sheet for 100% of chill/frozen items
  • Reject Handling: Immediate credit memo/invoice note for defective items
  • Seafood Freshness: 100% pass rate on gill, eye, and firm flesh checks
FB-15 Food Storage & Labeling

Objective & Scope

To properly store dry goods, chilled products, and frozen inventory, maintaining food safety and eliminating cross-contamination.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Storekeeper, Sous Chef, Line Cooks.
  • Secondary: Head Chef.

Execution Procedure

  1. The 15-Minute Storing Rule: Put away all received deliveries into cold/dry storage within 15 minutes of receiving.
  2. Mandatory Food Labeling: All prepped or opened food must have a waterproof date label stating: Item Name, Date/Time Prepared, Expiry Date, Staff Initials.
  3. Dry Storage Standards: Keep dry goods room temperature between 10°C–21°C. Store minimum 6 inches (15 cm) above floor on stainless shelving.
  4. Refrigerator Arrangement: Top Shelf (Ready-to-eat); 2nd Shelf (Raw fish/seafood); 3rd Shelf (Raw beef/pork); 4th Shelf (Ground meats); Bottom Shelf (Raw poultry).
  5. Cross-Contamination Protection: All containers must be tightly covered with fitted lids or food-grade plastic wrap.

Localized Context

High coastal humidity causes flour, sugar, and spices to cake or attract pests. Store all dry powders and grains in airtight plastic containers with rubber seals.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Date Labeling: 100% compliance on open/prepped items
  • Refrigerator Shelf Order: Zero violations of raw meat stacking rules
  • Off-Floor Elevation: 100% dry goods stored >6 inches off floor
FB-16 FIFO / FEFO Inventory Control

Objective & Scope

To implement First-In, First-Out (FIFO) and First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) stock rotation practices, preventing stock degradation, ingredient spoilage, and financial loss.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Storekeeper, Line Cooks, Bartenders.
  • Secondary: Head Chef, F&B Supervisor.

Execution Procedure

  1. Stock Rotation Upon Replenishment: Place newer items with later expiry dates BEHIND older stock.
  2. FEFO Priority Rule: Product with the earliest expiration date sits at the front of the shelf for immediate use, regardless of arrival date.
  3. Daily Pre-Shift Prep Review: Inspect shelf positioning before service starts, selecting front-row ingredients for daily batch prep.
  4. Visual Color-Coded Expiry Tags: Apply colored day-of-the-week labels to prep containers to visually highlight items nearing expiry.
  5. Slow-Moving Stock Identification: Conduct weekly checks for items with <30 days shelf life. Report to Head Chef for feature specials.

Localized Context

Gamay is subject to occasional ferry/road logistics delays during bad weather. Strict FIFO/FEFO prevents stock loss during periods when fresh shipments are interrupted.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Shelf Positioning: New stock placed consistently behind old stock
  • Expiration Audits: Zero expired products found in active storage
  • Color-Tag Compliance: 100% prep containers labeled with color tags
FB-17 Refrigerator & Freezer Temperature Monitoring

Objective & Scope

To systematically record cold storage temperatures, ensuring food remains outside the dangerous bacteria growth zone (4°C to 60°C).

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Duty Line Cook, Kitchen Steward, Bartender.
  • Secondary: Head Chef, Facilities Engineer.

Execution Procedure

  1. Thermometer Installation: Every refrigerator, chiller, and freezer must have a calibrated internal thermometer hung in warmest section.
  2. Standard Temperature Ranges: Refrigerators: 1°C to 4°C (34°F to 40°F); Freezers: -18°C to -22°C (0°F to -8°F).
  3. Three-Times-Daily Log Execution: Record temperatures at 06:00 AM (Opening), 02:00 PM (Midday), and 10:00 PM (Closing).
  4. Out-of-Range Action Plan: If fridge temp rises above 5°C, check gasket, alert Head Chef/Engineer, and transfer perishables to backup unit if needed.

Localized Context

Provincial power interruptions trigger generator cut-overs. During power transfer gaps, staff must verify all chillers restart automatically and do not trip circuit breakers.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Log Sheet Completion: 100% completed 3x daily without missing entries
  • Target Temp Maintenance: Chillers <=4°C, Freezers <=-18°C
  • Maintenance Response: Engineering response within 15 mins of alarm

FB-19 Kitchen Equipment Cleaning

Objective & Scope

To outline detailed cleaning and preventive care schedules for heavy kitchen appliances (grills, fryers, exhaust hoods, ranges).

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Line Cooks, Stewards.
  • Secondary: Head Chef, Maintenance Engineer.

Execution Procedure

Localized Context

Northern Samar's coastal environment rapidly corrodes metal. Wipe stainless steel surfaces with protective food-grade polish weekly. Oil iron grates daily.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Deep Fryer Oil Quality: TPM <24% verified daily
  • Exhaust Filter Clean: Free of grease build-up weekly
  • Stainless Rust Sweep: Zero rust buildup on stainless steel prep lines

To provide immediate, effective, and professional protocols for responding to alleged foodborne illness reports, foreign object contamination, or severe allergic reactions.

FB-22 Cashiering & Restaurant Billing

Objective & Scope

To process guest bill presentations, cash/card transactions, room charges, and discount applications accurately, securely, and transparently.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: F&B Cashier, Waitstaff.
  • Secondary: Front Office Cashier, Finance Manager.

Execution Procedure

  1. Bill Generation: Print itemized guest check from POS. Verify all ordered items match table ticket.
  2. Bill Presentation: Present bill inside clean leather presenter cover from the guest's RIGHT side.
  3. Payment Processing Methods: Cash (state amount received aloud, return exact change); Credit Card / GCash (process securely, verify transaction success); Room Charge (verify room number and guest name, obtain signature).
  4. Discount Processing: Apply official Senior Citizen / PWD discount (20% off F&B portion, VAT-exempt) in POS upon presentation of valid ID.

Localized Context

GCash and Maya are popular payment choices for domestic tourists in Samar. If cellular network drops, switch wireless terminal to resort backup Wi-Fi network.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Billing Accuracy: Zero item mismatch between ticket and POS bill
  • Senior/PWD Discount: 100% valid OSCA/PWD ID numbers recorded
  • Room Signature: 100% room charges signed with legible print name
FB-23 Daily F&B Inventory

Objective & Scope

To perform accurate daily counts of high-value proteins, alcoholic beverages, and key kitchen staples to maintain tight cost control.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Head Chef, Bar Captain, Storekeeper.
  • Secondary: F&B Cost Controller, Finance Officer.

Execution Procedure

  1. Daily High-Value Item Count: Conduct physical stock count every evening after kitchen/bar closure for high-cost inventory (proteins, liquor, wine, beer).
  2. Reconciliation against POS Consumption: Match physical count against formula: $$ \text{Opening Stock} + \text{Daily Received} - \text{POS Sales} = \text{Theoretical Ending Stock} $$
  3. Variance Identification: Calculate variance: $$ \text{Variance} = \text{Physical Count} - \text{Theoretical Stock} $$ Any variance exceeding +/- 1 portion triggers immediate review.
  4. Monthly Full Inventory: Perform end-of-month full physical inventory counting all dry goods, paper, and chemicals with Finance.

Localized Context

Fresh fish weight reduces during cleaning/gutting (yield percentage loss). Apply standard yield conversion formulas (e.g., 80% usable yield on whole fresh fish) during reconciliation.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Daily Count Completion: Executed 100% nightly without omission
  • High-Value Variance: Under <1% variance target threshold
  • Monthly Audit: Joint physical count completed on last day of month
FB-24 Daily Sales & Cash Reconciliation

Objective & Scope

To mandate secure daily cash handling, sales tallying, POS Z-reading reconciliation, and safe drop protocols at the close of daily operations.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: F&B Cashier, F&B Supervisor.
  • Secondary: Front Office Cashier, Finance Manager.

Execution Procedure

  1. POS Terminal Closeout: Perform POS End-of-Day closeout and print official Z-Reading report.
  2. Physical Cash Counting: Count cash in locked cashier office. Separate base cash float from daily cash sales collections.
  3. Reconciliation Summary Sheet: Fill out Daily Cashier Remittance Summary (Gross Sales, Cash, Card/GCash batches, Room charges, Discounts, Over/Short).
  4. Witness Verification & Sealing: Cashier and Supervisor recount cash, sign form, and seal inside tamper-evident cash drop envelope.
  5. Safe Drop Execution: Drop envelope into Front Office safe drop slot in presence of Duty Manager and sign Safe Drop Logbook.

Localized Context

Late night cash movements in provincial resort settings require security presence. Resort security must accompany F&B cashier during cash drops after 10:00 PM.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cash Variance: Zero unexplained cash shortage/overage
  • Dual Signatures: 100% cash drops witnessed and double-signed
  • Drop Timing: Dropped into drop safe immediately at shift close
FB-25 Catering Operations

Objective & Scope

To manage food production, buffet setup, service timing, and sanitation during private banquets, weddings, corporate events, and catering.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: F&B Supervisor, Head Chef, Event Captain, Banquet Servers.
  • Secondary: Banquet Sales Coordinator, Maintenance Staff.

Execution Procedure

  1. BEO Review: Conduct pre-event meeting 24 hours prior to review Banquet Event Order (BEO) specifications.
  2. Buffet Equipment Setup: Set chafing dishes 1 hour prior. Fill water pans with hot water and light gel warming sternos 30 mins before service. Maintain hot food >=60°C.
  3. Food Production & Replenishment: Prepare in controlled batches. Monitor buffet line constantly; replenish when food drops to 1/3 capacity.
  4. Service Execution & Clearing: Assign dedicated banquet servers to keep buffet clean. Busser team clears dirty plates continuously.
  5. Post-Event Tear Down: Extinguish flames safely. Chill leftover food or discard per safety standards. Clean and sanitize equipment.

Localized Context

For outdoor resort events in Gamay, sudden coastal rains or sea breeze gusts can extinguish sterno flames. Fit chafing dishes with wind guards and ensure popup canopies are erected.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Service Readiness: Buffet fully set and heated 15 mins prior to event start
  • Food Temperature: Chafing dish temperatures maintained >60°C
  • BEO Compliance: 100% match with contract menu specifications
FB-26 Staff Meal Procedure

Objective & Scope

To provide nutritious, hygienic, and balanced daily meals to all resort duty personnel in designated staff dining areas while maintaining strict cost control.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Primary: Duty Staff Cook, Sous Chef.
  • Secondary: Head Chef, HR Officer.

Execution Procedure

  1. Meal Schedule & Rotations: Serve at scheduled non-peak hours (Breakfast: 05:30-06:15; Lunch: 11:00-01:30 staggered; Dinner: 05:00-07:30 staggered).
  2. Menu Planning & Nutrition: Plan weekly menus ensuring balanced protein, carbs, and vegetables. Must be freshly cooked or utilize safe surplus prep.
  3. Dining Hygiene & Protocol: Staff must eat in designated canteen only. Eating in guest dining areas or kitchen lines is prohibited. Staff must clean their own plates.
  4. Kitchen Consumption Controls: Unrecorded sampling, grazing, or personal meal prep on kitchen line is considered a policy violation.

Localized Context

Working in Gamay's warm climate causes rapid dehydration. Staff canteen must be continuously supplied with clean, chilled drinking water, electrolytes, and rice options.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Schedule Adherence: Staff meals served punctually without disrupting guest ops
  • Canteen Sanitation: 100% clean and sanitized staff dining area
  • Menu Variety: Balanced weekly menu posted every Sunday
