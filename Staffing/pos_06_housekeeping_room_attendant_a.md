### HOUSEKEEPING / ROOM ATTENDANT A (`POS-06`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to Housekeeping & Laundry Lead (`POS-05`) | Supervises None
- **Role Summary:** Executes daily room cleaning, stay-over servicing, linen changing, and public area sanitation while supporting cross-functional venue resets.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Guest Room Turnover & Cleaning:** Clean checkout and stay-over guest rooms according to resort sanitation and bed-making standards (`SOP-HK-02`, `SOP-HK-03`, `SOP-HK-04`, `SOP-HK-07`, `SOP-HK-08`).
2. **In-Room Amenity Replenishment:** Restock guest toiletries, bath linens, drinking water, and minibar items (`SOP-HK-09`, `SOP-HK-10`, `SOP-HK-11`).
3. **In-House Laundry Operations:** Collect, sort, wash, dry, fold, and store guest room linens and towels (`SOP-HK-16`, `SOP-HK-17`).
4. **Maintenance & Loss Reporting:** Immediately log maintenance defects, stained linens, and guest lost-and-found items (`SOP-HK-19`, `SOP-HK-20`, `SOP-FM-03`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Public Area Deep Cleaning:** Maintain routine sanitation and deep cleaning across public hallways, lobbies, and restaurant spaces (`SOP-HK-13`, `SOP-HK-15`).
2. **Function Hall Event Setup:** Assist with banquet table/chair arrangements, venue cleaning, and post-event resetting (`SOP-FH-06`, `SOP-FH-19`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Turnover Efficiency:** Average room cleaning time maintained between 25–30 minutes per standard room.
- **Cleanliness Quality Score:** Guest room cleanliness feedback score $\ge 90\%$.
- **Asset Control:** Zero unreported room damage, linen loss, or missing inventory items.
