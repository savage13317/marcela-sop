### SOP-FB-01: RESTAURANT OPENING PROCEDURE

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-01
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To ensure the resort restaurant opens punctually, in immaculate condition, and fully prepared for breakfast and dining operations. Applies to all F&B service staff and kitchen opening personnel.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** F&B Supervisor, Morning Waitstaff, Morning Line Cook.
* **Secondary:** Duty Manager, Security Officer (key release).

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Punctual Arrival & Key Retrieval:**
   * Arrive 45 minutes prior to opening (e.g., 05:45 AM for 06:30 AM breakfast opening).
   * Sign out the restaurant and kitchen keys from the Front Desk key log.
2. **Facility Activation & Lighting:**
   * Disarm restaurant security/alarm if applicable.
   * Turn on main dining room lights, exterior ambient lighting, and ceiling fans.
   * Set air conditioning units to 22°C–24°C in enclosed dining areas.
3. **Environment & Pest Sweep:**
   * Inspect indoor and terrace dining areas for overnight pest activity or humidity condensation.
   * Wipe down all dining tables, chairs, and service stations with food-safe sanitizer.
4. **Mise en Place & Station Preparation:**
   * Verify all table setups are clean, aligned, and fully stocked (salt/pepper shakers, napkin caddies, table tents).
   * Check POS terminal: power on, test receipt printing paper, verify offline buffer status.
   * Prepare welcome station, water pitchers, and complimentary breakfast buffet/alacarte setup.
5. **Kitchen Briefing & Alignment:**
   * Conduct a 5-minute pre-shift briefing with kitchen staff to discuss daily specials, room occupancy count, expected breakfast influx, and 86'd items.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Humidity & Salt Air Prep:** Coastal humidity in Gamay causes salt shakers to clump overnight. Add rice grains to salt shakers daily and wipe condiment bottles clean of moisture.
* **Insects:** Morning sea breezes can bring flying insects; verify electronic insect lights are activated away from direct dining lines.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Doors Open | Exactly at scheduled opening time (06:30 AM) | F&B Supervisor |
| Dining Cleanliness | 100% dust/insect-free tables and seating | Waitstaff |
| POS Readiness | Power on, float logged, paper roll loaded | Cashier/Server |
| Pre-Shift Briefing | Completed daily prior to opening | F&B Supervisor |

### SOP-FB-02: RESTAURANT CLOSING PROCEDURE

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-02
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To safely shut down the restaurant dining area and kitchen, secure perishables, reconcile daily cash/sales, and prevent fire or pest hazards overnight.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** F&B Supervisor, Closing Waitstaff, Closing Line Cook, F&B Cashier.
* **Secondary:** Security Guard on duty.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Last Call & Service Wind-down:**
   * Announce last call for food orders 30 minutes before closing (e.g., 09:30 PM for 10:00 PM closing).
   * Gently inform remaining dining guests of closing hours while maintaining warm service.
2. **Dining Area Breakdown:**
   * Clear all dirty dishes, glassware, and condiments from tables.
   * Store tabletop condiments in sealed food storage containers overnight.
   * Wipe down all tables, chairs, and bar counter surfaces. Vacuum or damp-mop floors.
3. **POS & Cash Reconciliation:**
   * Print POS End-of-Day (Z-Reading) report.
   * Count cash float and total daily cash receipts; reconcile against POS totals.
   * Hand over cash pouch and daily drop report to Front Desk night cashier or deposit into safe per `SOP-FB-24`.
4. **Kitchen & Utility Shutdown:**
   * Verify all kitchen gas lines, griddles, ovens, and fryers are turned off.
   * Secure all perishable food items in cold storage; log closing freezer/fridge temperatures.
   * Ensure grease traps are cleared and dishwashing area is sanitized and drained.
5. **Security & Final Exit:**
   * Turn off AC units, dining lights, exhaust fans, and audio systems. Keep security night lights on.
   * Lock all exterior glass doors, terrace gates, and kitchen service doors.
   * Return keys to Front Desk key log and inform Security Guard that the F&B facility is locked.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Power & Moisture Lockup:** Typhoon-prone rainy nights in Samar can flood exterior patios; store all loose patio seat cushions inside the main dining hall before locking up.
* **Pest Deterrence:** Tropical humidity encourages ants and rodents; zero food debris or unwashed dishware may remain overnight.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Gas & Power Lines | 100% shut off (except cold storage) | Closing Line Cook |
| Cash Drop | Zero discrepancy between POS and physical cash drop | F&B Cashier |
| Physical Security | All doors, windows, and gates double-locked | F&B Supervisor |

### SOP-FB-03: TABLE SETUP & MISE EN PLACE

* **SOP-Code:** SOP-FB-03
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To standardize dining table arrangements, cutlery, glassware, and service station preparations to present a clean, high-standard boutique dining experience.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Waitstaff, Busser.
* **Secondary:** F&B Supervisor.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Table Cleanliness Check:**
   * Inspect dining table legs for stability; level table feet if wobbly.
   * Clean table surface with sanitized cloth; ensure zero water spots or grease streaks.
2. **Standard Breakfast Cover Setup:**
   * Place clean placemat centered 1 inch from table edge.
   * Dinner fork on the left; dinner knife on the right (cutting edge facing inward).
   * Water goblet placed at the tip of the knife (1 o'clock position).
   * Coffee cup and saucer placed on the right side of the knife with spoon on saucer.
   * Centerpiece: Clean napkin caddy, salt/pepper shakers, daily menu card/table tent.
3. **Lunch & Dinner Cover Setup:**
   * Replace coffee cup setup with polished wine glass or additional water goblet.
   * Add folded cloth napkin (or high-grade folded paper napkin) centered on placemat.
4. **Service Station Stocking (Mise en Place):**
   * Replenish service station drawers with polished cutlery wrapped in clean trays.
   * Stock folded napkins, toothpick holders, condiments (Knorr seasoning, banana ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar), extra water pitchers, and bill folders.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Local Condiment Standards:** Local resort guests frequently request native Samar dipping sauces (sinamak vinegar with calamansi and chili). Ensure service stations always have fresh *sinamak* cruets prepared daily.
* **Polishing Glassware:** High minerals in local municipal water leave spots; all washed glassware and cutlery must be hand-polished using micro-fiber cloths and warm water vapor before setting on tables.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Cutlery Alignment | Parallel, 1 inch from table edge | Waitstaff |

### SOP-FB-05: ORDER TAKING PROCEDURE

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-05
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To take food and beverage orders accurately, provide knowledgeable menu suggestions, identify guest dietary restrictions, and ensure a seamless culinary experience.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Waitstaff / Servers.
* **Secondary:** F&B Supervisor, Head Chef.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Timing & Readiness:**
   * Approach the table to take orders within 3 to 5 minutes after delivering menus or when guests close their menus.
   * Stand upright, maintaining pleasant eye contact and open posture.
2. **Suggestive Selling & Upselling:**
   * Highlight daily fresh seafood catches (e.g., fresh lapu-lapu or local Gamay mud crabs) and resort specialties (e.g., Samar-style cooked seafood in coconut cream).
   * Suggest an appetizer or signature drink to begin the meal.
3. **Recording the Order:**
   * Write down orders clearly on the server order pad using standard seat numbering (Seat 1 starts closest to server's left, going clockwise).
   * Record specific preferences: spice level, meat temperatures, ice preference, or allergen warnings (e.g., shellfish, peanut allergies).
4. **Order Confirmation:**
   * Read back the complete order to the guest to ensure absolute accuracy: *"To confirm, Sir, we have one Sinigang na Lapu-Lapu, one Crispy Pata, and two iced teas with light ice. Is that correct?"*
5. **Menu Retrieval:**
   * Collect physical menus unless guests request to keep one.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Fresh Catch Adjustments:** Fresh seafood availability depends on local Gamay fishing yields. Servers must know *exactly* what fresh catch arrived that morning before taking orders.
* **Spice & Sourness Preferences:** Local Samar cuisine often balances rich coconut and chili (*sili*). Always ask domestic and foreign tourists their preferred chili heat and sourness level for local dishes.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Read-Back Accuracy | 100% order read-back completed | Waitstaff |
| Allergen Inquiry | Mandatory check for dietary restrictions | Waitstaff |

### SOP-FB-07: FOOD PREPARATION STANDARDS

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-07
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations / Kitchen
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To establish uniform culinary preparation, recipe adherence, hygiene, and cooking safety for all dishes produced in Marcela's kitchen.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Head Chef, Sous Chef, Line Cooks.
* **Secondary:** Kitchen Stewards.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Recipe & Prep Standard Adherence:**
   * All dishes must strictly follow Marcela's standardized recipe cards (exact yields, ingredient measurements, and cooking times).
2. **Sanitary Prep Area Setup:**
   * Clean and sanitize stainless steel prep tables prior to starting prep work.
   * Use color-coded cutting boards:
     * **RED:** Raw red meat
     * **YELLOW:** Raw poultry
     * **BLUE:** Raw seafood/fish
     * **GREEN:** Fruits & vegetables
     * **WHITE:** Ready-to-eat / cooked foods / dairy
3. **Thawing & Defrosting:**
   * Thaw frozen meats in refrigerator (2°C–4°C) 24 hours in advance. Never thaw meat at ambient room temperature or in standing unheated water.
4. **Core Internal Temperature Cooking Standards:**
   * Cook all protein items to safe internal temperatures verified via probe thermometer:
     * **Poultry:** 74°C (165°F) for 15 seconds
     * **Pork / Ground Meat:** 70°C (158°F) for 15 seconds
     * **Seafood / Fish:** 63°C (145°F) for 15 seconds
5. **Holding Temperatures:**
   * Hot food held above 60°C (140°F); cold food held below 4°C (40°F).

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Fresh Seafood Handling:** Local Gamay seafood must be cleaned, descaled, and gutted immediately upon morning delivery. Store on crushed ice inside cold storage until prep to prevent rapid decomposition in ambient tropical warmth.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Recipe Compliance | 100% adherence to standard taste/weight | Head Chef |
| Cross-Contamination | Zero misuse of color-coded cutting boards | Sous Chef |
| Cooking Temps | 100% compliance with internal target temperatures | Line Cook |

### SOP-FB-09: FOOD SERVICE PROCEDURE

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-09
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To define professional food delivery techniques, guest interaction etiquette, and order sequencing during meal service.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Waitstaff, Food Runners.
* **Secondary:** F&B Supervisor.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Food Pick-Up at Pass:**
   * Pick up food within 1 minute of expediter bell ringing.
   * Verify table number and match dish to seat numbers on server slip.
   * Always carry plates using service trays or multi-plate carrying techniques. Never put thumbs inside or near plate rims.
2. **Serving Order & Etiquette:**
   * Serve ladies first, then elderly, children, gentlemen, and host last.
   * Serve food from the guest's **LEFT** side with your left hand whenever space permits.
   * Announce dish clearly upon serving: *"Your Sinigang na Lapu-Lapu, Ma'am. Please be careful, the bowl is hot."*
3. **Table Maintenance During Meal:**
   * Check guest satisfaction within 2 minutes (or 2 bites): *"How is everything tasting for you today, Sir?"*
   * Refill water glasses without being requested whenever filled below 1/3 level.
4. **Clearing Tables (Bussing):**
   * Clear dirty dishes from the guest's **RIGHT** side using your right hand after all guests at table have finished eating (or upon explicit guest request).
   * Never stack dirty plates directly in front of seated guests.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Family-Style Dining Etiquette:** Many local and Filipino resort guests prefer sharing dishes family-style. Provide adequate extra serving spoons (*pangsandok*) and small individual rice plates (*platito*) for all shared platters without waiting to be asked.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Service Timing | Dishes delivered within 15-20 minutes of ordering | F&B Supervisor |
| Two-Minute Check | Completed for 100% of dining tables | Waitstaff |
| Clearing Etiquette | Dishes cleared silently and politely from right | Busser/Waitstaff |

### SOP-FB-12: KITCHEN SANITATION

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FB-12
* **Department:** Food & Beverage Operations / Kitchen
* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To maintain uncompromising clean, sanitized, hazard-free kitchen environments meeting Philippine Department of Health (DOH) sanitation standards.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Head Steward, Kitchen Stewards, Line Cooks.
* **Secondary:** Head Chef, Safety Officer.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Clean-As-You-Go (CAYG) Rule:**
   * Maintain continuous cleaning during shift. Wipe prep surfaces immediately after processing raw materials using food-safe sanitizer solution.
2. **3-Compartment Sink Dishwashing:**
   * **Sink 1 (Wash):** Warm water (45°C) with commercial dishwashing detergent.
   * **Sink 2 (Rinse):** Clean warm water wash-off.
   * **Sink 3 (Sanitize):** Chemical sanitizing solution (100–200 ppm chlorine or thermal hot water >77°C) submerged for minimum 1 minute.
   * Air-dry all kitchen utensils and crockery on stainless steel racks. Never towel-dry dishes.
3. **Trash & Waste Disposal:**
   * Empty kitchen trash cans when 3/4 full or at minimum every 4 hours.
   * Use pedal-operated lined trash bins. Never touch trash bin lids with hands.
4. **Daily Deep Sanitation Schedule:**
   * End-of-shift scrubbing of kitchen floors using degreaser and hot water mop.
   * Sanitize all food contact stainless tables, stove knobs, refrigerator handles, and door pulls.
5. **Grease Trap Maintenance:**
   * Clean and clear kitchen grease traps daily at end-of-night shutdown.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **High-Humidity Mold Prevention:** Coastal humidity in Samar accelerates mold growth on damp surfaces. Ensure all kitchen walls, silicone seals, and storage racks are completely dried and sanitized daily.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|

* **Effective Date:** October 2026 | **Revision:** 1.0

#### 1. Purpose & Scope
To provide nutritious, hygienic, and balanced daily meals to all resort duty personnel in designated staff dining areas while maintaining strict cost control and kitchen discipline.

#### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary:** Duty Staff Cook, Sous Chef.
* **Secondary:** Head Chef, HR Officer.

#### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Meal Schedule & Rotations:**
   * Serve staff meals at scheduled non-peak hours in staff canteen/canteen area:
     * **Duty Breakfast:** 05:30 AM – 06:15 AM
     * **Duty Lunch:** 11:00 AM – 01:30 PM (staggered 30-min shifts)
     * **Duty Dinner:** 05:00 PM – 07:30 PM (staggered 30-min shifts)
2. **Menu Planning & Nutrition:**
   * Plan weekly staff meal menus in advance, ensuring balanced protein, carbohydrates, and vegetables.
   * Staff meals must be freshly cooked and prepared specifically for staff, or utilize safe, unserved prep surplus managed under strict hygiene standards.
3. **Dining Hygiene & Protocol:**
   * Staff must attend staff meal in designated canteen only. Eating or snacking in guest dining areas, bar counter, or kitchen prep lines is strictly prohibited.
   * Staff must clean their own plates, utensils, and tray tables after finishing meals.
4. **Kitchen Consumption Controls:**
   * Unrecorded sampling, grazing, or personal meal preparation on kitchen line by line cooks or servers is considered a security/policy violation.

#### 4. Localized Samar Context
* **Hydration & Warm Weather Nutrition:** Working in Gamay's warm climate causes rapid dehydration. The staff dining area must be continuously supplied with clean, chilled drinking water, electrolytes, and rice options to support physically demanding resort shifts.

#### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
| QA Item | Standard / Metric | Verified By |
|---|---|---|
| Schedule Adherence | Staff meals served punctually without disrupting guest ops | HR / F&B Sup |
| Canteen Sanitation | 100% clean and sanitized staff dining area | Duty Staff Cook |
| Menu Variety | Balanced weekly menu posted every Sunday | Head Chef |
