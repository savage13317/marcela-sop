### OPERATIONS & FRONT OFFICE SUPERVISOR (`POS-02`)

* **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager / Resort Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Guest Services Officer (`POS-03`), Night Auditor (`POS-04`), and Flexible On-Call Pool (`FLX-01` to `FLX-03`)

* **Role Summary:** Directs daily Front Office operations, manages staff shift scheduling, and coordinates cross-departmental guest service delivery and flexible roster activations under the resort's lean operational framework.

* **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties

1. **Front Office Operational Management:** Oversee daily front desk workflows, guest check-in/check-out procedures, and reservation accuracy (`SOP-FO-01`, `SOP-FO-04`, `SOP-FO-07`, `SOP-FO-14`).

2. **Shift Scheduling & Roster Activation:** Prepare weekly staff shift schedules and monitor hotel occupancy/event calendars to trigger flexible on-call personnel (`SOP-HR-05`, `SOP-HR-06`, `FLX-01`, `FLX-02`, `FLX-03`).

3. **Guest Service Recovery & Escalations:** Handle Level-2 guest complaints, complex service recoveries, and authorized room transfers (`SOP-FO-11`, `SOP-FO-12`).

4. **Cashiering & Shift Reconciliation:** Conduct daily shift handovers, supervise front desk cashiering, and review end-of-shift revenue balancing (`SOP-FO-15`, `SOP-FO-21`, `SOP-FO-22`).

5. **Operational Compliance & Health Audits:** Assist the General Manager in executing daily SOP checklists and health/safety monitoring across public guest areas (`SOP-FO-06`, `SOP-SS-01`, `SOP-SS-14`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties

1. **Function Hall Event Support:** Assist with event guest reception, registration, and client coordination during function hall events (`SOP-FH-06`, `SOP-FH-09`).

2. **Night Audit Relief:** Cover Night Audit duties (`POS-04`) during scheduled rest days, emergency leaves, or surge periods (`SOP-FO-22`).

3. **Public Area & Property Inspections:** Conduct routine walk-throughs of lobby and pool areas to ensure cleanliness and safety when Housekeeping lead is off-site (`SOP-HK-13`, `SOP-FM-17`).

#### KPI Metrics

* **Check-In Efficiency:** Average guest check-in processing time maintained under 3 minutes.

* **Flexible Roster Precision:** $100\%$ accuracy in triggering on-call staffing relative to occupancy and event thresholds.

* **Guest Satisfaction Rate:** Zero unhandled or escalated front office guest complaints.

* **Administrative Adherence:** $100\%$ compliance with weekly scheduling and timekeeping submissions.
