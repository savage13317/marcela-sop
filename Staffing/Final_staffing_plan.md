# Marcela's Resort Hotel

## Optimized Lean Core Staffing \& Organizational Structure Plan

**Property:** Marcela's Resort Hotel  
**Location:** Gamay, Northern Samar, Philippines  
**Property Scope:** 30 Guest Rooms • Restaurant • Function Hall • Swimming Pool  
**Operating Model:** Optimized Core Regular Workforce + Flexible On-Call Roster

\---

## 1\. Executive Summary \& Strategy Revision

Following a comprehensive audit against the **Master Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Manual (147 SOPs across 8 Modules)**, the baseline core staffing model has been adjusted from **8 to 11 Regular Core Employees**.

While the initial 8-employee model reduced payroll, operational analysis revealed critical single points of failure (SPOFs), legal/safety compliance gaps (pool supervision, food safety), lack of night audit/revenue control, and severe risk during disaster/typhoon situations in Northern Samar.

### The Revised Strategy: 11 Core + 16–20 On-Call Pool

> \*\*11 Regular Core Employees keep the resort operating safely, legally, and 24/7. The flexible on-call roster expands operational capacity during high weekend occupancy and event surges.\*\*

### Targeted Workforce Matrix

|Workforce Category|Headcount|Operational Purpose \& Coverage|
|-|-:|-|
|**Core Regular Employees**|**11**|Guarantees 24/7 front desk/night audit, baseline housekeeping, continuous maintenance, daily kitchen prep/sanitation, and administrative control.|
|**On-Call / Flexible Pool**|**16–20**|Pre-screened, trained roster activated strictly for peak weekend occupancy, weddings, corporate events, and surge periods.|
|**Contracted / Outsourced**|Variable|Licensed security agency, IT/network retainer, and specialized event AV contractors.|

## 3\. Departmental Coverage \& Gap Resolution Analysis

The addition of 3 regular core roles directly addresses the high-risk operational gaps identified in the baseline plan:

```
\[Old Baseline Model: 8 Core Staff] ──> Critical Gaps: No Night Coverage, HK Overload, Solo Cook/Hygiene Risk
                                          │
                                          ▼
\[Revised Model: 11 Core Staff]    ──> Fixed: 24/7 Front Desk, Multi-person HK Team, Dedicated Kitchen Sanitation
```

### Key Gap Fixes

1. **24/7 Operational Continuity \& Night Audit (`SOP-FO-22`, `SOP-SS-12`)**

   * *Old Issue:* No overnight core staff forced outsourced security guards to perform front desk check-ins and key handling, violating cash controls.
   * *Fix:* **Night Auditor (Staff #4)** ensures proper night audit daily revenue reconciliation, late check-ins, overnight emergency coordination, and internal financial controls.
2. **Housekeeping Capacity \& Rest Day Management (`SOP-HK-02 to 06`)**

   * *Old Issue:* 2 HK staff physically could not clean 30 turnover rooms plus manage daily laundry, public areas, and mandatory rest days.
   * *Fix:* **3-person HK team (Lead + 2 Attendants)** provides 18 working shifts per week, comfortably handling daily room turnovers, public areas, in-house linen washing, and staggered rest days.
3. **Food Safety \& Kitchen Sanitation (`SOP-FB-12 to 15`)**

   * *Old Issue:* A single cook receiving meat, prepping, cooking, plating, and washing dishes risks severe cross-contamination.
   * *Fix:* **Assistant Cook / Steward (Staff #9)** handles dishwashing, receiving, and sanitation. During low-demand weekdays, this role doubles as the F\&B server for walk-in/hotel breakfast guests.
4. **Pool \& Life Safety Protocol (`SOP-FM-17 to 26`, `SOP-SS-08`)**

   * *Old Issue:* Unmonitored swimming pool during off-peak days created severe drowning liabilities.
   * *Fix:* Clear operational rule: When certified on-call lifeguards are off-duty, the **Maintenance \& Pool Tech** performs chemical checks, and strict "Use Pool at Own Risk / Non-Swimmer Warning" safety signage (`SOP-FM-23`) is strictly enforced.

## 7\. Disaster Readiness Protocol: Typhoon \& Emergency Operations

Gamay, Northern Samar is situated in a direct typhoon path. SOP Module 06 (**Safety, Security \& Typhoon Preparedness**) requires an explicit staffing emergency contingency:

```text
\[Typhoon Alert Level Signal #2 Raised]
                   │
                   ▼
  AUTOMATIC STAY-IN ACTIVATION (SOP-SS-15)
  ├── 1 Management Lead (GM or Ops Supervisor)
  ├── 1 Maintenance Tech (Generator \& Power - SOP-FM-08)
  ├── 1 Housekeeping Utility (Water Ingress/Spills - SOP-SS-19)
  └── 1 Night Audit / FO (Guest Emergency Comms - SOP-SS-22)
```

* **On-Call Reserve Mandate:** When Typhoon Signal #2 or higher is declared, pre-designated local on-call staff transition to guaranteed stay-in hazard pay status to assist with property boarding (`SOP-SS-16`), food prep for stranded guests, and post-storm damage cleanup (`SOP-SS-20`).

## 9\. Conclusion \& Governance

The revised **11 Core Regular + 16–20 Flexible On-Call Model** strikes the optimal balance for **Marcela's Resort Hotel**:

1. **Fixed Overhead Control:** Protects resort profitability during low-demand weekdays.
2. **Comprehensive SOP Compliance:** Eliminates critical single points of failure across all 147 SOPs.
3. **Risk \& Safety Isolation:** Protects the resort against pool liabilities, food safety incidents, revenue leakage, and severe typhoon emergencies.
