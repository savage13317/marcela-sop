Module 05: Facilities, Engineering & Swimming Pool

SOPs for facilities maintenance, engineering systems, pool operations, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and safety inspections

FM-01 Daily Facilities Inspection

Objective & Scope

To perform systematic daily morning walkthrough inspections across all physical structures, guest spaces, utility rooms, and public grounds to identify maintenance deficiencies, safety hazards, or equipment malfunctions before they impact guests.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Maintenance Supervisor: Conducts primary morning inspection walkthrough and assigns work tickets.
  • Duty Maintenance Technician: Assists in testing mechanical and electrical fixtures during the walkthrough.
  • General Manager: Performs random spot checks and reviews daily inspection logs.

Execution Procedure

  1. Initiate Morning Walkthrough (07:00 AM): Retrieve the Daily Facilities Walkthrough Checklist and master keys from the Engineering Office.
  2. Public Areas & Lobby Inspection: Inspect all interior and exterior lighting fixtures for burnt-out bulbs or loose wiring. Check lobby air conditioning operation, target setpoint at 22°C-24°C. Verify doors, glass panels, locks, and automatic closures operate smoothly.
  3. Walkway & Grounds Inspection: Check pathway pavers, perimeter walls, and outdoor lamps for cracks, trip hazards, or weather damage. Verify clear drainage pathways along garden perimeters.
  4. Utility & Plant Room Inspection: Inspect main circuit breaker panels for excessive heat, buzzing sounds, or tripped breakers. Record water supply pressure gauge readings (40-60 psi). Inspect transfer pump and booster pump for leaks, abnormal vibration, or overheating. Check backup diesel generator fuel gauge (≥ 80% full) and battery voltage meter (12V / 24V).
  5. Log & Action Assignment: Log all identified defects into the digital PMS Maintenance Module or Physical Engineering Logbook. Issue immediate work tickets for high-priority items.

Localized Context

Salt Air Spray & Corrosion: Gamay's coastal atmosphere accelerates rust on outdoor metal lamps, door hinges, and screws. Inspect exterior metal fixtures daily for salt crusting and early corrosion. Heavy Rainfall Accumulation: During the Samar rainy season, check garden drainage basins daily to prevent localized pooling near ground-floor guest rooms.

FM-02 Preventive Maintenance Program

Objective & Scope

To maintain a scheduled, proactive maintenance calendar for all guest rooms, mechanical equipment, HVAC systems, and building structures to extend asset life and minimize unexpected operational breakdowns.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Chief Engineer / Maintenance Supervisor: Manages the Master PM Schedule, orders spare parts, and oversees execution.
  • Maintenance Technicians: Executes scheduled PM tasks according to equipment checklists.
  • Front Office Manager: Coordinates room blocks for Room Preventive Maintenance (RPM) cycles.

Execution Procedure

  1. Master PM Calendar Maintenance: Establish annual, quarterly, monthly, and weekly preventive maintenance schedules covering: Guest Rooms (RPM): Cycle through all 30 rooms (1 room per day, completing a full cycle every month), HVAC Systems: Deep coil cleaning and filter washing quarterly, Generators & Pumps: Engine oil, filter, and belt replacements every 250 run hours or semi-annually, Pool Filtration: Backwashing sand filters weekly; deep pump strainer cleanouts bi-weekly.
  2. Room PM (RPM) Execution: Block room with Front Office as "Out of Service - Maintenance" for 4 hours. Test AC cooling performance, flush drain line, clean filters. Inspect all plumbing fixtures, water heater tank, flush valve, and drain flow rate. Inspect electrical outlets, light switches, TV/cable connection, and safe box operation. Check furniture stability, silicon sealants around shower doors, and door lock battery voltage.
  3. Equipment PM Execution: Follow manufacturer service manuals for all heavy equipment (pumps, generator, kitchen freezers, water heaters).
  4. Sign-Off & Logging: Record completed PM tasks in the Master Asset Card and PMS PM Log.

Localized Context

High Humidity & Mold Mitigation: In Samar's tropical climate, AC condensate lines accumulate mold rapidly. Flush all room AC drain lines with a mild bleach solution during monthly RPM to prevent indoor water leaks. Pre-Typhoon PM Surge: Schedule comprehensive PM checks on all roof flashings, gutters, and generator systems prior to the peak typhoon season (September to December).

FM-03 Corrective Maintenance & Work Orders

Objective & Scope

To provide a fast, standardized workflow for reporting, prioritizing, assigning, executing, and closing corrective maintenance work orders across all departments.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Department Heads / Staff (Initiators): Spot defects and submit Work Orders via PMS or physical work order slips.
  • Maintenance Supervisor: Receives, prioritizes, and assigns work tickets to technicians.
  • Duty Maintenance Technician: Executes repairs and reports completion details.

Execution Procedure

  1. Work Order Creation: Upon discovering a defect (e.g., leaking faucet, dead light, noisy AC), the observing staff member creates a Work Order ticket specifying: Location / Room Number, Specific Description of Problem, Date and Time Reported, Priority Level: Urgent (Life safety/Guest discomfort), High (Operational impairment), Normal (Cosmetic/Routine).
  2. Priority Triaging: Urgent (Response < 15 mins): Power failure, active plumbing leak, AC failure in occupied room, door lock failure. High (Response < 2 hours): Public restroom defect, kitchen equipment malfunction, pool light failure. Normal (Response < 24 hours): Loose cabinet hinge, minor touch-up paint, garden fixture repair.
  3. Dispatch & Repair: Technician retrieves ticket, gathers necessary tools/parts from storage, and proceeds to location.
  4. In-Room Repair Protocol: Knock 3 times, announcing "Maintenance Service." If occupied, politely explain reason for visit and estimated duration. Place drop cloth under work zone to protect resort carpet or tile floor. Complete repair cleanly and sanitize work area post-repair.
  5. Testing & Sign-Off: Test repaired equipment in presence of requesting department lead or guest. Obtain requesting department signature on Work Order.

Localized Context

Provincial Parts Sourcing Delays: If replacement parts are not in stock locally in Gamay and require procurement from Catarman or Tacloban (2-5 days lead time), immediately notify Front Office to keep the guest room out of order or offer room change.
