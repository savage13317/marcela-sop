Safety Emergency Procedure

Click to expand details

1. Objective & Scope

To establish an Incident Command System (ICS) and emergency classification framework that directs resort personnel during critical threats, natural disasters, fires, medical crises, or security incidents to safeguard life, property, and business continuity.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Incident Commander (IC): General Manager or highest-ranking Duty Manager on shift.
  • Safety & Security Officer: Manages immediate tactical response and physical perimeter control.
  • Front Office Supervisor: Coordinates guest communications, headcounts, and external authority contacts.
  • Chief Engineer / Maintenance Lead: Manages utility isolation, emergency power, and structural integrity checks.

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. Emergency Level Classification:
    • Level 1 (Minor / Localized): Isolated event manageable by shift staff (e.g., minor pipe leak, localized small fire quickly extinguished, minor guest injury).
    • Level 2 (Moderate / Escalated): Requires multi-departmental coordination and potential local municipal assistance (e.g., localized structural damage, grid power blackout exceeding 4 hours, severe medical emergency, active intruder).
    • Level 3 (Major / Critical Emergency): Severe threat to life or property requiring total resort response and full activation of municipal disaster agencies (e.g., Signal #3+ Typhoon, uncontrolled fire, structural failure, drowning).
  2. Incident Command System (ICS) Activation:
    • Upon escalation to Level 2 or Level 3, the IC declares ICS activation via two-way radio Channel 1.
    • Command Center established at the Front Desk Office (Primary) or Executive Office (Secondary).
  3. Role Assignment & Execution:
    • IC delegates operational roles: Security (Perimeter/Access), Engineering (Utilities/Safety), Front Office (Guest Accounting), F&B (Emergency Rations).
  4. Log & Timeline Keeping:
    • IC designates a log keeper to document all decisions, authority calls, timeline events, and casualty/injury reports in real time.

4. Localized Context

Gamay Provincial Infrastructure Realities: Medical and fire response times in Gamay, Northern Samar may extend past standard urban benchmarks due to geographical distance and road conditions. The hotel must operate as a self-sufficient emergency unit for at least 2 - 4 hours before major provincial assistance arrives.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] ICS Command Center fully operable within 10 minutes of emergency escalation.
  • [ ] Radio Channel 1 clear and dedicated exclusively to Emergency Command.
  • [ ] Master emergency log initiated and maintained continuously during the event.
  • Target Metric: 100% staff role clarity during drills; zero response delays due to command confusion.

Safety Emergency Procedure

1. Objective & Scope

To maintain rigid fire prevention standards, inspect fire suppression hardware, enforce safe LPG and electrical handling, and conduct regular maintenance to eliminate fire risks across all 30 rooms, restaurant kitchen, function hall, and maintenance facilities.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Safety Officer / Chief Engineer: Oversees fire safety compliance, extinguisher servicing, and alarm testing.
  • Head Chef: Enforces kitchen fire safety, grease trap cleaning, and LPG shutoff routines.
  • Housekeeping Supervisor: Ensures emergency hallways, fire doors, and stairwells remain unobstructed.

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. Monthly Fire Extinguisher Audit:
    • Inspect all ABC Dry Chemical, CO₂, and Wet Chemical (K-Class) fire extinguishers across the property.
    • Check pressure gauge (needle must remain strictly in the green zone).
    • Verify safety pin and tamper seal are intact. Sign and date inspection tag.
  2. Kitchen Fire Safety Protocols:
    • Kitchen wet chemical suppression system above commercial stoves inspected semi-annually.
    • Exhaust hood filters cleaned weekly to prevent heavy grease accumulation.
    • LPG main supply line valves closed tightly every night post-kitchen closure.
  3. Electrical Fire Hazard Audits:
    • Daily check of utility panels for loose wiring, burning odors, or tripping breakers.
    • Ban high-wattage guest appliances (e.g., portable electric stoves) not provided by the resort.
  4. Quarterly Fire Alarm & Smoke Detector Testing:
    • Test optical smoke detectors in guest rooms, hallways, and function hall quarterly using aerosol smoke testers.

4. Localized Context

Coastal Corrosion on Fire Equipment: Salt air in Gamay corrodes fire extinguisher nozzles and steel brackets. Coat metal brackets with anti-corrosion grease quarterly and replace rusted safety pins immediately.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] 100% of fire extinguishers inspected, pressurized, and signed monthly.
  • [ ] Kitchen exhaust hoods deep-cleaned weekly without fail.
  • [ ] LPG safety shut-off valve verified closed every night at F&B close.
  • Target Metric: Zero fire incidents; 100% pass rate on Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP Gamay) inspections.

Safety Emergency Procedure

1. Objective & Scope

To define the rapid, step-by-step verification and response procedure upon activation of the main Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) to prevent panic, identify false alarms, or initiate immediate fire suppression and evacuation.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Desk Agent: Monitors Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) and dispatches floor response.
  • Duty Security Officer / Duty Technician: Proceeds immediately to indicated zone for physical verification.
  • Duty Manager: Takes overall command of response and decides on building evacuation.

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. FACP Signal Triggered:
    • Alarm horn sounds at Front Desk panel indicating specific zone (e.g., "Zone 3 - 2nd Floor Guest Corridor").
    • Front Desk Agent notes time and zone, silences panel buzzer locally, but DOES NOT reset the system.
  2. Immediate Physical Verification (Target Time: < 2 mins):
    • Front Desk dispatches Duty Security and Duty Tech via radio: "Security, verify Zone 3 immediately."
    • Security runner proceeds to zone with primary ABC fire extinguisher and master access card.
  3. Condition Assessment & Reporting:
    • Case A: FALSE ALARM (Dust, insect, steam): Security reports "Code Green - False Alarm" back to Front Desk. Tech resets panel. Front Desk logs cause.
    • Case B: REAL FIRE / UNCONTROLLED SMOKE: Security shouts warning, attempts initial suppression using extinguisher if small (< 1 sq. meter). If fire spreads, security reports "CODE RED Zone 3" via radio.
  4. Code Red Alarm Activation:
    • Front Desk Agent immediately hits the General Building Evacuation Siren on FACP.
    • Front Desk Agent dials BFP Gamay Emergency Line immediately.

4. Localized Context

Samar Humidity & False Alarms: High humidity or sea mist can occasionally trigger sensitive smoke detectors. Staff must execute physical verification rapidly (< 120 seconds) before triggering full resort panic, while treating every alarm as a real fire until proven otherwise.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] Zone verification completed on-site within 2 minutes of panel alarm.
  • [ ] Master FACP never reset prior to physical site confirmation.
  • [ ] BFP Gamay emergency speed-dial functional at Front Desk.
  • Target Metric: 100% compliance with the 2-minute verification rule; zero unverified panel resets.

Safety Emergency Procedure

1. Objective & Scope

To execute a swift, orderly, and complete evacuation of all hotel guests, staff, and visitors during an active fire or major life-safety threat using the R.A.C.E. protocol.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Incident Commander (GM/Duty Manager): Directs total building evacuation and liaises with BFP Gamay.
  • Floor Evacuation Wardens (Housekeeping / Security): Sweeps hallways, guest rooms, and public areas.
  • Front Desk Agent: Retrieves Master Guest In-House List, key cards, and emergency mobile phone.
  • Assembly Area Coordinator: Conducts guest headcount at designated assembly zone.

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. Evacuation Declaration: General Evacuation Siren sounded continuous pattern. Emergency Voice Announcement made over public address system: "Attention all guests and staff: Please proceed calmly via the nearest exit to the main assembly area on the front lawn."
  2. Execute R.A.C.E. Protocol:
    • R - Rescue: Guide guests in immediate danger to safety without putting staff lives at unreasonable risk.
    • A - Alarm: Ensure FACP general alarm is active and BFP Gamay called (911 / Local BFP).
    • C - Contain: Close all guest room doors and corridor fire doors behind evacuating persons to starve fire of oxygen.
    • E - Evacuate: Direct everyone to exterior exit routes. NEVER USE ELEVATORS.
  3. Floor Wardens Room Sweep:
    • Wardens knock loudly, unlock every room on assigned corridor: "Fire Alarm! Evacuate immediately!"
    • Mark checked room doors with a designated evacuation tag or leave door wide open once verified empty.
  4. Assembly Area Headcount:
    • Direct all evacuees to Assembly Area A (Main Garden Lawn near Entry Gate).
    • Front Desk Agent cross-checks evacuees against active PMS In-House Guest List and Staff Shift Roster.
    • Report missing persons immediately to BFP Fire Ground Commander.

4. Localized Context

Power Outage During Fire: Fires frequently trip main circuit breakers or damage wiring. Backup battery emergency exit lights automatically illuminate exit routes for 30-60 minutes. Floor wardens must carry heavy-duty LED flashlights attached to their duty belts at all times.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] PMS active guest list printed or accessed digitally within 3 minutes of evacuation signal.
  • [ ] Complete building floor sweep completed in < 5 minutes.
  • [ ] Headcount accounting completed at assembly area within 10 minutes.
  • Target Metric: 100% guest and staff safety accounting; zero structural entrapment.

1. Objective & Scope

To ensure all structural emergency exits, escape corridors, stairwells, and primary outdoor assembly zones remain illuminated, clearly signed, unobstructed, and ready for immediate deployment 24/7.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Safety Officer: Inspects exit hardware, emergency push bars, and signage illumination.
  • Public Area Houseman: Ensures no furniture, linen carts, or boxes block exit pathways.
  • Security Guard on Duty: Verifies exit doors are unlocked from the inside (panic hardware operational).

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. Daily Pathway & Exit Door Inspections:
    • Walk all emergency stairwells, corridors, and exterior exit gates daily at 07:00 AM.
    • Verify panic push bars on secondary emergency exit doors open smoothly outwards with < 15 lbs of pressure.
    • Ensure zero padlocks or chains are placed on emergency exit doors during operational hours.
  2. Exit Signage & Emergency Lighting Standards:
    • Photoluminescent or powered LED "EXIT / LABASAN" signs must remain lit continuously.
    • Test battery backup exit light units monthly by pressing the test button; lights must remain illuminated for full 30-second test.
  3. Assembly Zone Maintenance:
    • Assembly Area A (Primary): Main Garden Lawn near Front Entrance Gate.
    • Assembly Area B (Secondary / Tsunami Evacuation): High-ground concrete structure / Function Hall 2nd Floor (if coastal storm surge or tsunami alert coincides).
    • Keep assembly area signboards clean, unobstructed, and well-lit at night.

4. Localized Context

Tsunami / Storm Surge Coincidental Risk: Gamay is a coastal Samar municipality. If a fire or evacuation coincides with a strong offshore earthquake or marine surge alert, Assembly Area B (designated high ground / elevated structure) must be utilized instead of low-lying beach lawns.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] 100% of emergency exit doors unlocked from inside and opening freely.
  • [ ] Zero obstructions (linen carts, trash bins, maintenance ladders) in fire exits.
  • [ ] Emergency lights tested monthly and functional.
  • Target Metric: Zero exit pathway safety violations during internal or external audits.

Safety Emergency Procedure

Click to expand details

1. Objective & Scope

To provide immediate, competent basic first aid assessment, life support stabilization, and medical referral for guests or staff experiencing sudden illness, trauma, or injury on resort grounds.

2. Responsible Staff Roles

  • Certified First Responder (Shift Staff / Security): Administers immediate basic first aid / CPR.
  • Front Office Agent / Duty Manager: Coordinates emergency ambulance calls and guest medical transport.
  • Safety Officer: Maintains first aid kits, AED equipment, and medical supply inventory.

3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

  1. Initial Assessment (Scene Safety & Patient Status):
    • Ensure scene is safe to enter. Approach victim, check responsiveness (A-V-P-U: Alert, Verbal, Pain, Unresponsive).
    • Call for back-up staff and retrieve First Aid Kit + Automated External Defibrillator (AED) from Front Desk.
  2. First Aid Administration:
    • Minor Cut / Abrasion: Clean wound with sterile saline solution, apply povidone-iodine, cover with sterile gauze and adhesive bandage.
    • Heat Exhaustion / Fainting: Move patient to cool shaded area, loosen tight clothing, elevate feet, offer oral rehydration salts (ORS) in small sips if conscious.
    • Severe Trauma / Fractures: Immobilize injured limb, apply direct pressure to heavy bleeding using thick sterile pads. DO NOT move patient with suspected spinal injury unless immediate life threat exists.
  3. Medical Emergency Transport Escalation:
    • If patient exhibits severe symptoms (chest pain, breathing difficulty, deep unconsciousness, severe bleeding, stroke signs):
    • Contact Gamay Rural Health Unit (RHU) / Municipal Health Office (MHO) immediately.
    • Prepare resort emergency vehicle (or local municipal ambulance) for transport to Catarman Provincial Hospital for advanced trauma/cardiac care.
  4. Medical Incident Documentation: Record patient name, room number, incident details, vital signs, first aid given, and transport destination in the Resort Medical Logbook.

4. Localized Context

Transit Distance to Major Hospitals: Gamay RHU provides basic stabilization. Emergency transport to the primary tertiary hospital in Catarman, Northern Samar takes approximately 1.5 - 2 hours by ambulance. Early triage and prompt transport calls are vital for serious conditions.

5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

  • [ ] First Aid kits stationed at Front Desk, Kitchen, Engineering, and Pool Deck fully stocked.
  • [ ] Expiration dates on medications, antiseptics, and burn gels audited monthly.
  • [ ] Shift team includes at least one active BLS/CPR certified staff member per shift.
  • Target Metric: Emergency medical response initiated within < 3 minutes of notification.
