## SOP-FH-01: Function Hall Reservation

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FH-01
* **SOP Title:** Function Hall Reservation
* **Department:** Function Hall & Events / Front Office
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To establish a structured, error-free procedure for receiving, recording, and holding function hall date reservations. This procedure applies to all event inquiries handled by Front Office and Events staff for weddings, birthdays, corporate meetings, government seminars, and private gatherings at Marcela’s Hotel & Resort.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Events Coordinator / Banquet Manager:** Primary owner for log management, calendar verification, and tentative date blocking.
* **Front Desk Agent (FDA):** Receives walk-in or telephone inquiries during off-peak/night shifts and records initial reservation details.
* **General Manager:** Approves non-standard discounts, prolonged pencil bookings, or special multi-day function holds.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Receive Inquiry:** Greet the prospective client warmly. Gather basic details: target event date, event type, estimated guest count, preferred setup, and contact information.
2. **Check Availability:** Cross-check the requested date and time slot against the Master Events Calendar (Digital PMS & Physical Events Logbook). Ensure adequate buffer time ($3\text{ hours}$ before and $2\text{ hours}$ after) for cleaning, setup, and teardown.
3. **Log Pencil Booking:** If the slot is available, log a "Pencil Booking" status. Note the client's name, contact details, proposed event date, and temporary holding expiration date (maximum $7\text{ calendar days}$).
4. **Issue Reservation Hold Notice:** Send an SMS or Email confirmation to the client stating that the date is temporarily held for $7\text{ days}$ pending contract signing and deposit payment.
5. **Manage Expiration:** If no deposit is received by Day 7, contact the client once for confirmation. If no response or commitment is made within $24\text{ hours}$, release the date hold and update the Master Events Calendar.
6. **Confirm Booking:** Upon receipt of the contract and required $50\%$ initial deposit, convert the booking status from "Pencil Booked" to "Firm Booking" in both digital and physical logs.

### 4. Localized Context
* **LGU & Regional Calendar Awareness:** Pay close attention to local Gamay municipal town fiesta dates (July), Provincial Samar events, and local school graduation seasons (March–May) which create high demand for the Function Hall.
* **Power & Weather Buffers:** Inform clients during initial booking about the resort’s $100\%$ generator backup capability to reassure them regarding local NORSAMELCO power supply stability in Northern Samar.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Date logged accurately in both Digital PMS and Physical Events Logbook.
* [ ] $3\text{-hour}$ ingress/egress buffer strictly applied between consecutive events.
* [ ] Pencil booking release policy ($7\text{ days}$) strictly enforced.
* **Target Metric:** $0\%$ double-booking rate; $100\%$ client notification before releasing tentative dates.

## SOP-FH-03: Event Proposal & Quotation

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FH-03
* **SOP Title:** Event Proposal & Quotation
* **Department:** Function Hall & Events / Accounting
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To guide staff in calculating accurate, profitable, and transparent event quotations and issuing official proposals to clients for events hosted at Marcela’s Hotel & Resort.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Events Coordinator:** Prepares the itemized quotation and formal proposal document.
* **Finance / Cashier Manager:** Reviews non-standard pricing, government tax exemptions, and payment terms.
* **General Manager:** Signs off on proposals exceeding $\text{PHP } 100,000$ or custom corporate packages.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Cost Calculation:** Using the Event Inquiry Worksheet, calculate total charges based on:
   * Function Hall Rental Rate (Base rate includes $4\text{ hours}$ with aircon, basic light/sound).
   * Overtime Rate per hour (Hall + Aircon fees).
   * Food & Beverage Cost per head $\times$ minimum guaranteed headcount.
   * Special Setup / Decor / AV Equipment Rental fees.
   * Room accommodation packages (if bundled).
2. **Draft Proposal:** Fill out the standardized **Event Proposal Template**, specifying:
   * Itemized breakdown of rates (Venue, Food, AV, Inclusions).
   * Payment milestones ($50\%$ deposit upon contract signing, $50\%$ full balance $7\text{ days}$ before the event).
   * Included amenities (e.g., free use of pool for $5\text{ VIPs}$, sound system, podium, parking).
3. **Internal Review:** Verify prices against current F&B menu costing sheets and peak/off-peak venue rate cards.
4. **Dispatch:** Send the formal proposal via Email/PDF or present an official printed copy in a folder to the client within $24\text{ hours}$.
5. **Validity Notice:** State clearly on the proposal: *"Quotation valid for 14 calendar days from date of issue."*

### 4. Localized Context
* **Government Billing Compliance:** For Department of Education (DepEd), LGU Gamay, or Provincial Government events, ensure quotations include complete BIR Tax breakdown (VAT/Non-VAT exemptions where applicable) and support Purchase Order (PO) / Agency Voucher terms.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Quotation uses current pricing matrix approved by Resort Management.
* [ ] Payment terms, deposit rules, and overtime fees explicitly listed.
* [ ] Tax calculations verified (BIR compliance).
* **Target Metric:** $0\%$ pricing error rate in issued proposals; $100\%$ proposals delivered within $24\text{ hours}$.

## SOP-FH-07: Table & Chair Arrangement

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FH-07
* **SOP Title:** Table & Chair Arrangement
* **Department:** Housekeeping / Banquet Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To ensure uniform, visually appealing, and ergonomically correct table and chair setups for various event formats.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Banquet Captain:** Oversees table spacing, linen placement, and chair alignment.
* **Banquet Housemen:** Handles heavy lifting, table/chair positioning, and skirting.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Layout Selections:**
   * **Banquet Style (Round Tables):** Space $10\text{-seater}$ round tables $1.8\text{ meters}$ apart from table edge to table edge to permit comfortable server movement.
   * **Classroom Style:** Space long tables $1\text{ meter}$ apart; allow $60\text{ cm}$ per seated participant.
   * **Theater Style:** Space chair rows $80\text{ cm}$ front-to-back; limit to $10\text{ chairs}$ per row block before inserting a $1\text{-meter}$ aisle.
   * **U-Shape / Boardroom:** Align rectangular tables seamlessly, using tablecloth clips for uniform appearance.
2. **Linen Application:**
   * Inspect all tablecloths and chair covers for stains, tears, or wrinkles.
   * Lay main tablecloths centered with equal drop on all sides ($30\text{ cm}$ drop standard).
   * Fit seat covers tightly and align bows/ribbons uniformly if requested.
3. **Table Setting:** Position centerpieces, table numbers, water glasses, and folded napkins according to service standards.
4. **Chair Inspection:** Ensure every chair is stable, wobble-free, clean, and free from structural defects.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Insect and Humidity Safeguards:** Check chair linens carefully for storage dampness or humidity odors prior to dressing chairs. Keep backup dry linens staged in sealed plastic totes in the linen storage room.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Table spacing verified with tape measure or standard stride check.
* [ ] Linens freshly pressed, stain-free, and aligned.
* [ ] Chairs checked for stability and safety.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ compliance with table spacing and linen visual cleanliness standards.

* **Department:** Maintenance / Engineering / IT

### 4. Localized Context
* **Power Instability Protection:** Northern Samar experiences frequent grid voltage fluctuations. **MANDATORY:** All AV equipment MUST be plugged into automatic voltage regulators (AVR) and UPS units to prevent system reboots or damage during generator auto-transfer procedures.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] All floor cables taped down securely.
* [ ] Fresh AA batteries loaded into wireless microphones; spare batteries staged.
* [ ] AVR and UPS active on all AV circuits.

## SOP-FH-10: Catering for Events

* **SOP Code:** SOP-FH-10
* **SOP Title:** Catering for Events
* **Department:** Food & Beverage / Kitchen
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To govern buffet line staging, plated meal service, food temperature management, and hygiene standards for function hall events.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **F&B Supervisor / Banquet Captain:** Manages buffet flow, server assignments, and food replenishment timing.
* **Head Chef:** Ensures food quality, presentation, portion control, and food safety standards.
* **Banquet Servers:** Handles service, water refilling, and clearing dirty dishes (bus-out).

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Buffet Line Staging:**
   * Set up chafing dishes with gel fuel sternos $45\text{ minutes}$ prior to meal time.
   * Fill chafing dish water pans with hot water to ensure rapid heating.
   * Arrange food sequence logically: Salad/Soup $\rightarrow$ Rice $\rightarrow$ Main Courses (Pork/Beef/Poultry/Seafood/Vegetables) $\rightarrow$ Dessert Table $\rightarrow$ Beverage Station.
2. **Food Temperature Control:** Keep hot foods at $\ge 60^\circ\text{C}$ and cold foods/desserts at $\le 4^\circ\text{C}$.
3. **Buffet Opening & Refilling:**
   * Light sterno burners $20\text{ minutes}$ before meal service.
   * Serve VIP/Head tables first if plated or escorted buffet service is specified in BEO.
   * Monitor chafing dish levels; notify kitchen for refilling when a dish reaches $30\%$ remaining capacity. Never let chafing dishes run dry.
4. **Plate Cleansing & Bus-out:** Quietly clear finished plates from guest tables from the right side. Do not stack plates higher than 4 dirty dishes in front of guests.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Regional Waray Palate & Seafood:** Ensure signature dishes (e.g., fresh coastal seafood, coconut milk preparations, local pork roast) are kept properly covered between serve cycles to protect against insects and heat loss in provincial open-air or air-conditioned settings.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Chafing dish temperatures verified before service ($\ge 60^\circ\text{C}$).
* [ ] Serving spoons and tongs positioned for each dish.
* [ ] Buffet line replenished continuously before dishes hit $30\%$ capacity.
* **Target Metric:** $0\%$ food availability gaps during meal hours; $100\%$ compliance with sanitation standards.

## SOP-FH-14: Event Change & Cancellation Procedure

* **SOP Title:** Event Change & Cancellation Procedure
* **Department:** Function Hall & Events / Accounting
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To provide clear guidelines for managing event date modifications, scope downscaling, or cancellations caused by client requests or severe weather force majeure events.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Events Coordinator:** Processes client requests, issues change orders, and recalculates fees.
* **General Manager:** Approves deposit transfers or fee waivers for force majeure cancellations.
* **Finance Manager:** Handles credit memos or refund processing.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Client Request Receipt:** Require client to submit a formal written request (Email, Letter, or Signed Cancellation Form) for any date change or cancellation.
2. **Standard Cancellation Fee Matrix Evaluation:**
   * **$> 30\text{ Days}$ prior to event:** Full deposit retained as consumable credit valid for 6 months, OR refunded minus $20\%$ administrative fee.
   * **$15 - 30\text{ Days}$ prior:** $50\%$ deposit forfeited.
   * **$< 14\text{ Days}$ prior:** $100\%$ deposit forfeited.
3. **Force Majeure Protocol (Typhoons / Natural Disasters):**
   * If PAGASA issues Typhoon Signal No. 2 or higher for Northern Samar, or severe sea/road blockages occur:
   * Waive all cancellation and rebooking penalty fees.
   * Transfer $100\%$ of paid funds as credit toward a rebooked date within 12 months.
4. **Date Modification Processing:** Issue an **Event Modification Notice** detailing the new date, updated headcount, revised menu, and any additional fees. Both parties must sign.
5. **Master Calendar Update:** Immediately update the Digital PMS and physical Master Events Logbook to release or rebook the function hall dates.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Typhoon Preparedness (Samar Belt):** Northern Samar is on the typhoon belt. Management must maintain a compassionate, flexible rebooking policy during typhoon emergencies to preserve community goodwill and resort reputation.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Cancellation request received in writing.
* [ ] Force Majeure conditions verified against official PAGASA bulletins.
* [ ] Master Calendar updated immediately to open cancelled dates for new bookings.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ written documentation for cancellations/date shifts; zero client billing disputes.
