### FRONT DESK & GUEST SERVICES OFFICER (`POS-03`)

* **Reporting Line:** Reports to Operations & Front Office Supervisor (`POS-02`) | Supervises None

* **Role Summary:** Manages daily guest check-in/check-out interactions, PMS/OTA reservation processing, cashiering, and front desk communications while providing secondary support to F&B and event operations under a lean cross-functional model.

* **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties

1. **Guest Check-in & Check-out Operations:** Facilitate seamless guest registration, identity verification, room assignment, key issuance, and checkout settlement (`SOP-FO-04`, `SOP-FO-06`, `SOP-FO-07`, `SOP-FO-08`, `SOP-FO-14`).

2. **Reservations & Digital Channels Management:** Handle direct, phone, and online travel agency (OTA) booking inquiries, reservation modifications, and system entries (`SOP-FO-02`, `SOP-FO-03`, `SOP-DS-10`).

3. **Front Desk Cashiering & Billing:** Process guest payments, cash drops, credit card transactions, and maintain shift balance accuracy (`SOP-FO-15`, `SOP-FO-16`, `SOP-FO-17`).

4. **Guest Inquiries & Concierge Services:** Provide information regarding resort services, local area guidance, and fulfill routine guest requests (`SOP-FO-09`, `SOP-FO-10`).

5. **Shift Handover & Operations Reporting:** Complete daily shift handover documentation and record all pending guest service requests or operational flags (`SOP-FO-21`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties

1. **F&B Order Entry & Off-Peak Service:** Assist with Point-of-Sale (POS) order entry and light guest dining assistance during off-peak windows or staff meal breaks (`SOP-FB-05`, `SOP-FB-06`).

2. **Event & Function Hall Inquiries:** Log initial event inquiries, walk-in function hall leads, and transfer details to the General Manager (`SOP-FH-02`).

3. **Public Area Assistance:** Assist in inspecting lobby cleanliness and reporting immediate maintenance or housekeeping issues when supervisors are off-site (`SOP-HK-13`, `SOP-FM-01`).

#### KPI Metrics

* **Check-In Speed:** Average guest check-in time maintained under 3 minutes per party.

* **Reservation Entry Precision:** $100\%$ accuracy in PMS reservation details, room rates, and guest notes.

* **Cash Balance Accuracy:** Zero cash drawer discrepancies or missing guest billing charge slips.

* **Guest Satisfaction Rating:** Positive guest feedback on front desk service exceeding $90\%$.
