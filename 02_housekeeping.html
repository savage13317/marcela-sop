Module 02: Housekeeping & Laundry

Standard Operating Procedures for room cleaning standards, linen management, public area maintenance, and laundry operations.

Quick Jump to Procedure:

HK-01 Housekeeping Department Opening

Objective & Scope

Standardize morning operational setup to ensure smooth workflow, staff readiness, key distribution, and accurate room assignment tracking across all 30 rooms.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Room Attendants
  • Public Area (PA) Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Clock-in & Uniform Check (6:00 AM):
    • Arrive 15 minutes prior to shift start. Inspect personal grooming, clean uniform, anti-slip rubber footwear, and required name badge.
  2. Key Control Issuing:
    • Receive HK master keys from Front Office desk. Sign the Master Key Log with date, time, key ID, and employee signature.
  3. Daily Morning Briefing (6:15 AM):
    • Review overnight arrivals, high-priority check-outs, VIP stays, special guest requests, and daily weather outlook (e.g., wind velocity, tidal conditions, rainfall forecasts).
  4. Morning Room Status Reconciliation:
    • Retrieve current PMS Room Status Report. Reconcile overnight PMS statuses against physical room statuses with the Front Office Duty Manager.
  5. Trolley & Supply Preparation:
    • Stock housekeeping carts with fresh linens, towels, guest amenities, chemical caddies, vacuum cleaners, and color-coded cleaning cloths according to daily room assignment sheets.
  6. Equipment Check:
    • Verify operational readiness of vacuums, floor scrubbers, spray bottles, emergency flashlights, and two-way radios.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

During rainy mornings or post-typhoon conditions, check for water seepage along outdoor corridors and glass panels. Ensure wet-vacs and floor blowers are plugged into generator-backed outlets if main grid brownouts occur.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Master Key Log 100% signed before shift deployment.
  • Housekeeping carts fully stocked by 6:45 AM.
  • Zero discrepancy between PMS status and physical room status report at morning handover.
HK-02 Daily Guest Room Cleaning

Objective & Scope

Establish systematic room cleaning standards to maintain clinical hygiene, aesthetic appeal, and guest comfort across occupied and vacant rooms.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Room Entry:
    • Knock on guest room door 3 times, stating "Housekeeping." Wait 5 seconds between knocks. If no response, unlock door carefully and announce "Housekeeping" before stepping inside.
  2. Ventilation & Initial Inspection:
    • Open curtains and balcony sliding doors to let in natural light and fresh air. Inspect for property damage, left items, or pest activity.
  3. Trash Removal:
    • Empty all trash cans (bedroom and bathroom), replace poly-liners, and remove organic food waste to avoid attracting insects.
  4. Linen Removal:
    • Strip bed sheets, pillowcases, duvet covers, and bathroom towels. Place soiled linens directly into linen hamper bags on the cart; never lay dirty linen on the bare floor.
  5. Surface Dusting & Disinfection:
    • Wipe all high-touch surfaces (door handles, light switches, remote controls, phone, desk, nightstands) using microfiber cloths and multi-surface disinfectant.
  6. Floor Care:
    • Sweep and mop hard tile floors using neutral pH disinfectant cleaner. Vacuum carpeted or rug areas thoroughly.
  7. Final Quality Check:
    • Restock amenities, adjust air-conditioning setpoint (22°C–24°C), lock patio doors, turn off unnecessary lights, and exit closing door securely.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

High tropical coastal humidity causes rapid mold/mildew buildup. Wipe wooden furniture and metallic fixtures with dry microfiber after damp cleaning. Keep sliding doors closed while AC is running to prevent interior wall condensation.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Cleaning completed within target time (30 mins stay-over / 45 mins check-out).
  • 100% high-touch surface sanitization verified.
  • Zero dampness or residual moisture on furniture or mattress bases.
HK-03 Stay-over Room Cleaning

Objective & Scope

Perform non-intrusive daily maintenance cleaning for in-house guests while respecting guest privacy and personal belongings.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Status Verification:
    • Verify door does not display "Do Not Disturb" (DND). If DND is displayed, log time on worksheet and re-check after 2:00 PM. Contact Front Office if DND remains past 3:00 PM.
  2. Greeting & Entry:
    • If guest is present, politely ask permission to clean and inquire about preferred cleaning times or specific service requests.
  3. Bed Servicing:
    • Remake bed with existing linens if clean, pulling sheets tightly; replace linens if soiled or if guest requested replacement (standard eco-policy replaces sheets every 2nd day).
  4. Bathroom Touch-up:
    • Clean wash basin, toilet seat/bowl, shower screen, replace used towels hung on floor, and replenish toilet paper/toiletries.
  5. Personal Belongings Care:
    • Never move personal money, jewelry, electronics, or open luggage. Arrange personal clothing neatly if left on chairs or bed.
  6. Restocking:
    • Replenish complimentary water bottles, instant coffee/tea bags, and refresh drinking glasses.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Guests returning from island tours or pool areas often bring in sand and wet swimwear. Hang damp swimwear on designated balcony drying racks, sweep sand off tile entryways, and replace damp foot towels daily.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Room cleaned without displacing personal guest items or valuables.
  • Daily complimentary water and coffee items 100% replenished.
  • Floor sand and beach grit completely removed.
HK-04 Check-out Room Cleaning

Objective & Scope

Restore turn-over rooms to pristine, inspection-ready standard for incoming guests following guest departure.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Lost Item & Minibar Check:
    • Conduct immediate walkthrough for items left behind by departed guest and verify minibar consumption. Report found items and minibar charges to Front Office within 5 minutes of entry.
  2. Complete Linen & Towel Strip:
    • Strip 100% of bed linens, pillow protectors (if stained), bath towels, hand towels, and bath mats. Place into trolley hamper immediately.
  3. Deep Sanitation:
    • Disinfect bathroom fixtures, toilet bowl, shower floor, telephone, remote controls, door knobs, and closet interiors using hospital-grade sanitizer.
  4. Fresh Bed Setup:
    • Install fresh mattress protector, fitted sheet, flat sheet, pillowcases, and duvet cover per resort visual standards.
  5. In-room Inventory & Amenities Setup:
    • Restock complete room setup: hanger count (6 per closet), slippers, hair dryer, laundry bag, mug set, and full toilet amenity set.
  6. Floor & AC Servicing:
    • Mop tile floors with sanitizer solution, clean AC filter mesh if dusty, set AC to 24°C eco-mode, and perform final room scenting.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Check for salt spray film on glass windows and balcony doors facing the coast; wipe window glass using anti-smear glass cleaner to maintain clear ocean views.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Turn-around time per departure room under 45 minutes.
  • 100% full linen change completed.
  • Room passes Supervisor inspection on first attempt.
HK-05 Room Inspection Procedure

Objective & Scope

Validate room cleanliness, mechanical functionality, and amenity standards prior to releasing rooms as "Ready for Check-in" in the PMS.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Executive Housekeeper

Execution Procedure

  1. Entry Approach:
    • Approach room, verify room status on mobile PMS/checklist, enter room, and initiate a 360-degree clockwise visual inspection.
  2. Touch & Spot Inspection:
    • Bedding: Check sheet tautness, pillow alignment, bed skirt clearance, and inspect for stray hair or lint.
    • Furniture & Surfaces: Check top of wardrobe, behind TV, under bed frame, and inside drawer interiors for dust or debris.
    • Bathroom: Inspect tile grout, mirror clarity, drain flow rate, water pressure, toilet flush efficiency, and towel folding uniformity.
    • Appliances: Test remote controls, TV channels, air conditioner cooling performance, mini-fridge temperature, electric kettle, and light fixtures.
  3. Defect Logging:
    • If minor defect found (e.g., missed dust spot), direct Room Attendant to correct immediately. If technical fault found (e.g., air-con leak), log work order in maintenance system.
  4. Final Sign-off:
    • Sign physical Room Inspection Log and update room status to "Vacant Clean & Inspected" (VCI) in PMS.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Inspect water fixtures for mineral buildup or brownish tap discharge common in coastal deep wells; ensure inline water filters are active and water runs crystal clear.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% of vacant clean rooms physically inspected prior to guest check-in.
  • Rejection rate on Front Office guest move-in under 1%.
  • Inspection log updated in real-time in PMS.

HK-07 Bathroom Cleaning & Sanitization

Objective & Scope

Eliminate pathogens, mineral deposits, mold, and odor from all guest bathroom surfaces to achieve clinical-grade hygiene.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Chemical Pre-treatment:
    • Spray multi-surface bathroom disinfectant inside toilet bowl, shower walls, floor tiles, and basin. Allow 5 minutes contact dwell time.
  2. Basin & Countertop Cleaning:
    • Scrub sink basin, chrome faucets, pop-up stopper, and countertop using non-abrasive sponge. Polish fixtures dry with microfiber cloth.
  3. Shower Enclosure & Tub:
    • Scrub wall tiles, glass door enclosure, shower head, and floor drain. Remove soap scum, hair buildup from drain grate, and hard water spots.
  4. Toilet Bowl Sanitization:
    • Scrub interior toilet bowl with bowl brush, paying attention to under-rim areas. Wipe down toilet seat, lid, tank exterior, and flush handle using fresh sanitizing wipes.
  5. Mirror & Glass Care:
    • Apply glass cleaner to bathroom mirror and polish thoroughly using lint-free glass cloth to remove water spots and streaks.
  6. Floor Mopping:
    • Mop tile floor starting from furthest corner toward door, using disinfectant floor solution. Allow to dry completely before placing bath mat.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Northern Samar's high humidity accelerates mold growth in grout lines. Apply anti-fungal disinfectant solution twice weekly and verify exhaust fans run continuously during cleaning.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero hair, water spots, or soap residue on shower enclosures and chrome fixtures.
  • Toilet bowl sanitized and sealed with paper hygiene band.
  • Floor completely dry prior to guest availability.
HK-08 Bed Making & Linen Standards

Objective & Scope

Ensure consistent, crisp, wrinkle-free, and comfortable bed setups matching 4-star boutique resort standards.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Mattress & Protector Inspection:
    • Position mattress centered on bed base. Smooth out mattress protector, ensuring full elastic corner coverage and checking for stains or tears.
  2. Bottom Sheet Application:
    • Spread bottom fitted sheet over mattress protector. Pull corners tightly under mattress, ensuring zero wrinkles on sleep surface.
  3. Flat Sheet & Duvet Dressing:
    • Lay top flat sheet wrong-side-up, aligning top edge with mattress headboard end.
    • Insert duvet inner into crisp duvet cover, ensuring corners align perfectly and button/zipper closure is fastened.
    • Fold top sheet back over duvet by 15-20 cm to create a neat top cuff.
  4. Mitred Cornering:
    • Tuck sheet and duvet edges at foot of bed using tight 45-degree hospital/mitred corners.
  5. Pillow Dressing:
    • Insert pillow inner into pillowcase without allowing inner pillow to touch floor or uniform. Plump pillow and align seam neatly. Position 2 sleeping pillows upright against headboard, followed by decorative accent pillows centered in front.
  6. Bed Runner:
    • Lay resort decorative bed runner neatly across lower third of bed.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Due to high coastal humidity, store freshly ironed linen in dehumidified linen store rooms to prevent damp odors before bed dressing.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Sheets pulled 100% taut without wrinkles or visible sagging.
  • Mitred corners tight and symmetrical on both sides.
  • Pillows plump, upright, and uniform across all rooms.
HK-09 Towel Replacement Procedure

Objective & Scope

Maintain pristine towel inventory per room while honoring environmental towel reuse policies.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Inventory Verification Standard per Room (Base 2 Pax):
    • 2 Bath Towels (70 cm x 140 cm, white, 500+ GSM)
    • 2 Hand Towels (40 cm x 70 cm)
    • 2 Face Towels / Washcloths (30 cm x 30 cm)
    • 1 Bath Mat (50 cm x 80 cm, heavy-duty weave)
    • 2 Pool/Beach Towels (Resort Blue color-coded)
  2. Environmental Towel Policy Execution:
    • Towels hung neatly on bathroom towel racks mean "I will use them again." Do not replace unless visibly soiled or wet.
    • Towels placed on bathroom floor or inside tub mean "Please replace."
  3. Replacement Handling:
    • Collect used towels, place in linen bag, and restock clean, dry, fluffy towels matching standard folding patterns.
  4. Pool Towel Exchange:
    • Replace blue pool towels daily or upon guest request at HK station or poolside desk.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

High ocean air salinity and rain prevent towels from air-drying quickly on balconies. Ensure guest towels are dried thoroughly in industrial laundry dryers to maintain fluffiness and avoid mildew smells.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Exact towel count matches room category allocation.
  • Towels free from frayed edges, discoloration, or musty damp odors.
  • Towels folded neatly with edges facing away from bathroom entrance view.

HK-11 Minibar / In-room Inventory Control

Objective & Scope

HK-12 Deep Cleaning Procedure

Objective & Scope

Execute intensive, periodic deep cleaning tasks beyond daily room cleaning to preserve room assets and maintain high standards of hygiene.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Deep Cleaning Team
  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Maintenance Technician

Execution Procedure

  1. Scheduling:
    • Perform deep cleaning on rotational schedule (2 rooms per week during low occupancy) or minimum once every quarter per room.
  2. High Level & Architectural Cleaning:
    • Dust ceiling fan blades, light tracks, high corner moldings, and curtain rods.
    • Vacuum and wash curtains, blackout drapes, and window blinds.
  3. Furniture & Upholstery Care:
    • Shift heavy furniture (beds, sofas, wardrobes) to clean underneath.
    • Shampoo fabric sofa chairs and vacuum bed base enclosures.
    • Polish solid wooden furniture with timber conditioner.
  4. Deep Floor & Grout Scrubbing:
    • Scrub tile floor grout with floor scrubber machine and heavy-duty degreaser/sanitizer. Apply tile floor sealer if required.
  5. AC & Vent Servicing:
    • Coordinate with Engineering to flush AC condensate line, clean evaporator coils, and wash washable air filters.
  6. Post-Deep Clean Inspection:
    • Supervisor conducts comprehensive 50-point inspection before returning room to active inventory.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Coastal salt air and high rainfall create ideal conditions for wall algae and mildew behind wardrobes. Apply anti-mold solution behind all heavy wood furniture during deep cleaning cycles.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Every room completes deep cleaning cycle every 90 days.
  • Supervisor deep clean checklist signed off with 100% pass score.
  • Air-con cooling efficiency restored to factory specs post-coil cleaning.

HK-17 Linen & Towel Washing

Objective & Scope

Process dirty hotel linens using optimized wash cycles, water temperatures, and chemical dosages to guarantee spotlessly clean, sanitized linens.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Laundry Attendants
  • Laundry Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Machine Loading:
    • Load commercial washer-extractors to 80% maximum rated drum capacity (do not overload or underload).
  2. Cycle Selection & Chemical Dosing:
    • White Bed Linen: Main Wash 60°C - Hot Water, heavy duty alkali detergent + oxygen bleach + neutralizer/sour in final rinse.
    • White Towels: Main Wash 65°C - Hot Water, heavy duty detergent + fabric softener in final rinse cycle.
    • Colored / Pool Towels: Warm Wash 40°C - Color-safe bleach detergent.
  3. Extraction Cycle:
    • Ensure high-speed spin extraction removes excess water prior to transfer to dryers.
  4. Drying Cycle:
    • Transfer towels to tumble dryers; set to High Heat (70°C) for 35–40 minutes until completely dry and fluffy.
    • Transfer bed sheets to flatwork ironer/roller while slightly damp (15% moisture retention) for crisp flatwork pressing.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

During frequent local grid power disruptions, ensure washer/dryer operations switch seamlessly to the property's backup diesel generator. Avoid starting high-amp dryer heaters simultaneously to manage generator electrical load.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Re-wash rate for stained linens below 2%.
  • 100% compliance with chemical dosing specifications.
  • Linens thoroughly dry with zero dampness before folding/storage.
HK-18 Linen Storage & Inventory

  • Central Linen Room relative humidity maintained under 55%.

HK-19 Damaged / Stained Linen Procedure

Objective & Scope

Standardize detection, chemical reclaim attempts, discard logging, and replacement of compromised hotel linens.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Laundry Attendants
  • Room Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Identification:
    • Separate damaged (torn, frayed, burnt) or heavily stained (makeup, food grease, rust, shoe polish) linens during sorting or room stripping.
  2. Stain Reclaim Bath:
    • Route stained linens to Reclaim Wash Station.
    • Soak in specialized stain remover solution (rust remover, enzyme soak, or concentrated bleach solution) for 4 to 6 hours at 50°C.
    • Re-wash through heavy-duty cycle.
  3. Condemnation / Discard Procedure:
    • If stain cannot be removed or fabric has permanent tears, present item to Housekeeping Supervisor for inspection.
    • Stamp item as "CONDEMNED" with fabric ink.
    • Record item in Discard / Condemned Linen Log book with date, item type, size, and cause.
  4. Repurposing:
    • Cut down unrepairable sheets/towels into color-coded cleaning rags for Housekeeping/Engineering use (never use guest towels as cleaning rags).

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Local iron-rich deep well water can cause reddish rust spots on white linens. Utilize water softener filtration and iron-out chemical rinses in laundry process to prevent permanent water stains.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Minimum 70% stain reclaim success rate achieved through soaking.
  • Discard log updated weekly and audited by Finance/Admin monthly.
  • Zero condemned or stained linens issued to guest rooms.
HK-20 Lost & Found Handling

Objective & Scope

Safeguard guest property left behind in guest rooms or public areas with absolute integrity, structured logging, and secure storage.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Finder (Any Staff Member)
  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Front Office Manager

Execution Procedure

  1. Discovery & Immediate Handover:
    • When an item is found, place it in clear plastic bag immediately. Bring item to Housekeeping Office within 30 minutes of discovery.
  2. Categorization:
    • Valuables: Cash, jewelry, watches, mobile phones, laptops, passports, official documents.
    • Non-Valuables: Clothing, shoes, books, sunglasses, toiletries, toys.
    • Perishables: Food, unsealed beverages, fruits (discarded after 24 hours).
  3. Logging & Storage:
    • Log details in Master Lost & Found Register: Date/Time, Found Location (Room #), Item Description, Serial #, Finder's Name.
    • Place item inside sealed L&F Bag with reference label number.
    • Secure Valuables inside Housekeeping Double-Lock Safe; store Non-Valuables in locked L&F Cabinet.
  4. Guest Claim & Dispatch:
    • Verify claimant's identity and matching item details before release.
    • Guest signs Lost & Found Release Form upon physical receipt or courier dispatch.
  5. Holding Period & Disposition:
    • Hold non-valuables for 90 days; hold valuables for 180 days. Unclaimed non-valuables are awarded to finder via supervisor authorization.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

For items left by regional tourists returning to Manila or Tacloban, coordinate secure door-to-door courier shipment (e.g., LBC) upon guest payment for freight fee.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% of found items logged within 1 hour of discovery.
  • Safe and cabinet audit conducted weekly with zero unaccounted items.
  • Lost item claimed rate tracked and maximized through proactive FO guest notification.
HK-21 Pest Observation & Reporting

Objective & Scope

Maintain pest-free hotel environment through vigilance, early detection, clear reporting, and preventive physical barriers.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • All Housekeeping Personnel
  • Pest Control Contractor
  • Maintenance Staff

Execution Procedure

  1. Vigilant Observation During Cleaning:
    • Insects (Ants, Cockroaches, Termites): Check under sinks, inside timber wardrobes, bed frames, behind mini-fridge, wall skirtings. Look for wood dust/mud tubes (termites).
    • Rodents: Check for droppings, gnaw marks in pantry cabinets, electrical wire casing damage.
    • Bed Bugs: Check mattress seams, headboard reverse side, sheet corners for small reddish-brown specks.
  2. Immediate Isolation & Reporting:
    • If active bed bug or major pest infestation found in guest room, halt cleaning, seal room, remove room from inventory (OOO status), and report to HK Supervisor instantly.
    • Fill Pest Activity Log entry noting room number, pest type, date, exact location.
  3. Preventive Sanitation:
    • Ensure all room trash cans are emptied daily, no standing food crumbs remain, and drain covers are fitted with mesh screens.
  4. Pest Control Spraying Coordination:
    • Assist certified Pest Control Provider during bi-weekly treatment runs, unlocking rooms and inspecting treatment points.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Gamay's humid, lush tropical environment presents high termite, ant, and mosquito exposure. Ensure fly screens on bathroom louvers are intact, and keep exterior room doors closed during dawn and dusk hours.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero active guest complaints regarding pests in rooms.
  • 100% of reported pest observations attended to by pest contractor within 24 hours.
  • Monthly pest control treatment audit completed and signed.
HK-22 Housekeeping Chemical Handling

Objective & Scope

Standardize safe handling, dilution, dispensing, and storage of commercial cleaning chemicals to protect staff health and surface integrity.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants
  • PA Attendants
  • Laundry Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Mandatory Requirement:
    • Always wear rubber gloves, eye protection goggles, and protective apron when diluting concentrated chemicals.
    • Wear closed anti-slip rubber boots when floor scrubbing or handling laundry chemicals.
  2. Chemical Color-Coding & Usage Standard:
    • Chemical 1 (Neutral Cleaner - Blue Label): Floor mopping & daily multi-surface wiping.
    • Chemical 2 (Bathroom Disinfectant - Yellow Label): Basin, tiles, tub, and shower enclosure.
    • Chemical 3 (Glass Cleaner - Light Blue/Clear): Mirrors, window glass, picture frames.
    • Chemical 4 (Heavy Bowl Cleaner - Red Label): Toilet bowl interior only.
    • Chemical 5 (Degreaser - Orange Label): Kitchen floors, deep clean grease surfaces.
  3. Auto-Dispenser & Dilution System:
    • Always use automated chemical dilution dispensers in chemical dispensing room to mix chemical concentrate with water. Never manually pour unmeasured concentrates into bottles.
  4. Secondary Container Labeling:
    • Every secondary spray bottle must carry official manufacturer label indicating chemical name, dilution ratio, and safety warnings.
  5. Safety Data Sheets (SDS):
    • Maintain physical SDS Binder at chemical station in both English and Waray-Waray/Filipino.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Store chemical drums on raised plastic spill pallets in well-ventilated areas to prevent chemical runoff during accidental room flooding or heavy downpours.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% compliance with PPE usage during chemical handling.
  • Zero un-labeled or mislabeled spray bottles on HK carts.
  • SDS binder complete, legible, and accessible 24/7.
HK-23 Housekeeping Equipment Maintenance

Objective & Scope

Ensure longevity, operational safety, and optimal performance of all mechanical and manual housekeeping equipment.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Housekeeping Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Maintenance Engineer

Execution Procedure

  1. Daily Post-Use Equipment Cleaning:
    • Vacuum Cleaners: Empty dust bags/canisters after shift, clean roller brush hair/lint, wipe exterior housing, coil cord loosely without twisting.
    • Floor Scrubbers / Buffers: Drain dirty water recovery tanks, rinse tank with clean water, clean pad drivers, wipe machine frame.
    • Mop Buckets & Mop Heads: Wash mop heads thoroughly with disinfectant, wring dry, hang mop heads head-up to air dry. Drain dirty bucket water.
    • Housekeeping Carts: Remove leftover trash, clean top trays, organize shelves, wipe cart wheels free of hair/string buildup.
  2. Weekly Preventive Checks:
    • Inspect power plugs for frayed wiring, check wheel castors, check vacuum suction hoses for clogs.
  3. Fault Tagging & Repair Work Order:
    • If equipment malfunctions (e.g., burning smell, damaged wire, weak suction), attach red "OUT OF SERVICE" tag, log work order with Engineering, and move to repair shop.
  4. Equipment Log:
    • Record maintenance services in Housekeeping Equipment History Log.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Coastal salt air corrodes metal components quickly. Wipe stainless steel and metal frames of HK trolleys and scrubber handles weekly with anti-corrosion light oil or silicon spray.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Zero electrical safety incidents from damaged power cords.
  • 100% of mop heads washed and hung dry at end of shift (zero sour smell).
  • Preventive equipment maintenance log updated weekly.
HK-24 Housekeeping Shift Handover

Objective & Scope

Guarantee seamless continuity of operational communication, pending tasks, room status, and guest requests between outgoing and incoming shifts.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Outgoing Housekeeping Supervisor / Attendants
  • Incoming Housekeeping Supervisor / Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Handover Timing:
    • Conduct physical shift handover 15 minutes before outgoing shift departure (e.g., 2:45 PM for Morning-to-Afternoon shift change).
  2. Logbook & Status Review:
    • Review Master Housekeeping Shift Logbook together:
    • Pending check-out rooms still dirty or under inspection.
    • Special guest requests or delayed stay-over cleanings.
    • Out-of-Order (OOO) rooms status and estimated completion time.
    • Pending maintenance work orders submitted to Engineering.
    • Lost & Found items logged during shift.
  3. Key Audit & Asset Reconciliation:
    • Audit Master Key Board; verify all issued master keys are accounted for or transferred to incoming shift supervisor with signature.
    • Count radio transceivers, handheld tablets, and specialized equipment.
  4. Physical Walkthrough (if necessary):
    • Perform joint inspection of floor pantries, rush rooms, or public area trouble spots.
  5. Handover Sign-off:
    • Both outgoing and incoming Supervisors sign Shift Handover Log.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

Incoming evening shift must double-check night storm readiness, verifying all public area windows/shutters are latched and exterior trash bins are weighted/secured against overnight coastal wind gusts.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Master keys 100% reconciled and signed during every shift change.
  • Zero dropped guest requests or uncommunicated room status delays.
  • Handover log signed by both outgoing and incoming supervisors daily.
