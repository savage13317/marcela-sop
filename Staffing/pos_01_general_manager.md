### GENERAL MANAGER / RESORT MANAGER (`POS-01`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to Owner / Proprietor | Supervises `POS-02`, `POS-05`, `POS-08`, `POS-10`, `POS-11`
- **Role Summary:** Drives commercial sales, GOP targets, operational SOP compliance, and overall resort performance under a high-efficiency lean staffing framework.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Commercial & Event Sales:** Oversee corporate/government lead conversions, contract negotiations, event proposals, and function hall bookings (`SOP-FH-01`, `SOP-FH-03`, `SOP-FH-04`).
2. **Financial & GOP Management:** Monitor monthly P&L, authorize operational expenses, review daily revenue reports, and control departmental labor/operating costs (`SOP-AD-09`, `SOP-AD-10`).
3. **SOP Auditing & Operational Governance:** Conduct daily walk-through inspections across Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B, Facilities, and Pool areas to enforce strict SOP compliance (`SOP-FM-01`, `SOP-HK-05`, `SOP-FB-12`).
4. **Disaster Incident Command:** Act as Incident Commander during typhoon alerts, severe weather, power grid failures, and property emergencies (`SOP-SS-14`, `SOP-SS-15`, `SOP-SS-21`).
5. **Staffing & HR Supervision:** Oversee recruitment, performance evaluations, disciplinary actions, and staff scheduling across core and flexible on-call rosters (`SOP-HR-01`, `SOP-HR-06`, `SOP-HR-11`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **High-Level Guest Recovery:** Intervene personally during complex or escalated guest complaints to secure service recovery (`SOP-FO-11`).
2. **Peak Event & Surge Support:** Support function hall registration, VIP reception, or front desk check-in surges during major group arrivals (`SOP-FH-09`, `SOP-FO-20`).
3. **Supplier & Permit Management:** Coordinate directly with local government units (LGUs) and key vendors during contract renewals or license audits (`SOP-AD-04`, `SOP-AD-12`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Gross Operating Profit (GOP):** Realization of monthly and annual revenue/profit targets.
- **Guest Satisfaction Rating:** $\ge 90\%$ positive feedback across online and on-site reviews.
- **Labor & Operational Cost Ratio:** Payroll and departmental costs maintained within budget allocations.
- **Audit & Compliance Pass Rate:** $\ge 95\%$ compliance on daily SOP and safety checklists.
