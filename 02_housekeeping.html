Module 02: Housekeeping & Laundry

SOPs for room cleaning, linen handling, public area maintenance, and laundry operations

HK-01 Housekeeping Department Opening

Objective & Scope

Standardize morning operational setup to ensure smooth workflow, staff readiness, key distribution, and accurate room assignment tracking across all 30 rooms.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Housekeeping Supervisor
  • Room Attendants
  • Public Area (PA) Attendants

Execution Procedure

  1. Clock-in & Uniform Check (6:00 AM):

    • Arrive 15 minutes prior to shift start.
    • Inspect personal grooming, clean uniform, anti-slip rubber footwear, and required name badge.

  2. Key Control Issuing:

    • Receive HK master keys from Front Office desk.
    • Sign the Master Key Log with date, time, key ID, and employee signature.

  3. Daily Morning Briefing (6:15 AM):

    • Review overnight arrivals, high-priority check-outs, VIP stays, special guest requests, and daily weather outlook (e.g., wind velocity, tidal conditions, rainfall forecasts).

  4. Morning Room Status Reconciliation:

    • Retrieve current PMS Room Status Report.
    • Reconcile overnight PMS statuses against physical room statuses with the Front Office Duty Manager.

  5. Trolley & Supply Preparation:

    • Stock housekeeping carts with fresh linens, towels, guest amenities, chemical caddies, vacuum cleaners, and color-coded cleaning cloths according to daily room assignment sheets.

  6. Equipment Check:

    • Verify operational readiness of vacuums, floor scrubbers, spray bottles, emergency flashlights, and two-way radios.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

During rainy mornings or post-typhoon conditions, check for water seepage along outdoor corridors and glass panels. Ensure wet-vacs and floor blowers are plugged into generator-backed outlets if main grid brownouts occur.

HK-02 Daily Guest Room Cleaning

Objective & Scope

Establish systematic room cleaning standards to maintain clinical hygiene, aesthetic appeal, and guest comfort across occupied and vacant rooms.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Room Attendants
  • Housekeeping Supervisor

Execution Procedure

  1. Room Entry:

    • Knock on guest room door 3 times, stating "Housekeeping."
    • Wait 5 seconds between knocks. If no response, unlock door carefully and announce "Housekeeping" before stepping inside.

  2. Ventilation & Initial Inspection:

    • Open curtains and balcony sliding doors to let in natural light and fresh air.
    • Inspect for property damage, left items, or pest activity.

  3. Trash Removal:

    • Empty all trash cans (bedroom and bathroom), replace poly-liners, and remove organic food waste to avoid attracting insects.

  4. Linen Removal:

    • Strip bed sheets, pillowcases, duvet covers, and bathroom towels.
    • Place soiled linens directly into linen hamper bags on the cart; never lay dirty linen on the bare floor.

  5. Surface Dusting & Disinfection:

    • Wipe all high-touch surfaces (door handles, light switches, remote controls, phone, desk, nightstands) using microfiber cloths and multi-surface disinfectant.

  6. Floor Care:

    • Sweep and mop hard tile floors using neutral pH disinfectant cleaner.
    • Vacuum carpeted or rug areas thoroughly.

  7. Final Quality Check:

    • Restock amenities, adjust air-conditioning setpoint (22°C–24°C), lock patio doors, turn off unnecessary lights, and exit closing door securely.

Localized Context (Gamay, Northern Samar)

High tropical coastal humidity causes rapid mold/mildew buildup. Wipe wooden furniture and metallic fixtures with dry microfiber after damp cleaning. Keep sliding doors closed while AC is running to prevent interior wall condensation.
