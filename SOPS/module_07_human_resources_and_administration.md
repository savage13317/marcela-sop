## SOP-HR-01: Recruitment & Hiring

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-01
* **SOP Title:** Recruitment & Hiring
* **Department:** Human Resources / Administration
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To establish a structured, transparent, non-discriminatory, and merit-based recruitment process compliant with Philippine Labor Code standards to source, interview, and select qualified personnel for Marcela's Resort Hotel.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Department Head:** Submits Personnel Requisition Form (PRF) with justified operational headcount needs.
* **HR Officer:** Manages job postings, candidate screening, interview scheduling, and background checks.
* **General Manager (GM):** Conducts final interview for supervisory/head roles and approves all hiring offers.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Personnel Requisition:**
   * Department Head completes PRF detailing position title, job level, salary grade, and operational justification.
   * PRF requires dual approval from HR Officer and GM prior to posting.
2. **Sourcing & Job Sourcing:**
   * Post vacancies internally (notice boards) and externally (local job portals, PESO Gamay / Northern Samar, social media pages, local university job boards in Catarman/Tacloban).
   * Minimum posting window: $7\text{ calendar days}$.
3. **Screening & Initial Interview:**
   * HR Officer screens CVs against minimum position standards (SOP-HR-04).
   * Conduct initial structured interview (in-person or video call for non-local applicants) evaluating basic qualifications, communication skills, and service orientation.
4. **Technical / Practical Assessment & Department Interview:**
   * Shortlisted candidates undergo position-specific practical tests (e.g., mock table setup for F&B, bed-making drill for HK, practical cooking exam for Kitchen).
   * Department Head conducts technical interview using standardized scoring rubrics ($1 - 5\text{ scale}$).
5. **Final Interview & Background Check:**
   * GM interviews top 2 candidates.
   * HR verifies work references from prior employers (at least 2 professional references verified via phone/email).
6. **Job Offer & Appointment:**
   * HR issues formal Job Offer Letter detailing salary, employment status (probationary/contractual), benefits, start date, and reporting structure. Candidate must sign within $3\text{ business days}$.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Community First Hiring:** Priority sourcing is given to qualified residents of Gamay and neighboring municipalities in Northern Samar to foster local employment and community integration.
* **DOLE Compliance:** All recruitment activities must strictly comply with Republic Act No. 10911 (Anti-Age Discrimination in Employment Act) and DOLE regulations prohibiting unauthorized recruitment fees.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] PRF fully signed before job vacancy announcement.
* [ ] Minimum 2 professional background checks completed and documented in candidate file.
* [ ] Practical competency test conducted for all technical operational positions.
* **Target Metric:** Vacancy fill time $< 30\text{ calendar days}$; zero DOLE non-compliance findings.

## SOP-HR-02: Employee Pre-employment Requirements

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-02
* **SOP Title:** Employee Pre-employment Requirements
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To ensure all newly hired personnel complete mandatory Philippine statutory registrations, background verifications, and medical fitness assessments prior to their first official working shift.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **New Employee:** Submits all required legal, medical, and statutory documents within the stipulated onboarding timeframe.
* **HR Officer:** Validates authenticity of documents, creates physical/digital 201 Files, and registers employee in statutory portals.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Issuance of Checklist:**
   * Upon signing the Job Offer, HR hands the candidate the **Pre-employment Requirements Checklist**.
2. **Mandatory Document Submission:**
   * **Statutory Identification Numbers:**
     * SSS (Social Security System) Number
     * PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)
     * Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) MID Number
     * BIR Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) / Form 1902 or 1905
   * **Clearances & Certifications:**
     * Valid NBI Clearance (Original)
     * Barangay Clearance from resident barangay in Samar
     * PSA Birth Certificate (Copy) & Marriage Certificate (if applicable)
     * Academic Records (Transcript of Records / Diploma / TESDA NC II Certificate)
     * Certificate of Employment (COE) from previous employers
3. **Medical & Health Clearances:**
   * Pre-employment Medical Examination (PEME) from an accredited clinic/hospital (CBC, Chest X-Ray, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Drug Test).
   * **Sanitary Permit / Health Card** issued by the Gamay Rural Health Unit (RHU), mandatory for all F&B, Kitchen, and Housekeeping personnel.
4. **201 File Creation:**
   * HR verifies submitted documents against originals, stamps "COPY VERIFIED WITH ORIGINAL," and files them in the employee's physical 201 File and digital HRIS directory.
5. **Statutory Enrollment:**
   * HR registers employee in SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR online portals prior to first payroll run.

### 4. Localized Context
* **RHU Gamay Health Card:** F&B and Kitchen staff must obtain their official Health Card from the Gamay Municipal Health Office prior to handling food or guestware.
* **Travel Lead Time:** Applicants from outlying Samar island barangays are granted a grace period of up to $10\text{ business days}$ post-hire to submit final NBI clearances, provided local Police Clearance is presented immediately.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] $100\%$ completion of statutory registration numbers (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN) prior to first salary payment.
* [ ] Active RHU Health Card filed for $100\%$ of F&B, Kitchen, and Housekeeping personnel.
* [ ] Physical and digital 201 file verified and locked before Day 1 shift.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ compliance with pre-employment documentation standards; zero statutory penalty fines.

## SOP-HR-05: Employee Attendance & Timekeeping

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-05
* **SOP Title:** Employee Attendance & Timekeeping
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To establish accurate, fraud-proof timekeeping standards using biometric registration, manual backup logs, and strict attendance monitoring to ensure fair payroll processing and operational coverage.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **All Staff:** Clock in and clock out accurately using biometric scanners for all shifts and meal breaks.
* **HR Officer / Timekeeper:** Audits daily biometric logs, verifies discrepancies, and prepares payroll timecards.
* **Department Head:** Approves shift logs, grace period exemptions, and official business trips.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Clocking In/Out Mandate:**
   * Staff must clock in at the employee entrance biometric terminal in full uniform no earlier than $15\text{ minutes}$ prior to scheduled shift start.
   * Clock out required for: Lunch/Meal break start, Lunch/Meal break end, and Shift end.
   * **Biometric Failure Protocol:** If fingerprint scanner fails due to wet hands or technical outage, staff must sign the **Manual Time Register** co-signed by the Duty Supervisor within $1\text{ hour}$.
2. **Grace Period & Tardiness Definitions:**
   * Scheduled Shift Start = $T_0$.
   * $T_0 + 1\text{ min to } 5\text{ mins}$: On-time / Grace window (not penalized, but tracked).
   * $T_0 + 6\text{ mins to } 15\text{ mins}$: LATE (Deducted per minute from salary).
   * $T_0 > 15\text{ mins}$: SEVERE LATE (Deducted + subject to verbal/written warning upon repeat).
   * $T_0 > 2\text{ hours}$ without notice: UNEXCUSED ABSENCE / HALF-DAY ABSENT.
3. **Absence Notification Protocol:**
   * Employee must notify Supervisor via direct call/SMS at least $3\text{ hours}$ before shift start if unable to report due to sudden illness or emergency.
4. **Timecard Audit & Reconciliation:**
   * HR pulls biometric data weekly, cross-checks against approved shift schedules, and routes **Weekly Time Verification Sheets** to Department Heads for approval.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Power & Internet Resiliency:** During Samar grid outages, the biometric clock runs on internal battery backup. If completely discharged, the manual hardcopy logbook managed by Security at the staff entrance serves as official legal timekeeping.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Biometric data synchronized weekly with payroll system.
* [ ] $100\%$ of manual time log entries co-signed by Department Head within $24\text{ hours}$.
* [ ] Tardiness reports generated and reviewed weekly.
* **Target Metric:** Timecard error rate $< 0.5\%$ per payroll cycle.

## SOP-HR-06: Shift Scheduling

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-06
* **SOP Title:** Shift Scheduling
* **Department:** Human Resources & Operations
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To regulate employee work shift creation, rest day allocation, rotational scheduling, and schedule adjustments in full compliance with the 8-hour Labor Code mandate and resort operational demands.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Department Head:** Drafts weekly shift rosters based on resort occupancy and function hall bookings.
* **HR Officer:** Reviews rosters for Labor Code compliance (rest days, maximum hours) before publication.
* **Employee:** Adheres to published schedules and submits swap requests in advance.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Shift Schedule Drafting Guidelines:**
   * Standard work shift: $8\text{ working hours} + 1\text{ hour unpaid meal break} = 9\text{ total elapsed hours}$.
   * Every employee is entitled to **1 mandatory 24-hour continuous rest day** for every $6\text{ consecutive working days}$.
   * Minimum $11\text{ hours}$ rest time required between consecutive shifts ("turnaround time").
2. **Roster Publication Timeline:**
   * Department Heads draft weekly rosters every Wednesday by 12:00 PM.
   * HR reviews and GM approves roster by Thursday 03:00 PM.
   * Final weekly schedule posted on employee bulletin boards and digital communication channels by **Thursday 05:00 PM** for the upcoming work week (Monday to Sunday).
3. **Shift Swap / Change Requests:**
   * Shift swaps between employees in the same position require a completed **Shift Swap Request Form**.
   * Submitted at least $24\text{ hours}$ in advance; must be approved in writing by Department Head.
   * Unapproved informal shift swapping is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Typhoon Schedule Flexibility:** When PAGASA declares TCWS #2 or higher for Northern Samar, Department Heads may reconfigure shifts into 12-hour disaster readiness rotations (with proper OT authorization) to keep essential staff on-site before road conditions deteriorate.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Shift schedule posted every Thursday by 05:00 PM without fail.
* [ ] $100\%$ compliance with 24-hour weekly rest day rule per employee.
* [ ] All shift swaps supported by signed request forms in HR files.
* **Target Metric:** Zero Labor Code rest day violations; schedule publication timeliness $100\%$.

## SOP-HR-07: Overtime & Rest Day Work

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-07
* **SOP Title:** Overtime & Rest Day Work
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To govern the authorization, computation, and recording of Overtime (OT) work, Rest Day work, and Holiday premiums in strict accordance with the Philippine Labor Code.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Department Head:** Evaluates operational necessity and pre-approves OT requests.
* **HR / Payroll Officer:** Computes OT pay multipliers based on verified biometric timecards and approved OT slips.
* **General Manager:** Grants final approval for high-volume or emergency OT authorization.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Prior Authorization Requirement:**
   * Overtime work is **strictly pre-authorized**. Performing work beyond 8 hours without an approved **Overtime Authorization Form (OTAF)** will not be compensated as OT.
   * Department Head completes OTAF specifying employee name, reason (e.g., late event cleanup, emergency repair, typhoon setup), and estimated OT hours.
2. **Filing & Biometric Verification:**
   * Approved OTAF submitted to HR prior to or within $12\text{ hours}$ post-shift (for unforeseen operational emergencies).
   * HR cross-checks biometric timecard against approved OTAF hours. Pay is calculated on actual clocked OT up to approved limit.
3. **Statutory Overtime Pay Multiplier Rates:**

| Work Category | Statutory Pay Rate Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| **Regular Workday Overtime** | Base hourly rate $\times 125\%$ ($1.25$) for work beyond 8 hours |
| **Scheduled Rest Day Work (First 8 Hours)** | Base daily rate $\times 130\%$ ($1.30$) |
| **Scheduled Rest Day Overtime** | Hourly rest day rate $\times 130\%$ ($1.69$ base hourly rate) |
| **Regular Holiday Work (First 8 Hours)** | Base daily rate $\times 200\%$ ($2.00$) |
| **Regular Holiday Overtime** | Hourly regular holiday rate $\times 130\%$ ($2.60$ base hourly rate) |
| **Special Non-Working Day Work (First 8 Hours)** | Base daily rate $\times 130\%$ ($1.30$) |
| **Special Non-Working Day Overtime** | Hourly special day rate $\times 130\%$ ($1.69$ base hourly rate) |
| **Night Shift Differential (10:00 PM - 06:00 AM)** | Applicable hourly rate $\times 110\%$ ($1.10$) |

### 4. Localized Context
* **Disaster Emergency OT Exemption:** Under Article 89 of the Labor Code (Emergency Overtime), management may require employees to perform OT work during typhoons, floods, or structural emergencies in Gamay to protect guest lives and property. Statutory emergency OT rates apply.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] $100\%$ of processed OT supported by pre-approved OTAF slips.
* [ ] Overtime computations verified against DOLE multiplier tables every payroll.
* [ ] Night shift differential applied accurately for shifts spanning 10:00 PM to 06:00 AM.
* **Target Metric:** Zero unauthorized overtime cost variance; $100\%$ payroll calculation accuracy.

## SOP-HR-08: Leave Application

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-08
* **SOP Title:** Leave Application
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To standardize the application, approval, tracking, and liquidation of statutory and company-provided leave entitlements for all regular and eligible employees.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Employee:** Submits Leave Application Form within required lead time.
* **Department Head:** Recommends approval/disapproval based on operational manpower coverage.
* **HR Officer:** Verifies leave balance credits, approves filing, and logs usage in HRIS.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Leave Entitlements Overview:**
   * **Service Incentive Leave (SIL):** $5\text{ days}$ paid annual leave for regularized employees after 1 year of service (DOLE statutory).
   * **Maternity Leave (RA 11210):** $105\text{ days}$ paid leave for female employees ($115\text{ days}$ for solo parents), plus optional $30\text{ days}$ unpaid.
   * **Paternity Leave (RA 8187):** $7\text{ days}$ paid leave for married male employees for first 4 deliveries of legitimate spouse.
   * **Solo Parent Leave (RA 8972 / RA 11861):** $7\text{ days}$ paid flexible leave per year for qualified solo parents.
   * **VAWC Leave (RA 9262):** Up to $10\text{ days}$ paid leave for female employees victimized by violence.
   * **Special Leave for Women (RA 9710 Magna Carta):** Up to $2\text{ months}$ paid leave following gynecological surgery.
2. **Application Lead Times & Workflow:**
   * **Planned Leave (Vacation / SIL):** Application submitted at least $7\text{ calendar days}$ prior to intended start date using **Leave Application Form**.
   * **Sick Leave:** Employee notifies supervisor within $2\text{ hours}$ of shift start. Upon return, employee submits Leave Form attached with a **Medical Certificate** from a licensed physician (mandatory for absences $\ge 2\text{ consecutive days}$).
3. **Approval & HR Logging:**
   * Department Head reviews coverage, signs recommendation, forwards to HR.
   * HR verifies credit availability, approves, updates leave ledger, and informs employee.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Calamity / Severe Weather Emergency Leave:** If an employee's residence in Northern Samar is directly devastated by a typhoon or severe flooding, GM may approve up to $3\text{ days}$ emergency calamity leave upon verification by HR and local Barangay certification.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Leave balance ledgers updated within $24\text{ hours}$ of leave execution.
* [ ] Valid medical certificates attached for sick leaves exceeding 1 day.
* [ ] Statutory leaves (Maternity, Paternity, Solo Parent) properly reported to SSS/DOLE portals.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ accuracy in leave credit ledger records.

## SOP-HR-10: Employee Conduct & Discipline

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-10
* **SOP Title:** Employee Conduct & Discipline
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To enforce fair, constructive, progressive, and legally sound disciplinary procedures adhering strictly to Philippine Labor Code **Twin Notice Rule** due process for offenses detailed in the Resort Employee Code of Conduct.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Supervisor / Department Head:** Documents infractions, issues incident reports, enforces workplace rules.
* **HR Officer:** Issues formal notices, leads administrative hearings, ensures due process compliance.
* **General Manager:** Reviews panel findings and approves major disciplinary actions (suspension, termination).

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Progressive Discipline Framework:**
   * **Class A (Minor Offenses):** Verbal Warning $\rightarrow$ Written Reprimand $\rightarrow$ 1-Day Suspension. (e.g., Minor tardiness, uniform non-compliance).
   * **Class B (Moderate Offenses):** Written Reprimand $\rightarrow$ 3-Day Suspension $\rightarrow$ 5-Day Suspension. (e.g., Unexcused absence, unauthorized shift swap, rudeness to colleague).
   * **Class C (Major / Severe Offenses):** 7 to 15-Day Suspension or Termination on First Offense depending on gravity. (e.g., Theft, physical fight, drunkenness on duty, sexual harassment, gross guest neglect, drug usage).
2. **Due Process Procedure (Twin Notice Rule):**
   * **Incident Report (IR):** Supervisor files IR within $24\text{ hours}$ of offense discovery.
   * **First Notice: Notice to Explain (NTE):**
     * HR issues written NTE to employee detailing specific offense, date, code violation, and relevant evidence.
     * Employee is given **at least 5 calendar days** ($120\text{ hours}$) to submit written explanation.
   * **Preventive Suspension (Optional):** Applied only if employee presence poses an imminent threat to life, property, or investigation integrity (maximum $30\text{ calendar days}$ unpaid).
   * **Administrative Hearing:**
     * Convened for major offenses. Panel consists of HR, GM, and Department Head. Employee may be accompanied by a colleague or legal counsel.
   * **Second Notice: Notice of Decision (NOD):**
     * Issued within $10\text{ business days}$ post-investigation/hearing stating management’s final decision (Exoneration, Warning, Suspension, or Termination with Just Cause per Article 297 of Labor Code).

### 4. Localized Context
* **Substance Abuse & Alcohol Policy:** Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs is strictly prohibited. Given the resort setting, random alcohol breathalyzer and drug testing are conducted in compliance with DOLE Department Order 53-03.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] $100\%$ adherence to 5-day response window for all NTEs issued.
* [ ] Complete documentation attached to 201 File for every disciplinary action.
* [ ] Twin Notice Rule strictly fulfilled prior to enforcing suspensions or terminations.
* **Target Metric:** Zero DOLE illegal dismissal cases or lost labor arbitrations.

## SOP-HR-11: Performance Evaluation

* **SOP Code:** SOP-HR-11
* **SOP Title:** Performance Evaluation
* **Department:** Human Resources
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To provide a objective, transparent evaluation system assessing employee job performance during probationary periods ($3\text{rd}$ and $5\text{th}$ month review) and annual reviews for regularized employees to drive continuous professional growth.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Department Head:** Conducts objective performance evaluation ratings and face-to-face feedback sessions.
* **HR Officer:** Schedules review cycles, audits rating consistency, and manages performance records.
* **General Manager:** Approves regularization recommendations, merit increases, and promotion proposals.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Evaluation Timelines & Cycles:**
   * **Probationary Evaluation:** Conducted at the end of the $3\text{rd}$ Month (Mid-probationary feedback) and $5\text{th}$ Month (Regularization decision).
   * **Annual Performance Appraisal:** Conducted every October/November for all regular employees.
2. **Evaluation Criteria & Grading Scale:**
   * Evaluated across 5 Core Pillars: Job Competency & Accuracy ($25\%$), Attendance & Punctuality ($20\%$), Guest Service & Interpersonal Skills ($25\%$), Teamwork & Initiative ($15\%$), Safety & SOP Compliance ($15\%$).
   * Rating Scale:
     * **5 = Outstanding** (Consistently exceeds standards)
     * **4 = Exceeds Expectations** (Frequently above standards)
     * **3 = Meets Expectations** (Satisfactory / Standard baseline)
     * **2 = Needs Improvement** (Below standard; requires Performance Improvement Plan)
     * **1 = Unsatisfactory** (Unacceptable performance)
3. **Evaluation Process Workflow:**
   * HR dispatches **Performance Appraisal Forms** 2 weeks prior to evaluation due date.
   * Supervisor completes rating score, provides specific written justifications for ratings of $1, 2, \text{or } 5$.
   * **Face-to-Face Evaluation Interview:** Supervisor meets privately with employee to discuss scores, celebrate strengths, address development areas, and establish mutual goals.
   * Employee signs form (signature acknowledges receipt/discussion, not necessarily complete agreement). Employee may attach written comments.
4. **Regularization / Action Outcomes:**
   * Score $\ge 3.0$ at 5th month = Recommendation for **Regularization**.

   * Maximum single disbursement limit per voucher: **$\text{PHP } 2,000.00$**. Expenses exceeding $\text{PHP } 2,000$ must go through standard PO / Accounts Payable check processing.
2. **Disbursement Workflow:**
   * Requisitioner submits **Petty Cash Voucher (PCV)** signed by Department Head detailing exact operational purpose.
   * Custodian verifies request feasibility, issues cash advance, and records entry in PCV Ledger.
3. **Liquidation Requirement:**
   * Expense liquidated within **24 hours** (or next business day).
   * Requisitioner submits PCV attached with original BIR Official Receipts (OR), Sales Invoices, or signed Jeepney/Tricycle fare receipts, plus change.
   * Custodian reviews receipt validity, stamps "PAID & LIQUIDATED," and attaches receipts to voucher.
4. **Replenishment Procedure:**
   * Replenishment requested when cash balance drops to **$30\%$ ($\text{PHP } 4,500.00$)**.
   * Custodian compiles Petty Cash Summary Sheet attached with all liquidated vouchers/receipts.
   * Accounting audits summary, issues replenishment check payable strictly to "Petty Cash Custodian" to restore fund to $\text{PHP } 15,000.00$.
5. **Surprise Cash Counts:**
   * Finance Lead conducts unannounced monthly surprise physical cash counts. Any unverified cash shortage must be reimbursed immediately by Custodian and investigated.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Local Transportation & Informal Receipts:** For local market purchasing or municipal trips in Gamay where formal BIR ORs are unavailable from local tricycle drivers, a signed **Reimbursement Expense Receipt (RER)** co-signed by the Department Head is accepted up to a maximum limit of $\text{PHP } 300.00$.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Petty cash box locked inside main executive office vault 24/7.
* [ ] $100\%$ of disbursements supported by pre-approved PCVs and original ORs/SIs.
* [ ] Monthly surprise cash counts logged with zero unaccounted variances.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ compliance with 24-hour liquidation policy; zero unverified cash shortages.

## SOP-AD-08: Accounts Payable Processing

* **SOP Code:** SOP-AD-08
* **SOP Title:** Accounts Payable Processing
* **Department:** Administration & Accounting
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To establish a systematic workflow for matching, auditing, approving, scheduling, and paying vendor invoices, utility bills, and contractual obligations while maximizing cash flow and capturing early payment discounts.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Accounts Payable (AP) Clerk:** Receives invoices, performs 3-way matching, prepares Disbursement Vouchers.
* **Finance Lead / Accountant:** Audits voucher packages, schedules payment runs, verifies tax withholding.
* **General Manager / Authorized Signatories:** Approves disbursement vouchers and signs payment checks / bank transfers.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Invoice Receipt & Log:**
   * All incoming vendor invoices, utility bills (NORSAMELCO power, water, telecom), and service bills logged immediately in the **AP Tracking Register**.
2. **Three-Way Matching Verification:**
   * AP Clerk performs mandatory 3-way match across three documents:
     1. Approved **Purchase Order (PO)**.
     2. Signed **Receiving Report (RR)** / Delivery Receipt.
     3. Vendor **Sales Invoice (SI)** / Official Receipt (OR).
   * Verify unit prices, extensions, tax computations, and terms match $100\%$. Discrepancies flagged to Purchasing Officer immediately.
3. **Disbursement Voucher (DV) Preparation & Tax Withholding:**
   * AP Clerk prepares DV, computing applicable Creditable Withholding Tax (EWT - BIR Form 2307) based on supplier classification ($1\%$ for goods, $2\%$ for services).
4. **Payment Schedule & Approval:**
   * Standard payment terms: **Net 30 days** from invoice date (or per contract).
   * Payment releases processed bi-weekly (15th and 30th).
   * DV package (PO + RR + Invoice + BIR 2307 + DV) routed for approval per authorization matrix.
5. **Payment Release & OR Collection:**
   * Check or Electronic Fund Transfer (EFT) processed. Vendor surrenders official BIR Official Receipt (OR) upon check collection or bank transfer confirmation.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Critical Local Utility Payment Dates:** NORSAMELCO power and municipal water bills must be processed with high priority prior to due dates to avoid disconnection, as municipal service restoration in Gamay can take up to $48\text{ hours}$.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] 3-way matching verified and attached for $100\%$ of trade vendor payments.
* [ ] BIR Form 2307 certificates issued to vendors alongside tax-withheld payments.
* [ ] Zero late penalty charges incurred on utility bills.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ on-time payment processing; zero duplicate vendor disbursements.

To govern property asset tagging, inventory recording, preventive maintenance tracking, asset transfers, semi-annual physical audits, and retirement/disposal of hotel capital assets.

* **Property Asset Officer / Admin:** Tags assets, maintains Fixed Asset Register (FAR), records transfers.

* **Internal Auditor / Finance Lead:** Conducts semi-annual physical asset counts and depreciation audits.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

   * Every physical asset with a unit value $\ge \text{PHP } 5,000.00$ or useful life $> 1\text{ year}$ (e.g., TVs, AC units, beds, kitchen equipment, generators, computers) must be tagged upon receiving.
