Typhoon Readiness & Stay-In Contingency

SOP Module 06 Special Response — Mandated for Gamay, Northern Samar typhoon paths.

Signal #2 Automatic Activation: Mandatory transition of core team to stay-in emergency status with hazard pay provisions.

1. Incident Commander

GM / Operations Supervisor

Overall disaster command, guest safety coordination, LGU/PDRRMO liaison (SOP-SS-14).

2. Engineering & Utilities

Maintenance & Pool Tech

Backup generator fuel checks, solar system isolation, window shutter boarding (SOP-FM-08).

3. Housekeeping Lead

Room Attendant / Utility Lead

Water ingress control, emergency towel distribution, guest room safety checks (SOP-SS-19).

4. Guest Communications

Night Auditor / Guest Ops

24/7 front desk hotline, satellite phone/radio monitoring, guest headcount (SOP-SS-22).
