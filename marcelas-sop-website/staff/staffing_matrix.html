Staffing Matrix

Optimized Core Regular Workforce + Flexible On-Call Roster

Executive Summary

Following a comprehensive audit against the Master Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Manual (147 SOPs across 8 Modules), the baseline core staffing model has been adjusted from 8 to 11 Regular Core Employees.

11 Regular Core Employees keep the resort operating safely, legally, and 24/7. The flexible on-call roster expands operational capacity during high weekend occupancy and event surges.

Workforce Category Headcount Operational Purpose & Coverage
Core Regular Employees 11 Guarantees 24/7 front desk/night audit, baseline housekeeping, continuous maintenance, daily kitchen prep/sanitation, and administrative control.
On-Call / Flexible Pool 16–20 Pre-screened, trained roster activated strictly for peak weekend occupancy, weddings, corporate events, and surge periods.
Contracted / Outsourced Variable Licensed security agency, IT/network retainer, and specialized event AV contractors.

Gap Resolution Analysis

The addition of 3 regular core roles directly addresses high-risk operational gaps:

  • Night Coverage & Audit: Night Auditor (Staff #4) ensures proper night audit daily revenue reconciliation, late check-ins, overnight emergency coordination, and internal financial controls.
  • Housekeeping Capacity: 3-person HK team (Lead + 2 Attendants) provides 18 working shifts per week, comfortably handling daily room turnovers, public areas, in-house linen washing, and staggered rest days.
  • Kitchen Safety & Efficiency: Assistant Cook / Steward (Staff #9) handles dishwashing, receiving, and sanitation. During low-demand weekdays, this role doubles as the F&B server for walk-in/hotel breakfast guests.
  • Pool Safety Protocol: Clear operational rule: When certified on-call lifeguards are off-duty, the Maintenance & Pool Technician performs chemical checks with strict safety signage enforcement.
