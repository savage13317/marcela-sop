HR-01 Recruitment & Hiring

Objective & Scope

To establish a structured, transparent, non-discriminatory, and merit-based recruitment process compliant with Philippine Labor Code standards to source, interview, and select qualified personnel for Marcela's Resort Hotel.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Department Head: Submits Personnel Requisition Form (PRF) with justified operational headcount needs.
  • HR Officer: Manages job postings, candidate screening, interview scheduling, and background checks.
  • General Manager (GM): Conducts final interview for supervisory/head roles and approves all hiring offers.

Execution Procedure

  1. Personnel Requisition:
    • Department Head completes PRF detailing position title, job level, salary grade, and operational justification.
    • PRF requires dual approval from HR Officer and GM prior to posting.
  2. Sourcing & Job Posting:
    • Post vacancies internally (notice boards) and externally (local job portals, PESO Gamay / Northern Samar, social media pages, local university job boards in Catarman/Tacloban).
    • Minimum posting window: 7 calendar days.
  3. Screening & Initial Interview:
    • HR Officer screens CVs against minimum position standards (SOP-HR-04).
    • Conduct initial structured interview (in-person or video call for non-local applicants) evaluating basic qualifications, communication skills, and service orientation.
  4. Technical / Practical Assessment & Department Interview:
    • Shortlisted candidates undergo position-specific practical tests (e.g., mock table setup for F&B, bed-making drill for HK, practical cooking exam for Kitchen).
    • Department Head conducts technical interview using standardized scoring rubrics (1–5 scale).
  5. Final Interview & Background Check:
    • GM interviews top 2 candidates.
    • HR verifies work references from prior employers (at least 2 professional references verified via phone/email).
  6. Job Offer & Appointment:
    • HR issues formal Job Offer Letter detailing salary, employment status (probationary/contractual), benefits, start date, and reporting structure. Candidate must sign within 3 business days.

Localized Context

Community First Hiring: Priority sourcing is given to qualified residents of Gamay and neighboring municipalities in Northern Samar to foster local employment and community integration. All recruitment activities must strictly comply with RA 10911 (Anti-Age Discrimination in Employment Act) and DOLE regulations prohibiting unauthorized recruitment fees.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • PRF fully signed before job vacancy announcement.
  • Minimum 2 professional background checks completed and documented in candidate file.
  • Practical competency test conducted for all technical operational positions.
  • Target Metric: Vacancy fill time < 30 calendar days; zero DOLE non-compliance findings.
HR-02 Employee Pre-employment Requirements

Objective & Scope

To ensure all newly hired personnel complete mandatory Philippine statutory registrations, background verifications, and medical fitness assessments prior to their first official working shift.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • New Employee: Submits all required legal, medical, and statutory documents within the stipulated onboarding timeframe.
  • HR Officer: Validates authenticity of documents, creates physical/digital 201 Files, and registers employee in statutory portals.

Execution Procedure

  1. Issuance of Checklist:
    • Upon signing the Job Offer, HR hands the candidate the Pre-employment Requirements Checklist.
  2. Mandatory Document Submission:
    • Statutory ID Numbers: SSS Number, PhilHealth PIN, Pag-IBIG Fund MID Number, BIR TIN / Form 1902 or 1905.
    • Clearances & Certifications: Valid NBI Clearance (Original), Barangay Clearance from resident barangay in Samar, PSA Birth Certificate & Marriage Certificate (if applicable), Academic Records (TOR / Diploma / TESDA NC II Certificate), Certificate of Employment from previous employers.
  3. Medical & Health Clearances:
    • Pre-employment Medical Examination (PEME) from an accredited clinic/hospital (CBC, Chest X-Ray, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Drug Test).
    • Sanitary Permit / Health Card issued by the Gamay Rural Health Unit (RHU) — mandatory for all F&B, Kitchen, and Housekeeping personnel.
  4. 201 File Creation:
    • HR verifies submitted documents against originals, stamps "COPY VERIFIED WITH ORIGINAL," and files them in the employee's physical 201 File and digital HRIS directory.
  5. Statutory Enrollment:
    • HR registers employee in SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR online portals prior to first payroll run.

Localized Context

RHU Gamay Health Card: F&B and Kitchen staff must obtain their official Health Card from the Gamay Municipal Health Office prior to handling food or guestware. Applicants from outlying Samar island barangays are granted a grace period of up to 10 business days post-hire to submit final NBI clearances, provided local Police Clearance is presented immediately.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% completion of statutory registration numbers (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, TIN) prior to first salary payment.
  • Active RHU Health Card filed for 100% of F&B, Kitchen, and Housekeeping personnel.
  • Physical and digital 201 file verified and locked before Day 1 shift.
  • Target Metric: 100% compliance with pre-employment documentation standards; zero statutory penalty fines.

HR-05 Employee Attendance & Timekeeping

Objective & Scope

To establish accurate, fraud-proof timekeeping standards using biometric registration, manual backup logs, and strict attendance monitoring to ensure fair payroll processing and operational coverage.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • All Staff: Clock in and clock out accurately using biometric scanners for all shifts and meal breaks.
  • HR Officer / Timekeeper: Audits daily biometric logs, verifies discrepancies, and prepares payroll timecards.
  • Department Head: Approves shift logs, grace period exemptions, and official business trips.

Execution Procedure

  1. Clocking In/Out Mandate:
    • Staff must clock in at the employee entrance biometric terminal in full uniform no earlier than 15 minutes prior to scheduled shift start.
    • Clock out required for: Lunch/Meal break start, Lunch/Meal break end, and Shift end.
    • Biometric Failure Protocol: If fingerprint scanner fails due to wet hands or technical outage, staff must sign the Manual Time Register co-signed by the Duty Supervisor within 1 hour.
  2. Grace Period & Tardiness Definitions:
    • T₀ + 1 to 5 mins: On-time / Grace window (not penalized, but tracked).
    • T₀ + 6 to 15 mins: LATE (deducted per minute from salary).
    • T₀ > 15 mins: SEVERE LATE (deducted + subject to verbal/written warning upon repeat).
    • T₀ > 2 hours without notice: UNEXCUSED ABSENCE / HALF-DAY ABSENT.
  3. Absence Notification Protocol: Employee must notify Supervisor via direct call/SMS at least 3 hours before shift start if unable to report due to sudden illness or emergency.
  4. Timecard Audit & Reconciliation: HR pulls biometric data weekly, cross-checks against approved shift schedules, and routes Weekly Time Verification Sheets to Department Heads for approval.

Localized Context

During Samar grid outages, the biometric clock runs on internal battery backup. If completely discharged, the manual hardcopy logbook managed by Security at the staff entrance serves as official legal timekeeping.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Biometric data synchronized weekly with payroll system.
  • 100% of manual time log entries co-signed by Department Head within 24 hours.
  • Tardiness reports generated and reviewed weekly.
  • Target Metric: Timecard error rate < 0.5% per payroll cycle.
HR-06 Shift Scheduling

Objective & Scope

To regulate employee work shift creation, rest day allocation, rotational scheduling, and schedule adjustments in full compliance with the 8-hour Labor Code mandate and resort operational demands.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Department Head: Drafts weekly shift rosters based on resort occupancy and function hall bookings.
  • HR Officer: Reviews rosters for Labor Code compliance (rest days, maximum hours) before publication.
  • Employee: Adheres to published schedules and submits swap requests in advance.

Execution Procedure

  1. Shift Schedule Drafting Guidelines:
    • Standard work shift: 8 working hours + 1 hour unpaid meal break = 9 total elapsed hours.
    • Every employee is entitled to 1 mandatory 24-hour continuous rest day for every 6 consecutive working days.
    • Minimum 11 hours rest time required between consecutive shifts ("turnaround time").
  2. Roster Publication Timeline:
    • Department Heads draft weekly rosters every Wednesday by 12:00 PM.
    • HR reviews and GM approves roster by Thursday 03:00 PM.
    • Final weekly schedule posted on employee bulletin boards and digital communication channels by Thursday 05:00 PM for the upcoming work week (Monday to Sunday).
  3. Shift Swap / Change Requests:
    • Shift swaps between employees in the same position require a completed Shift Swap Request Form.
    • Submitted at least 24 hours in advance; must be approved in writing by Department Head.
    • Unapproved informal shift swapping is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action.

Localized Context

When PAGASA declares TCWS #2 or higher for Northern Samar, Department Heads may reconfigure shifts into 12-hour disaster readiness rotations (with proper OT authorization) to keep essential staff on-site before road conditions deteriorate.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Shift schedule posted every Thursday by 05:00 PM without fail.
  • 100% compliance with 24-hour weekly rest day rule per employee.
  • All shift swaps supported by signed request forms in HR files.
  • Target Metric: Zero Labor Code rest day violations; schedule publication timeliness 100%.

  2. Filing & Biometric Verification: Approved OTAF submitted to HR prior to or within 12 hours post-shift (for unforeseen operational emergencies). HR cross-checks biometric timecard against approved OTAF hours.
  3. Statutory Overtime Pay Multiplier Rates:

    Regular Holiday Work (First 8 Hours)Base daily rate × 200% (2.00)
    Regular Holiday OvertimeHourly regular holiday rate × 130% (2.60 base hourly)
    Special Non-Working Day WorkBase daily rate × 130% (1.30)
    Special Non-Working Day OTHourly special day rate × 130% (1.69 base hourly)

Under Article 89 of the Labor Code (Emergency Overtime), management may require employees to perform OT work during typhoons, floods, or structural emergencies in Gamay to protect guest lives and property. Statutory emergency OT rates apply.

HR-08 Leave Application

Objective & Scope

To standardize the application, approval, tracking, and liquidation of statutory and company-provided leave entitlements for all regular and eligible employees.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Employee: Submits Leave Application Form within required lead time.
  • Department Head: Recommends approval/disapproval based on operational manpower coverage.
  • HR Officer: Verifies leave balance credits, approves filing, and logs usage in HRIS.

Execution Procedure

  1. Leave Entitlements Overview:
    • Service Incentive Leave (SIL): 5 days paid annual leave for regularized employees after 1 year of service (DOLE statutory).
    • Maternity Leave (RA 11210): 105 days paid leave (115 days for solo parents), plus optional 30 days unpaid.
    • Paternity Leave (RA 8187): 7 days paid leave for married male employees for first 4 deliveries of legitimate spouse.
    • Solo Parent Leave (RA 8972 / RA 11861): 7 days paid flexible leave per year for qualified solo parents.
    • VAWC Leave (RA 9262): Up to 10 days paid leave for female employees victimized by violence.
    • Special Leave for Women (RA 9710 Magna Carta): Up to 2 months paid leave following gynecological surgery.
  2. Application Lead Times & Workflow:
    • Planned Leave (Vacation / SIL): Application submitted at least 7 calendar days prior to intended start date using Leave Application Form.
    • Sick Leave: Employee notifies supervisor within 2 hours of shift start. Upon return, employee submits Leave Form attached with Medical Certificate from a licensed physician (mandatory for absences ≥ 2 consecutive days).
  3. Approval & HR Logging: Department Head reviews coverage, signs recommendation, forwards to HR. HR verifies credit availability, approves, updates leave ledger, and informs employee.

Localized Context

If an employee's residence in Northern Samar is directly devastated by a typhoon or severe flooding, GM may approve up to 3 days emergency calamity leave upon verification by HR and local Barangay certification.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Leave balance ledgers updated within 24 hours of leave execution.
  • Valid medical certificates attached for sick leaves exceeding 1 day.
  • Statutory leaves (Maternity, Paternity, Solo Parent) properly reported to SSS/DOLE portals.
  • Target Metric: 100% accuracy in leave credit ledger records.

HR-10 Employee Conduct & Discipline

Objective & Scope

To enforce fair, constructive, progressive, and legally sound disciplinary procedures adhering strictly to Philippine Labor Code Twin Notice Rule due process for offenses detailed in the Resort Employee Code of Conduct.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Supervisor / Department Head: Documents infractions, issues incident reports, enforces workplace rules.
  • HR Officer: Issues formal notices, leads administrative hearings, ensures due process compliance.
  • General Manager: Reviews panel findings and approves major disciplinary actions (suspension, termination).

Execution Procedure

  1. Progressive Discipline Framework:
    • Class A (Minor): Verbal Warning → Written Reprimand → 1-Day Suspension (e.g., minor tardiness, uniform non-compliance).
    • Class B (Moderate): Written Reprimand → 3-Day Suspension → 5-Day Suspension (e.g., unexcused absence, unauthorized shift swap, rudeness to colleague).
    • Class C (Major/Severe): 7 to 15-Day Suspension or Termination on First Offense depending on gravity (e.g., theft, physical fight, drunkenness on duty, sexual harassment, gross guest neglect, drug usage).
  2. Due Process Procedure (Twin Notice Rule):
    • Incident Report (IR): Supervisor files IR within 24 hours of offense discovery.
    • First Notice — Notice to Explain (NTE): HR issues written NTE to employee detailing specific offense, date, code violation, and relevant evidence. Employee is given at least 5 calendar days (120 hours) to submit written explanation.
    • Preventive Suspension (Optional): Applied only if employee presence poses an imminent threat to life, property, or investigation integrity (maximum 30 calendar days unpaid).
    • Administrative Hearing: Convened for major offenses. Panel consists of HR, GM, and Department Head. Employee may be accompanied by a colleague or legal counsel.
    • Second Notice — Notice of Decision (NOD): Issued within 10 business days post-investigation/hearing stating management's final decision (Exoneration, Warning, Suspension, or Termination with Just Cause per Article 297 of Labor Code).

Localized Context

Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs is strictly prohibited. Given the resort setting, random alcohol breathalyzer and drug testing are conducted in compliance with DOLE Department Order 53-03.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 100% adherence to 5-day response window for all NTEs issued.
  • Complete documentation attached to 201 File for every disciplinary action.
  • Twin Notice Rule strictly fulfilled prior to enforcing suspensions or terminations.
  • Target Metric: Zero DOLE illegal dismissal cases or lost labor arbitrations.
HR-11 Performance Evaluation

Objective & Scope

To provide an objective, transparent evaluation system assessing employee job performance during probationary periods (3rd and 5th month review) and annual reviews for regularized employees to drive continuous professional growth.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Department Head: Conducts objective performance evaluation ratings and face-to-face feedback sessions.
  • HR Officer: Schedules review cycles, audits rating consistency, and manages performance records.
  • General Manager: Approves regularization recommendations, merit increases, and promotion proposals.

Execution Procedure

  1. Evaluation Timelines: Probationary evaluation at end of 3rd month (mid-probationary feedback) and 5th month (regularization decision). Annual Performance Appraisal every October/November for all regular employees.
  2. Evaluation Criteria & Grading Scale:
    • Evaluated across 5 Core Pillars: Job Competency & Accuracy (25%), Attendance & Punctuality (20%), Guest Service & Interpersonal Skills (25%), Teamwork & Initiative (15%), Safety & SOP Compliance (15%).
    • Rating Scale: 5 = Outstanding, 4 = Exceeds Expectations, 3 = Meets Expectations, 2 = Needs Improvement, 1 = Unsatisfactory.
  3. Evaluation Process Workflow:
    • HR dispatches Performance Appraisal Forms 2 weeks prior to evaluation due date.
    • Supervisor completes rating score, provides specific written justifications for ratings of 1, 2, or 5.
    • Face-to-Face Evaluation Interview: Supervisor meets privately with employee to discuss scores, celebrate strengths, address development areas, and establish mutual goals.
    • Employee signs form (signature acknowledges receipt/discussion). Employee may attach written comments.
  4. Regularization / Action Outcomes: Score ≥ 3.0 at 5th month = Recommendation for Regularization. Score < 3.0 at 5th month = Non-regularization / End of probationary contract with lawful written notice prior to 6th month.

Localized Context

Evaluations emphasize supportive provincial mentorship, helping staff overcome local skill gaps through internal training rather than punitive scoring.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • 5th-month probationary evaluations completed at least 30 days before the 6th-month employment mark.
  • Face-to-face evaluation interview conducted for 100% of appraised employees.
  • Signed appraisal forms archived in 201 files within 5 business days.
  • Target Metric: 100% appraisal completion rate; zero missed probationary regularization deadlines.

HR-13 Employee Grievance Procedure

Objective & Scope

To provide a neutral, confidential, fair, and accessible mechanism for employees to report workplace grievances, interpersonal conflicts, unfair treatment, or policy violations without fear of retaliation.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Employee: Raises concerns honestly following the established step-by-step escalation path.
  • HR Officer: Mediates conflict, investigates complaints confidentially, and records resolution.
  • General Manager: Serves as final internal appeal authority for unresolved grievances.

Execution Procedure

  1. Step 1 — Informal Resolution (Immediate Supervisor Level): Employee discusses issue verbally with immediate supervisor within 5 business days of incident. Supervisor conducts informal discussion to resolve concern within 3 business days. (If grievance involves the immediate supervisor, employee proceeds directly to Step 2.)
  2. Step 2 — Formal Written Grievance (HR Level): If unresolved at Step 1, employee completes and submits Formal Grievance Form to HR Officer. HR opens confidential case file, notifies respondent within 48 hours, and conducts separate private interviews with complainant, respondent, and witnesses. HR conducts formal mediation session within 5 business days of receipt and issues written resolution.
  3. Step 3 — Management Appeal (GM Level): If either party rejects HR resolution, written appeal submitted to GM within 3 business days. GM reviews evidence, convenes Grievance Committee (GM, HR, neutral Department Head), and issues final binding internal written decision within 7 business days.

Localized Context

In tight-knit provincial communities like Gamay, strict confidentiality is enforced during grievance proceedings to prevent gossip, preserve working relationships, and protect employee dignity.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Grievances logged in confidential HR Grievance Register upon receipt.
  • Step 2 HR investigation initiated within 48 hours.
  • Zero acts of retaliation against reporting employees tolerated or recorded.
  • Target Metric: 100% of formal grievances investigated and resolved internally within 14 business days.
HR-14 Workplace Health & Safety

Objective & Scope

To establish a comprehensive Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) program complying with Republic Act No. 11058 (DOLE OSH Law) to prevent workplace injuries, eliminate hazards, provide Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), and promote staff well-being.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Certified OSH Safety Officer: Oversees safety inspections, leads OSH Committee, and files DOLE accident reports.
  • Department Heads: Enforces PPE usage and immediate hazard reporting within their shift areas.
  • All Staff: Complies strictly with safety rules, wears required PPE, and reports hazards immediately.

Execution Procedure

  1. OSH Committee Structure: Resort establishes an active OSH Committee meeting monthly, comprising: GM (Chairman), Safety Officer (Secretary), Chief Engineer, HR Officer, and 2 Employee Representatives.
  2. Mandatory PPE Provision:
    • Housekeeping: Rubber gloves, facemasks, eye protection goggles (chemical diluting).
    • Kitchen: Heat-resistant aprons, cut-resistant gloves, anti-slip water-resistant shoes.
    • Maintenance: Hardhats, safety goggles, heavy-duty insulated gloves, steel-toe boots, safety harness.
    • Pool Attendants: UV protective hats, sunblock, flotation throw devices.
  3. Hazard Identification & Inspection Routine: Weekly physical OSH audit across all departments checking wet floors, exposed wiring, chemical storage, fire extinguisher access, and ventilation.
  4. Workplace Accident Reporting Workflow:
    • Medical first aid administered immediately per SOP-SS-06.
    • Supervisor files Work Accident / Incident Report within 12 hours.
    • For disabling injuries or severe accidents, Safety Officer files official DOLE Work Accident Report (WAIR) within 24 hours.

Localized Context

High heat and humidity during Samar summer months pose heat exhaustion risks for outdoor groundskeepers and kitchen staff. Mandatory 10-minute shaded hydration breaks mandated every 2 hours during peak heat warnings (≥ 38°C Heat Index).

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Monthly OSH Committee meeting minutes recorded and posted on bulletin board.
  • 100% free PPE issuance verified for all technical/operational staff.
  • DOLE annual OSH reports submitted on schedule.
  • Target Metric: Zero lost-time work accidents; 100% OSH compliance audit score.
HR-15 Payroll Coordination

Objective & Scope

To define the bi-monthly payroll processing workflow, ensuring accurate calculation of base salaries, overtime premiums, statutory deductions, tax withholdings, and timely salary disbursements.

Responsible Staff Roles

Execution Procedure

Execution Procedure
