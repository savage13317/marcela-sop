### HOUSEKEEPING & LAUNDRY LEAD (`POS-05`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager / Resort Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Housekeeping / Room Attendant A (`POS-06`), Housekeeping / Room Attendant B (`POS-07`), and Housekeeping & Laundry Relief (`FLX-03`)
- **Role Summary:** Directs daily guest room turnarounds, public area hygiene, linen/laundry processing, and inventory controls to maintain resort cleanliness standards under a lean operational model.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Room Inspections & Status Authorization:** Conduct thorough quality inspections of cleaned guest rooms and release updated room inventory in the PMS (`SOP-HK-05`, `SOP-HK-06`).
2. **Laundry & Linen Operations Management:** Oversee in-house laundry collection, washing, drying, folding, and linen/towel storage workflows (`SOP-HK-16`, `SOP-HK-17`, `SOP-HK-18`).
3. **Chemical Safety & Equipment Controls:** Enforce strict chemical dilution protocols, hazardous material safety, and housekeeping machinery maintenance (`SOP-HK-22`, `SOP-HK-23`).
4. **Inventory & Shift Governance:** Conduct daily shift handovers and maintain accurate stock logs for guest room amenities, minibars, and linen inventory (`SOP-HK-11`, `SOP-HK-24`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Public Area & Grounds Auditing:** Inspect public restrooms, lobby spaces, and pool deck cleanliness when supervisory oversight is required (`SOP-HK-13`, `SOP-HK-14`).
2. **Function Hall Event Resets:** Oversee deep cleaning, post-event waste removal, and hall restoration following client functions (`SOP-HK-15`, `SOP-FH-19`).
3. **Turnover Surge Assistance:** Actively assist room attendants in direct cleaning duties during heavy Sunday checkout surges (`SOP-HK-04`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Room Inspection Pass Rate:** 100% pass rate on room readiness prior to guest check-in.
- **Inventory Control:** Zero stockouts of clean linens, towels, or essential room amenities.
- **Safety Compliance:** 100% adherence to chemical handling standards and safety procedures.
