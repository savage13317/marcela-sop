FO-01 Front Desk Opening Procedure

Objective & Scope

Establish operational readiness for the Front Desk prior to peak guest traffic, ensuring accounting accuracy, physical cleanliness, system synchronization, and local infrastructure preparedness for the day[cite: 11].

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Associate (Morning Shift)[cite: 11]
  • Duty Supervisor[cite: 11]

Execution Procedure

  1. Physical Readiness & Security:[cite: 11]

    • Arrive 15 minutes before the scheduled shift (05:45 AM for a 06:00 AM start)[cite: 11].
    • Unlock the main Front Office drawer/counter and inspect the lobby, reception desk, and guest seating areas for cleanliness and safety[cite: 11].
    • Turn on lobby lights, air conditioning, and guest display screens[cite: 11].

  2. System & Terminal Startup:[cite: 11]

    • Power on the Property Management System (PMS) terminal, point-of-sale (POS) card terminals, and receipt printers[cite: 11].
    • Log into the PMS using personal staff credentials[cite: 11].

  3. Cash Float Verification:[cite: 11]

    • Retrieve the shift cash drawer/float from the Front Office safe in the presence of the outgoing Night Auditor or Duty Supervisor[cite: 11].
    • Count and verify the standard opening float (â‚±10,000 in mixed denominations)[cite: 11].
    • Sign the Cash Float Logbook detailing denomination counts and variance notes (if any)[cite: 11].

  4. Information Review & Handover Audit:[cite: 11]

    • Review the Night Audit Handover Log, unhandled guest requests from the previous night, and pending maintenance tickets[cite: 11].
    • Print or generate digital daily reports: Arrivals List, Departures List, In-House Guest List, and Housekeeping Status Report[cite: 11].

  5. Local Infrastructure & Power Check:[cite: 11]

    • Verify grid power status (SAMELCO I). Confirm that the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) and standby generator unit are set to auto-start mode[cite: 11].
    • Check local internet connectivity (primary and backup satellite/LTE connections) and test landlines[cite: 11].

Localized Context

Given Gamay's coastal provincial location, morning high humidity can affect electronic terminals. Ensure counter surfaces and card reader terminals are wiped dry. Keep physical backup printouts of daily arrival lists in the emergency binder to handle potential local grid power interruptions or internet lag[cite: 11].

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Front desk and lobby neatness score: 100%[cite: 11].
FO-02 Guest Reservation Procedure

Objective & Scope

Standardize intake for direct and indirect room bookings across telephone, walk-in inquiries, messaging platforms, and online travel agencies (OTAs) while optimizing room yield and rate integrity[cite: 11].

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Reservations Clerk[cite: 11]
  • Front Office Associate[cite: 11]

Execution Procedure

  1. Inquiry Intake & Greetings:[cite: 11]

    • Answer incoming calls within three (3) rings: "Warm greetings from Marcela's Resort Hotel Gamay! This is [Name], how may I assist your stay today?"[cite: 11]
    • Capture caller details: full name, contact number, email, and preferred communication channel[cite: 11].

  2. Needs Analysis & Availability Verification:[cite: 11]

    • Identify travel dates, number of guests, room preference, and special requirements[cite: 11].
    • Check room availability on the PMS calendar for requested dates[cite: 11].

  3. Rate Quote & Inventory Management:[cite: 11]

    • Quote Published Rates, Seasonal Rates, or Corporate Rates following property structures[cite: 11].
    • Detail inclusions clearly (breakfast allocation, pool access, taxes)[cite: 11].

  4. Booking Capture in PMS:[cite: 11]

    • Create a new reservation record in the PMS with complete guest data and booking source tags[cite: 11].

  5. Payment Guarantee & Deposit Terms:[cite: 11]

    • Inform guest of deposit policy (50% initial deposit required within 48 hours for regular season; 100% advance payment for peak holidays)[cite: 11].
    • Provide accepted options: Bank Transfer (BDO/Landbank), GCash, Maya, or Credit Card link[cite: 11].

Localized Context

Guests traveling to Gamay often face travel delays due to weather conditions along the Pacific board, ferry crossing delays, or provincial road conditions. Always ask for expected modes of arrival to estimate realistic arrival times[cite: 11].

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Call answered within 3 rings[cite: 11].
