### ADMIN, FINANCE & PURCHASING OFFICER (`POS-11`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager / Resort Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises None
- **Role Summary:** Manages resort accounting, cash management, purchasing, payroll processing, and administrative compliance within the lean operational framework.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Financial Bookkeeping & Cash Control:** Manage petty cash funds, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and process supplier invoice settlements (`SOP-AD-07`, `SOP-AD-08`, `SOP-AD-10`).
2. **Procurement & Supplier Management:** Process purchase requisitions, maintain accredited vendor lists, negotiate pricing, and oversee supply receiving procedures (`SOP-AD-03`, `SOP-AD-04`, `SOP-AD-05`).
3. **Inventory Management & Asset Controls:** Audit departmental inventory logs, perform monthly physical stock counts, and maintain fixed asset records (`SOP-AD-06`, `SOP-AD-11`).
4. **Timekeeping, Payroll & Compliance:** Process employee attendance records, prepare payroll calculations, and monitor government permits, licenses, and insurance renewals (`SOP-HR-05`, `SOP-HR-15`, `SOP-AD-12`, `SOP-AD-13`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Daily Revenue Verification:** Reconcile daily Front Office and F&B revenue reports against system PMS/POS audits (`SOP-AD-09`, `SOP-FO-22`, `SOP-FB-24`).
2. **Administrative & Document Control:** Maintain secure digital and physical record systems for resort administrative files (`SOP-AD-01`, `SOP-AD-02`, `SOP-AD-14`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Payroll Timeliness:** 100% on-time and accurate payroll distribution.
- **Permit & Regulatory Compliance:** Zero penalties or delays in business permit, tax, and license renewals.
- **Financial Audit Accuracy:** 100% reconciliation precision across petty cash, accounts payable, and daily revenue logs.
