Module 03: Food & Beverage Operations

SOPs for restaurant operations, kitchen procedures, food safety, beverage service, room dining, and F&B management

FB-01 Restaurant Opening Procedure

Objective & Scope

To ensure the resort restaurant opens punctually, in immaculate condition, and fully prepared for breakfast and dining operations. Applies to all F&B service staff and kitchen opening personnel.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • F&B Supervisor, Morning Waitstaff, Morning Line Cook.
  • Secondary: Duty Manager, Security Officer (key release).

Execution Procedure

  1. Punctual Arrival & Key Retrieval:

    • Arrive 45 minutes prior to opening (e.g., 05:45 AM for 06:30 AM breakfast opening).
    • Sign out the restaurant and kitchen keys from the Front Desk key log.

  2. Facility Activation & Lighting:

    • Disarm restaurant security/alarm if applicable.
    • Turn on main dining room lights, exterior ambient lighting, and ceiling fans.
    • Set air conditioning units to 22°C–24°C in enclosed dining areas.

  3. Environment & Pest Sweep:

    • Inspect indoor and terrace dining areas for overnight pest activity or humidity condensation.
    • Wipe down all dining tables, chairs, and service stations with food-safe sanitizer.

  4. Mise en Place & Station Preparation:

    • Verify all table setups are clean, aligned, and fully stocked (salt/pepper shakers, napkin caddies, table tents).
    • Check POS terminal: power on, test receipt printing paper, verify offline buffer status.
    • Prepare welcome station, water pitchers, and complimentary breakfast buffet/alacarte setup.

  5. Kitchen Briefing & Alignment:

    • Conduct a 5-minute pre-shift briefing with kitchen staff to discuss daily specials, room occupancy count, expected breakfast influx, and 86'd items.

Localized Context

Coastal humidity in Gamay causes salt shakers to clump overnight. Add rice grains to salt shakers daily and wipe condiment bottles clean of moisture. Morning sea breezes can bring flying insects; verify electronic insect lights are activated away from direct dining lines.

FB-02 Restaurant Closing Procedure

Objective & Scope

To safely shut down the restaurant dining area and kitchen, secure perishables, reconcile daily cash/sales, and prevent fire or pest hazards overnight.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • F&B Supervisor, Closing Waitstaff, Closing Line Cook, F&B Cashier.
  • Secondary: Security Guard on duty.

Execution Procedure

  1. Last Call & Service Wind-down: Announce last call for food orders 30 minutes before closing. Gently inform remaining dining guests of closing hours.
  2. Dining Area Breakdown: Clear all dirty dishes, glassware, and condiments from tables. Store tabletop condiments in sealed food storage containers overnight.
  3. POS & Cash Reconciliation: Print POS End-of-Day (Z-Reading) report. Count cash float and total daily cash receipts; reconcile against POS totals.
  4. Kitchen & Utility Shutdown: Verify all kitchen gas lines, griddles, ovens, and fryers are turned off. Secure all perishable food items in cold storage.
  5. Security & Final Exit: Turn off AC units, dining lights, exhaust fans, and audio systems. Keep security night lights on. Lock all exterior glass doors, terrace gates, and kitchen service doors.

Localized Context

Typhoon-prone rainy nights in Samar can flood exterior patios; store all loose patio seat cushions inside the main dining hall before locking up. Tropical humidity encourages ants and rodents; zero food debris or unwashed dishware may remain overnight.

FB-03 Table Setup & Mise en Place

Objective & Scope

To standardize dining table arrangements, cutlery, glassware, and service station preparations to present a clean, high-standard boutique dining experience.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Waitstaff, Busser.
  • Secondary: F&B Supervisor.

Execution Procedure

  1. Table Cleanliness Check: Inspect dining table legs for stability; level table feet if wobbly. Clean table surface with sanitized cloth; ensure zero water spots or grease streaks.
  2. Standard Breakfast Cover Setup: Place clean placemat centered 1 inch from table edge. Dinner fork on the left; dinner knife on the right (cutting edge facing inward). Water goblet placed at the tip of the knife. Coffee cup and saucer placed on the right side of the knife with spoon on saucer.
  3. Lunch & Dinner Cover Setup: Replace coffee cup setup with polished wine glass or additional water goblet. Add folded cloth napkin (or high-grade folded paper napkin) centered on placemat.
  4. Service Station Stocking (Mise en Place): Replenish service station drawers with polished cutlery wrapped in clean trays. Stock folded napkins, toothpick holders, condiments, extra water pitchers, and bill folders.

Localized Context

Local resort guests frequently request native Samar dipping sauces (sinamak vinegar with calamansi and chili). Ensure service stations always have fresh sinamak cruets prepared daily. High minerals in local municipal water leave spots; all washed glassware and cutlery must be hand-polished using micro-fiber cloths and warm water vapor before setting on tables.

FB-04 Guest Seating & Greeting

Objective & Scope

To warmly welcome guests into Marcela's Resort Restaurant within 15 seconds of arrival, delivering authentic Waray/Filipino hospitality while managing dining flow.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Host/Hostess, Waitstaff.
  • Secondary: F&B Supervisor.

Execution Procedure

  1. Immediate Greeting: Greet guests within 15 seconds of approaching the restaurant entrance. Offer a warm smile, eye contact, and standard greeting: "Maupay nga adlaw / Good day! Welcome to Marcela's. How many are in your party today?"
  2. Table Assignment & Escort: Inquire about seating preferences (e.g., indoor air-conditioned, poolside terrace, shaded garden). Lead guests to their table at a comfortable pace. Do not walk far ahead of them. Assist guests with seating by pulling out chairs (priority for elderly, pregnant guests, and small children).
  3. Menu Presentation: Present clean, physical menus open to the first page (or direct to digital QR code menu if preferred).

Localized Context

Waray hospitality greeting (Maupay nga adlaw) should be used consistently with all local and visiting guests.

FB-05 Order Taking Procedure

Objective & Scope

To take food and beverage orders accurately, provide knowledgeable menu suggestions, identify guest dietary restrictions, and ensure a seamless culinary experience.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Waitstaff / Servers
  • F&B Supervisor, Head Chef.

Execution Procedure

  1. Timing & Readiness: Approach the table to take orders within 3 to 5 minutes after delivering menus or when guests close their menus. Stand upright, maintaining pleasant eye contact and open posture.
  2. Suggestive Selling & Upselling: Highlight daily fresh seafood catches (e.g., fresh lapu-lapu or local Gamay mud crabs) and resort specialties (e.g., Samar-style cooked seafood in coconut cream). Suggest an appetizer or signature drink to begin the meal.
  3. Recording the Order: Write down orders clearly on the server order pad using standard seat numbering (Seat 1 starts closest to server's left, going clockwise). Record specific preferences: spice level, meat temperatures, ice preference, or allergen warnings (e.g., shellfish, peanut allergies).
  4. Order Confirmation: Read back the complete order to the guest to ensure absolute accuracy: *"To confirm, Sir, we have one Sinigang na Lapu-Lapu, one Crispy Pata, and two iced teas with light ice. Is that correct?"*
  5. Menu Retrieval: Collect physical menus unless guests request to keep one.

Localized Context

Fresh seafood availability depends on local Gamay fishing yields. Servers must know exactly what fresh catch arrived that morning before taking orders. Local Samar cuisine often balances rich coconut and chili (sili). Always ask domestic and foreign tourists their preferred chili heat and sourness level for local dishes.

FB-06 POS Order Entry

Objective & Scope

To ensure accurate, instant transmission of guest orders from the dining hall to the kitchen and bar POS printers, eliminating order delays and billing errors.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Waitstaff, F&B Cashier.
  • F&B Supervisor, System Administrator.

Execution Procedure

  1. Immediate POS Logging: Log into the Point of Sale (POS) system immediately upon leaving the guest table (within 2 minutes of order taking).
  2. Table & Covers Selection: Select correct Table Number and input exact headcount (number of covers).
  3. Item Selection & Modifiers: Input ordered food and beverage items under correct categories. Select mandatory modifiers: doneness, cooking preference, side dish choice, and special notes (e.g., "NO MSG", "ALLERGY: SHELLFISH", "DRESSING ON SIDE").
  4. Send / Firing Order: Verify order items on screen against manual order pad. Press `SEND` to dispatch Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) to kitchen printers and Bar Order Tickets (BOT) to bar printers.
  5. KOT Verification & Physical Check: Visually verify that the kitchen/bar receipt printers successfully print ticket slips. If paper jam or error occurs, verbally alert kitchen hot line immediately.

Localized Context

During brief provincial power switches or generator cut-overs, POS network may drop momentarily. Servers must check offline syncing status. If POS system fails, switch instantly to triplicate manual receipt books and manually hand tickets to the kitchen line.

FB-07 Food Preparation Standards

Objective & Scope

To establish uniform culinary preparation, recipe adherence, hygiene, and cooking safety for all dishes produced in Marcela's kitchen.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Head Chef, Sous Chef, Line Cooks.
  • Kitchen Stewards.

Execution Procedure

  1. Recipe & Prep Standard Adherence: All dishes must strictly follow Marcela's standardized recipe cards (exact yields, ingredient measurements, and cooking times).
  2. Sanitary Prep Area Setup: Clean and sanitize stainless steel prep tables prior to starting prep work. Use color-coded cutting boards: RED (raw red meat), YELLOW (raw poultry), BLUE (raw seafood/fish), GREEN (fruits & vegetables), WHITE (ready-to-eat / cooked foods / dairy).
  3. Thawing & Defrosting: Thaw frozen meats in refrigerator (2°C–4°C) 24 hours in advance. Never thaw meat at ambient room temperature or in standing unheated water.
  4. Core Internal Temperature Cooking Standards: Cook all protein items to safe internal temperatures verified via probe thermometer: Poultry 74°C (165°F) for 15 seconds, Pork / Ground Meat 70°C (158°F) for 15 seconds, Seafood / Fish 63°C (145°F) for 15 seconds.
  5. Holding Temperatures: Hot food held above 60°C (140°F); cold food held below 4°C (40°F).

Localized Context

Local Gamay seafood must be cleaned, descaled, and gutted immediately upon morning delivery. Store on crushed ice inside cold storage until prep to prevent rapid decomposition in ambient tropical warmth.
