### ASSISTANT COOK / KITCHEN STEWARD (`POS-09`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to Cook / Kitchen Lead (`POS-08`) | Supervises None
- **Role Summary:** Maintains kitchen sanitation, dishwashing, and food prep operations while providing cross-functional F&B service during off-peak shifts.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Dishwashing & Kitchen Sanitation:** Operate dishwashing stations, clean cookware/utensils, and maintain kitchen equipment sanitation (`SOP-FB-12`, `SOP-FB-19`).
2. **Ingredient Prep & Staging:** Wash, peel, chop, and stage raw ingredients according to kitchen lead prep sheets (`SOP-FB-07`).
3. **Receiving & Temperature Logging:** Assist in receiving stock shipments, labeling inventory, and maintaining daily refrigerator/freezer temperature logs (`SOP-FB-14`, `SOP-FB-15`, `SOP-FB-17`).
4. **Kitchen Closing & Waste Removal:** Execute daily kitchen closing cleaning routines and proper waste disposal (`SOP-FB-02`, `SOP-FB-18`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Off-Peak Dining Service & Cashiering:** Act as restaurant server, table attendant, and POS cashier during low-occupancy dining windows (`SOP-FB-04`, `SOP-FB-05`, `SOP-FB-09`, `SOP-FB-22`).
2. **Breakfast Staging:** Assist in unlocking kitchen areas and staging early morning breakfast prep (`SOP-FB-01`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Temperature Log Accuracy:** $100\%$ daily compliance with cold storage temperature logging.
- **Sanitation Audit Pass Rate:** $100\%$ compliance on kitchen cleanliness checklists.
- **Breakage Rate:** Zero dishware breakage and zero stockouts of clean service items.
