SOPs for event reservations, coordination, setup, catering, corporate events, weddings, and special celebrations

FH-01 Function Hall Reservation

Objective & Scope

To establish a structured, error-free procedure for receiving, recording, and holding function hall date reservations. This procedure applies to all event inquiries handled by Front Office and Events staff for weddings, birthdays, corporate meetings, government seminars, and private gatherings at Marcela's Hotel & Resort.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator / Banquet Manager: Primary owner for log management, calendar verification, and tentative date blocking.
  • Front Desk Agent (FDA): Receives walk-in or telephone inquiries during off-peak/night shifts and records initial reservation details.
  • General Manager: Approves non-standard discounts, prolonged pencil bookings, or special multi-day function holds.

Execution Procedure

  1. Receive Inquiry: Greet the prospective client warmly. Gather basic details: target event date, event type, estimated guest count, preferred setup, and contact information.
  2. Check Availability: Cross-check the requested date and time slot against the Master Events Calendar (Digital PMS & Physical Events Logbook). Ensure adequate buffer time (3 hours before and 2 hours after) for cleaning, setup, and teardown.
  3. Log Pencil Booking: If the slot is available, log a "Pencil Booking" status. Note the client's name, contact details, proposed event date, and temporary holding expiration date (maximum 7 calendar days).
  4. Issue Reservation Hold Notice: Send an SMS or Email confirmation to the client stating that the date is temporarily held for 7 days pending contract signing and deposit payment.
  5. Manage Expiration: If no deposit is received by Day 7, contact the client once for confirmation. If no response or commitment is made within 24 hours, release the date hold and update the Master Events Calendar.
  6. Confirm Booking: Upon receipt of the contract and required 50% initial deposit, convert the booking status from "Pencil Booked" to "Firm Booking" in both digital and physical logs.

Localized Context

LGU & Regional Calendar Awareness: Pay close attention to local Gamay municipal town fiesta dates (July), Provincial Samar events, and local school graduation seasons (March-May) which create high demand for the Function Hall. Power & Weather Buffers: Inform clients during initial booking about the resort's 100% generator backup capability to reassure them regarding local NORSAMELCO power supply stability in Northern Samar.

FH-02 Event Inquiry & Client Coordination

Objective & Scope

To provide a standardized process for conducting client walkthroughs, determining exact event requirements, and offering personalized event options tailored to provincial and corporate client preferences.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Lead contact for conducting site tours, answering inquiries, and gathering requirements.
  • F&B Supervisor: Consults on custom menu preferences and dietary needs.
  • Head Chef: Consults on custom culinary requests and Waray local specialty inclusions.

Execution Procedure

  1. Initial Engagement: Welcome the client at the Front Office or Events Office. Offer refreshments (iced tea or cold water).
  2. Site Walkthrough: Escort the client to the Function Hall, pool area, and sample guest rooms if overnight accommodations are required. Point out air conditioning units, stage area, emergency exits, and restrooms.
  3. Requirement Gathering: Complete the Event Inquiry Worksheet, recording: Event Title / Occasion, Estimated Number of Attendees, Budget Range, Required Date & Time (Start & End), Preferred Seating Arrangement (Banquet, Classroom, Theater, Cocktail), Meal Requirements (Buffet, Plated, Snacks, Drinks), Audio-Visual Requirements (Microphones, Sound System, Projector).
  4. Provide Standard Package Sheets: Present pre-printed function packages (Weddings, Debuts, Seminars, Birthdays) with transparent pricing.
  5. Follow-Up: Send a written summary via Email or Messenger/Viber within 24 hours after the site tour.

Localized Context

Samar Hospitality: Greet clients with genuine local warmth (Kamusta po/Maupay nga adlaw). Transport Considerations: Ask if guests are traveling from neighboring towns (Lapinig, Mapanas, Palapag, Catarman) to coordinate vehicle parking spaces, shuttle coordination, or early room check-ins.

FH-03 Event Proposal & Quotation

Objective & Scope

To guide staff in calculating accurate, profitable, and transparent event quotations and issuing official proposals to clients for events hosted at Marcela's Hotel & Resort.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Prepares the itemized quotation and formal proposal document.
  • Finance / Cashier Manager: Reviews non-standard pricing, government tax exemptions, and payment terms.
  • General Manager: Signs off on proposals exceeding PHP 100,000 or custom corporate packages.

Execution Procedure

  1. Cost Calculation: Using the Event Inquiry Worksheet, calculate total charges based on: Function Hall Rental Rate (Base rate includes 4 hours with aircon, basic light/sound), Overtime Rate per hour (Hall + Aircon fees), Food & Beverage Cost per head × minimum guaranteed headcount, Special Setup / Decor / AV Equipment Rental fees, Room accommodation packages (if bundled).
  2. Draft Proposal: Fill out the standardized Event Proposal Template, specifying: Itemized breakdown of rates (Venue, Food, AV, Inclusions), Payment milestones (50% deposit upon contract signing, 50% full balance 7 days before the event), Included amenities (e.g., free use of pool for 5 VIPs, sound system, podium, parking).
  3. Internal Review: Verify prices against current F&B menu costing sheets and peak/off-peak venue rate cards.
  4. Dispatch: Send the formal proposal via Email/PDF or present an official printed copy in a folder to the client within 24 hours.
  5. Validity Notice: State clearly on the proposal: "Quotation valid for 14 calendar days from date of issue."

Localized Context

Government Billing Compliance: For Department of Education (DepEd), LGU Gamay, or Provincial Government events, ensure quotations include complete BIR Tax breakdown (VAT/Non-VAT exemptions where applicable) and support Purchase Order (PO) / Agency Voucher terms.

FH-04 Event Contract & Deposit

Objective & Scope

To ensure legally binding agreements, formal terms of agreement, and clear financial commitments between Marcela's Hotel & Resort and event clients prior to event execution.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Drafts contract, presents terms, and collects client signature.
  • Front Office / Finance Cashier: Receives payments, issues Official Receipts (OR).
  • General Manager: Executes signature on behalf of Marcela's Hotel & Resort.

Execution Procedure

  1. Contract Drafting: Populate the standard Event Service Agreement template with approved quotation details, agreed menu items, exact headcount, setup times, and breakdown of fees.
  2. Reviewing Terms with Client: Walk the client through key clauses: Minimum Guaranteed Headcount Policy (billing will be based on guaranteed headcount or actual attendance, whichever is higher), Cancellation & Refund Policy, Damage / Loss Liability clause, Force Majeure clause (Typhoons, Natural Disasters, Power Disruptions).
  3. Contract Signing: Require the client (or authorized corporate signatory) to sign all pages of the contract in duplicate.
  4. Deposit Collection: Collect the 50% Non-Refundable Mobilization Deposit.
  5. Issue Receipt: Issue an Official Receipt (OR) specifying: Client Name, Event Title, Event Date, Deposit Amount, and Remaining Balance.
  6. File Documents: Place the signed contract and receipt copy into the Master Event File (Digital & Hardcopy).

Localized Context

Provincial Payment Options: Accept cash, GCash, Bank Transfer (BDO/LBP), or Manager's Check. For regional LGU/Government clients, accept approved Purchase Orders (PO) accompanied by an official Letter of Commitment signed by the Municipal Treasurer/Mayor/Head of Agency.

FH-05 Event Planning Checklist

Objective & Scope

To maintain a synchronized operational timeline leading up to an event, ensuring seamless inter-departmental communication across Front Office, Kitchen, Housekeeping, and Engineering.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Events Coordinator: Tracks execution milestones and distributes Event Orders.
  • Head Chef: Plans food purchasing and kitchen staging.
  • Executive Housekeeper: Schedules deep cleaning and linen prep.
  • Chief Engineer / Maintenance Technician: Conducts AV, AC, and generator readiness checks.

Execution Procedure

  1. Issue Banquet Event Order (BEO): At least 14 days prior to the event, generate and distribute the official BEO to all department heads.
  2. 30-Day Milestone Check: Confirm event program outline, color themes, and special vendor requests.
  3. 14-Day Milestone Check: Reconfirm estimated headcount, finalize F&B menu choices, confirm guest accommodation counts.
  4. 7-Day Milestone Check: Collect final 50% payment balance, Lock in Final Guaranteed Headcount, Conduct inter-departmental BEO Briefing Meeting.
  5. 2-Day Milestone Check: Kitchen completes raw material procurement (seafood, meats, fresh produce), Housekeeping prepares linens, chair covers, and table skirts, Maintenance inspects AC, lighting, backup generator, and sound systems.
  6. Day of Event (4 Hours Before): Execute physical hall setup, test AV, align catering lines, and conduct final pre-event walkthrough.

Localized Context

Supply Chain Buffers in Northern Samar: Procurement of specialized items (decorations, sound gear, specific beverages, imported cheeses/meats) from Catarman or Tacloban requires at least 5 to 7 days advance ordering due to provincial transit schedules.
