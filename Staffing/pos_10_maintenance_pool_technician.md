### MAINTENANCE & POOL TECHNICIAN (`POS-10`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager / Resort Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises External Service Contractors
- **Role Summary:** Maintains resort facilities, HVAC, plumbing, backup power systems, and swimming pool water chemistry while supporting event setups and emergency response.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Preventive Maintenance & Inspections:** Conduct daily facility walk-throughs, log equipment conditions, and execute scheduled preventive maintenance across guest rooms, public spaces, and utility systems (`SOP-FM-01`, `SOP-FM-02`, `SOP-FM-03`).
2. **Electrical, HVAC & Plumbing Operations:** Inspect and maintain room air conditioning units, plumbing lines, water pumps, lighting, and general electrical systems (`SOP-FM-04`, `SOP-FM-05`, `SOP-FM-06`, `SOP-FM-07`).
3. **Backup Generator & Energy Management:** Oversee emergency generator readiness, perform load tests, manage fuel reserves, and monitor solar power systems (`SOP-FM-08`, `SOP-FM-09`).
4. **Swimming Pool Water Quality & Maintenance:** Execute daily pool chemical testing, water treatment, vacuuming, filtration system cleaning, and safety equipment maintenance (`SOP-FM-17`, `SOP-FM-18`, `SOP-FM-19`, `SOP-FM-20`, `SOP-FM-21`, `SOP-FM-22`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Function Hall Audio-Visual & Electrical Setup:** Coordinate audio-visual equipment, lighting, and power configurations for corporate and social events (`SOP-FH-08`).
2. **Typhoon Preparation & Property Protection:** Lead structural fortification, storm shutter installation, roof/gutter clearing, and post-storm damage remediation (`SOP-FM-12`, `SOP-SS-16`, `SOP-SS-20`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Generator Readiness:** 100% start-up reliability and backup power availability during grid outages.
- **Pool Safety Standard:** 100% daily compliance with chemical balance and water clarity benchmarks.
- **Work Order Resolution:** Response and resolution time maintained within 2 hours for urgent repair requests.
