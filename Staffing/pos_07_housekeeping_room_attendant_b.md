### HOUSEKEEPING / ROOM ATTENDANT B (`POS-07`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to Housekeeping & Laundry Lead (`POS-05`) | Supervises None
- **Role Summary:** Executes daily guest room cleaning and linen processing with primary secondary responsibility for pool deck maintenance and emergency spill response.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Room Cleaning & Sanitization:** Perform complete checkout turnovers, stay-over servicing, and bathroom sanitization (`SOP-HK-02`, `SOP-HK-03`, `SOP-HK-04`, `SOP-HK-07`).
2. **Guest Supplies & Minibar Restocking:** Replenish room amenities, towels, and track minibar consumption (`SOP-HK-09`, `SOP-HK-10`, `SOP-HK-11`).
3. **Pool Deck Area Housekeeping:** Clean, arrange, and sanitize pool loungers, tables, walkways, and outdoor guest facilities daily (`SOP-HK-14`).
4. **Linen & Towel Processing:** Assist in operating laundry equipment to wash, dry, fold, and store resort linens (`SOP-HK-16`, `SOP-HK-17`, `SOP-HK-18`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Event Cleanup & Venue Reset:** Assist in post-event clearing, waste disposal, and deep cleaning of the function hall (`SOP-HK-15`, `SOP-FH-19`).
2. **Weather & Spill Emergency Response:** Respond immediately to rainwater ingress, floor spills, or typhoon storm cleanup (`SOP-SS-19`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Turnover Speed:** Average room turnover cycle maintained between 25–30 minutes per room.
- **Cleanliness Rating:** Guest cleanliness score $\ge 90\%$ across all assigned areas.
- **Pool Area Upkeep:** 100% daily execution of pool deck sanitation and arrangement tasks.
