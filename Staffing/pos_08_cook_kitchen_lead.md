### COOK / KITCHEN LEAD (`POS-08`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager / Resort Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Assistant Cook / Kitchen Steward (`POS-09`) and On-Call Kitchen Helpers (`FLX-02`)
- **Role Summary:** Leads culinary operations, meal preparation, food safety compliance, and inventory management while executing event catering under the lean resort structure.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Culinary Execution & Plating:** Oversee food preparation, cooking, plating, and presentation for restaurant orders and room service (`SOP-FB-07`, `SOP-FB-08`, `SOP-FB-11`).
2. **Food Safety & Hygiene Governance:** Enforce HACCP principles, kitchen sanitation protocols, and personal hygiene standards (`SOP-FB-12`, `SOP-FB-13`, `SOP-FB-20`).
3. **Receiving & Inventory Control:** Manage raw food receiving, cold storage temperature compliance, and FIFO/FEFO stock rotation (`SOP-FB-14`, `SOP-FB-15`, `SOP-FB-16`, `SOP-FB-17`).
4. **Kitchen Admin & Waste Management:** Conduct daily stock counts, monitor food waste logs, and prepare prep sheets (`SOP-FB-18`, `SOP-FB-23`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Banquet & Event Catering:** Plan and prepare banquet menus for corporate and private events in the function hall (`SOP-FB-25`).
2. **Staff Meal Preparation:** Direct and execute daily staff meal preparation within designated budget guidelines (`SOP-FB-26`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Kitchen Ticket Time:** Average order preparation and ticket time maintained under 20 minutes.
- **Sanitation Compliance:** Kitchen hygiene and HACCP audit pass rate $\ge 95\%$.
- **Food Cost Control:** Departmental food cost percentage maintained strictly within budgetary limits.
