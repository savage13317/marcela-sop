### OPERATIONS & GUEST EXPERIENCE LEAD (`POS-COMB-01`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Night Security Personnel (`FLX-04`) & On-Call Front Office Relief (`FLX-01`/`FLX-02` Pool)
- **Role Summary:** Manages end-to-end front-of-house operations, guest reservations, check-in/out touchpoints, shift handovers, and overnight revenue auditing under a unified, multi-skilled supervisory model.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Shift Supervision & Operations:** Manage front desk operations, schedule team shifts, and coordinate on-call roster triggers (`SOP-HR-06`, `SOP-FO-21`).
2. **Guest Intake & PMS Management:** Execute guest registration, room assignments, walk-in management, and PMS/OTA updates (`SOP-FO-02`, `SOP-FO-03`, `SOP-FO-04`, `SOP-FO-05`, `SOP-FO-06`, `SOP-FO-07`, `SOP-FO-08`, `SOP-DS-10`).
3. **Cashiering & Night Revenue Audit:** Handle guest billings, deposits, and process daily revenue reconciliation and night audit roll-overs (`SOP-FO-15`, `SOP-FO-22`, `SOP-AD-09`).
4. **Overnight Safety & Security:** Monitor CCTV cameras, conduct perimeter security checks, and handle late-night arrivals (`SOP-SS-10`, `SOP-SS-12`, `SOP-FO-24`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Off-Peak F&B Order Capture:** Assist F&B operations during off-peak windows by capturing room service and restaurant POS orders (`SOP-FB-05`, `SOP-FB-06`).
2. **Event Reception & Registration:** Oversee Function Hall guest reception during event setups (`SOP-FH-09`).
3. **Early Kitchen Staging:** Assist in early breakfast staging and kitchen prep handoff during overnight hours (`SOP-FB-01`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Check-in Speed:** Check-in processing time under 3 minutes per guest.
- **Audit Accuracy:** 100% precision in night audit revenue balance reconciliation with zero unhandled discrepancies.
- **Escalation Management:** Zero unhandled escalated guest complaints.
