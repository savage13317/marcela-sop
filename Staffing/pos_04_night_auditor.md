### NIGHT AUDITOR & GUEST OPERATIONS NIGHT STAFF (`POS-04`)

* **Reporting Line:** Reports to Operations & Front Office Supervisor (`POS-02`) | Supervises `FLX-04` (Contracted Night Security Personnel)

* **Role Summary:** Executes daily system audits, revenue roll-over, and midnight guest check-in/out procedures while ensuring overnight security monitoring and early morning F&B staging under a lean cross-functional model.

* **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties

1. **Night Audit & Daily Revenue Reconciliation:** Perform end-of-day PMS audit, verify room charges, balance restaurant/bar POS bills, reconcile credit card and cash transactions, and run the daily system rollover (`SOP-FO-22`, `SOP-AD-09`).

2. **Overnight Guest Front Desk Operations:** Manage late-night guest check-ins, early check-outs, key issuances, and emergency guest inquiries during the night shift (`SOP-FO-04`, `SOP-FO-13`, `SOP-FO-14`, `SOP-FO-24`).

3. **Perimeter Security & CCTV Oversight:** Supervise contracted night security guards (`FLX-04`), conduct regular property walk-throughs, and continuously monitor CCTV security systems (`SOP-SS-10`, `SOP-SS-12`).

4. **Night Operations Reporting:** Prepare and distribute daily financial summaries, room status audit reports, and night incident logs to the General Manager and Finance Officer (`SOP-FO-22`, `SOP-AD-14`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties

1. **Early Kitchen Prep Staging:** Assist kitchen operations by unlocking dining areas, turning on essential kitchen/beverage equipment, and staging early morning breakfast prep (`SOP-FB-01`).

2. **Overnight Incident Response:** Serve as primary on-duty emergency liaison for power outages, guest medical emergencies, or severe weather alarms during night shift hours (`SOP-SS-01`, `SOP-SS-06`, `SOP-SS-18`).

3. **Public Area Night Maintenance:** Maintain cleanliness of the main lobby, front entrance, and immediate public rest areas during off-peak overnight hours (`SOP-HK-13`).

#### KPI Metrics

* **Audit Balance Accuracy:** $100\%$ precision in daily revenue reconciliation and zero unposted PMS room charges.

* **Reporting Timeliness:** Night audit closing reports completed and delivered prior to 06:00 AM daily.

* **Security & Incident Compliance:** Zero unrecorded security breaches, night shift incidents, or unauthorized facility access.
