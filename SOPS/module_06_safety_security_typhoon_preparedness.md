## SOP-SS-01: Hotel Emergency Response System

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-01
* **SOP Title:** Hotel Emergency Response System
* **Department:** Security & Management
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To establish an Incident Command System (ICS) and emergency classification framework that directs resort personnel during critical threats, natural disasters, fires, medical crises, or security incidents to safeguard life, property, and business continuity.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Incident Commander (IC):** General Manager or highest-ranking Duty Manager on shift.
* **Safety & Security Officer:** Manages immediate tactical response and physical perimeter control.
* **Front Office Supervisor:** Coordinates guest communications, headcounts, and external authority contacts.
* **Chief Engineer / Maintenance Lead:** Manages utility isolation, emergency power, and structural integrity checks.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Emergency Level Classification:**
   * **Level 1 (Minor / Localized):** Isolated event manageable by shift staff (e.g., minor pipe leak, localized small fire quickly extinguished, minor guest injury).
   * **Level 2 (Moderate / Escalated):** Requires multi-departmental coordination and potential local municipal assistance (e.g., localized structural damage, grid power blackout exceeding 4 hours, severe medical emergency, active intruder).
   * **Level 3 (Major / Critical Emergency):** Severe threat to life or property requiring total resort response and full activation of municipal disaster agencies (e.g., Signal #3+ Typhoon, uncontrolled fire, structural failure, drowning).
2. **Incident Command System (ICS) Activation:**
   * Upon escalation to Level 2 or Level 3, the IC declares ICS activation via two-way radio Channel 1.
   * Command Center established at the Front Desk Office (Primary) or Executive Office (Secondary).
3. **Role Assignment & Execution:**
   * IC delegates operational roles: Security (Perimeter/Access), Engineering (Utilities/Safety), Front Office (Guest Accounting), F&B (Emergency Rations).
4. **Log & Timeline Keeping:**
   * IC designates a log keeper to document all decisions, authority calls, timeline events, and casualty/injury reports in real time.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Gamay Provincial Infrastructure Realities:** Medical and fire response times in Gamay, Northern Samar may extend past standard urban benchmarks due to geographical distance and road conditions. The hotel must operate as a self-sufficient emergency unit for at least $2 - 4\text{ hours}$ before major provincial assistance arrives.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] ICS Command Center fully operable within $10\text{ minutes}$ of emergency escalation.
* [ ] Radio Channel 1 clear and dedicated exclusively to Emergency Command.
* [ ] Master emergency log initiated and maintained continuously during the event.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ staff role clarity during drills; zero response delays due to command confusion.

## SOP-SS-02: Fire Prevention & Fire Safety

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-02
* **SOP Title:** Fire Prevention & Fire Safety
* **Department:** Facilities & Security
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To maintain rigid fire prevention standards, inspect fire suppression hardware, enforce safe LPG and electrical handling, and conduct regular maintenance to eliminate fire risks across all 30 rooms, restaurant kitchen, function hall, and maintenance facilities.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Safety Officer / Chief Engineer:** Oversees fire safety compliance, extinguisher servicing, and alarm testing.
* **Head Chef:** Enforces kitchen fire safety, grease trap cleaning, and LPG shutoff routines.
* **Housekeeping Supervisor:** Ensures emergency hallways, fire doors, and stairwells remain unobstructed.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Monthly Fire Extinguisher Audit:**
   * Inspect all ABC Dry Chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, and Wet Chemical (K-Class) fire extinguishers across the property.
   * Check pressure gauge (needle must remain strictly in the green zone).
   * Verify safety pin and tamper seal are intact. Sign and date inspection tag.
2. **Kitchen Fire Safety Protocols:**
   * Kitchen wet chemical suppression system above commercial stoves inspected semi-annually.
   * Exhaust hood filters cleaned weekly to prevent heavy grease accumulation.
   * LPG main supply line valves closed tightly every night post-kitchen closure.
3. **Electrical Fire Hazard Audits:**
   * Daily check of utility panels for loose wiring, burning odors, or tripping breakers.
   * Ban high-wattage guest appliances (e.g., portable electric stoves) not provided by the resort.
4. **Quarterly Fire Alarm & Smoke Detector Testing:**
   * Test optical smoke detectors in guest rooms, hallways, and function hall quarterly using aerosol smoke testers.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Coastal Corrosion on Fire Equipment:** Salt air in Gamay corrodes fire extinguisher nozzles and steel brackets. Coat metal brackets with anti-corrosion grease quarterly and replace rusted safety pins immediately.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] $100\%$ of fire extinguishers inspected, pressurized, and signed monthly.
* [ ] Kitchen exhaust hoods deep-cleaned weekly without fail.
* [ ] LPG safety shut-off valve verified closed every night at F&B close.
* **Target Metric:** Zero fire incidents; $100\%$ pass rate on Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP Gamay) inspections.

## SOP-SS-03: Fire Alarm Response

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-03
* **SOP Title:** Fire Alarm Response
* **Department:** Front Office, Security & Engineering
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To define the rapid, step-by-step verification and response procedure upon activation of the main Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) to prevent panic, identify false alarms, or initiate immediate fire suppression and evacuation.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Front Desk Agent:** Monitors Fire Alarm Control Panel (FACP) and dispatches floor response.
* **Duty Security Officer / Duty Technician:** Proceeds immediately to indicated zone for physical verification.
* **Duty Manager:** Takes overall command of response and decides on building evacuation.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **FACP Signal Triggered:**
   * Alarm horn sounds at Front Desk panel indicating specific zone (e.g., "Zone 3 - 2nd Floor Guest Corridor").
   * Front Desk Agent notes time and zone, silences panel buzzer locally, but **DOES NOT** reset the system.
2. **Immediate Physical Verification (Target Time: $< 2\text{ mins}$):**
   * Front Desk dispatches Duty Security and Duty Tech via radio: *"Security, verify Zone 3 immediately."*
   * Security runner proceeds to zone with primary ABC fire extinguisher and master access card.
3. **Condition Assessment & Reporting:**
   * **Case A: FALSE ALARM (Dust, insect, steam):** Security reports "Code Green - False Alarm" back to Front Desk. Tech resets panel. Front Desk logs cause.
   * **Case B: REAL FIRE / UNCONTROLLED SMOKE:** Security shouts warning, attempts initial suppression using extinguisher if small ($< 1\text{ sq. meter}$). If fire spreads, security reports "CODE RED Zone 3" via radio.
4. **Code Red Alarm Activation:**
   * Front Desk Agent immediately hits the **General Building Evacuation Siren** on FACP.
   * Front Desk Agent dials **BFP Gamay Emergency Line** immediately.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Samar Humidity & False Alarms:** High humidity or sea mist can occasionally trigger sensitive smoke detectors. Staff must execute physical verification rapidly ($< 120\text{ seconds}$) before triggering full resort panic, while treating every alarm as a real fire until proven otherwise.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Zone verification completed on-site within $2\text{ minutes}$ of panel alarm.
* [ ] Master FACP never reset prior to physical site confirmation.
* [ ] BFP Gamay emergency speed-dial functional at Front Desk.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ compliance with the 2-minute verification rule; zero unverified panel resets.

## SOP-SS-04: Fire Evacuation Procedure

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-04
* **SOP Title:** Fire Evacuation Procedure
* **Department:** All Resort Departments
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To execute a swift, orderly, and complete evacuation of all hotel guests, staff, and visitors during an active fire or major life-safety threat using the **R.A.C.E.** protocol.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Incident Commander (GM/Duty Manager):** Directs total building evacuation and liaises with BFP Gamay.
* **Floor Evacuation Wardens (Housekeeping / Security):** Sweeps hallways, guest rooms, and public areas.
* **Front Desk Agent:** Retrieves Master Guest In-House List, key cards, and emergency mobile phone.
* **Assembly Area Coordinator:** Conducts guest headcount at designated assembly zone.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Evacuation Declaration:** General Evacuation Siren sounded continuous pattern. Emergency Voice Announcement made over public address system: *"Attention all guests and staff: Please proceed calmly via the nearest exit to the main assembly area on the front lawn."*
2. **Execute R.A.C.E. Protocol:**
   * **R - Rescue:** Guide guests in immediate danger to safety without putting staff lives at unreasonable risk.
   * **A - Alarm:** Ensure FACP general alarm is active and BFP Gamay called ($911$ / Local BFP).
   * **C - Contain:** Close all guest room doors and corridor fire doors behind evacuating persons to starve fire of oxygen.
   * **E - Evacuate:** Direct everyone to exterior exit routes. **NEVER USE ELEVATORS.**
3. **Floor Wardens Room Sweep:**
   * Wardens knock loudly, unlock every room on assigned corridor: *"Fire Alarm! Evacuate immediately!"*
   * Mark checked room doors with a designated evacuation tag or leave door wide open once verified empty.
4. **Assembly Area Headcount:**
   * Direct all evacuees to **Assembly Area A (Main Garden Lawn near Entry Gate)**.
   * Front Desk Agent cross-checks evacuees against active PMS In-House Guest List and Staff Shift Roster.
   * Report missing persons immediately to BFP Fire Ground Commander.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Power Outage During Fire:** Fires frequently trip main circuit breakers or damage wiring. Backup battery emergency exit lights automatically illuminate exit routes for 30–60 minutes. Floor wardens must carry heavy-duty LED flashlights attached to their duty belts at all times.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] PMS active guest list printed or accessed digitally within $3\text{ minutes}$ of evacuation signal.
* [ ] Complete building floor sweep completed in $< 5\text{ minutes}$.
* [ ] Headcount accounting completed at assembly area within $10\text{ minutes}$.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ guest and staff safety accounting; zero structural entrapment.

## SOP-SS-06: First Aid & Medical Emergency

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-06
* **SOP Title:** First Aid & Medical Emergency
* **Department:** Front Office, Security & Housekeeping
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To provide immediate, competent basic first aid assessment, life support stabilization, and medical referral for guests or staff experiencing sudden illness, trauma, or injury on resort grounds.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Certified First Responder (Shift Staff / Security):** Administers immediate basic first aid / CPR.
* **Front Office Agent / Duty Manager:** Coordinates emergency ambulance calls and guest medical transport.
* **Safety Officer:** Maintains first aid kits, AED equipment, and medical supply inventory.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Initial Assessment (Scene Safety & Patient Status):**
   * Ensure scene is safe to enter. Approach victim, check responsiveness ($A-V-P-U$: Alert, Verbal, Pain, Unresponsive).
   * Call for back-up staff and retrieve First Aid Kit + Automated External Defibrillator (AED) from Front Desk.
2. **First Aid Administration:**
   * **Minor Cut / Abrasion:** Clean wound with sterile saline solution, apply povidone-iodine, cover with sterile gauze and adhesive bandage.
   * **Heat Exhaustion / Fainting:** Move patient to cool shaded area, loosen tight clothing, elevate feet, offer oral rehydration salts (ORS) in small sips if conscious.
   * **Severe Trauma / Fractures:** Immobilize injured limb, apply direct pressure to heavy bleeding using thick sterile pads. **DO NOT** move patient with suspected spinal injury unless immediate life threat exists.
3. **Medical Emergency Transport Escalation:**
   * If patient exhibits severe symptoms (chest pain, breathing difficulty, deep unconsciousness, severe bleeding, stroke signs):
     * Contact **Gamay Rural Health Unit (RHU) / Municipal Health Office (MHO)** immediately.
     * Prepare resort emergency vehicle (or local municipal ambulance) for transport to **Catarman Provincial Hospital** for advanced trauma/cardiac care.
4. **Medical Incident Documentation:** Record patient name, room number, incident details, vital signs, first aid given, and transport destination in the **Resort Medical Logbook**.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Transit Distance to Major Hospitals:** Gamay RHU provides basic stabilization. Emergency transport to the primary tertiary hospital in Catarman, Northern Samar takes approximately $1.5 - 2\text{ hours}$ by ambulance. Early triage and prompt transport calls are vital for serious conditions.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] First Aid kits stationed at Front Desk, Kitchen, Engineering, and Pool Deck fully stocked.
* [ ] Expiration dates on medications, antiseptics, and burn gels audited monthly.
* [ ] Shift team includes at least one active BLS/CPR certified staff member per shift.
* **Target Metric:** Emergency medical response initiated within $< 3\text{ minutes}$ of notification.

* **General Manager:** Manages long-term guest relations, insurance claims, and legal considerations.

## SOP-SS-08: Drowning / Pool Emergency

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-08
* **SOP Title:** Drowning / Pool Emergency
* **Department:** Pool Attendants, Security & Front Office
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To define the critical water rescue, victim extraction, cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), automated external defibrillation (AED), and emergency notification workflow during a pool drowning or near-drowning emergency.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Primary Rescuer (Pool Attendant / Security / Staff):** Executes immediate water rescue and CPR.
* **Secondary Rescuer:** Calls emergency assistance, retrieves AED and First Aid Kit.
* **Front Office Agent:** Dispatches ambulance and clears driveway entry.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **RESCUE INITIATION (Target: $< 20\text{ seconds}$ to water entry):**
   * Spot swimmer in distress. Sound whistle loudly (3 long blasts) to alert staff.
   * Enter water, support victim's head and neck, extract victim safely onto pool deck.
2. **CPR & AED SEQUENCE (Target: $< 60\text{ seconds}$ post-extraction):**
   * Lay victim flat on back on pool deck. Check breathing and pulse for max $10\text{ seconds}$.
   * If non-breathing / no pulse:
     * Assign staff member: *"You! Call Front Desk for Ambulance and bring the AED!"*
     * Begin CPR immediately: Give $5\text{ initial rescue breaths}$, followed by $30\text{ chest compressions}$ to $2\text{ rescue breaths}$ at a rate of $100 - 120\text{ compressions/min}$.
     * Apply AED pads as soon as unit arrives (dry victim's chest quickly before applying pads). Follow AED voice prompts strictly.
3. **AMBULANCE DISPATCH & SCENE MANAGEMENT:**
   * Front Desk dials Gamay RHU / Emergency Ambulance service.
   * Clear all swimming guests from pool and close pool gates immediately.
4. **POST-INCIDENT PROTOCOL:** Keep pool closed until water clarity, chemical balance, and police/management incident reports are fully cleared.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Community Emergency Referral:** If Gamay municipal ambulance is delayed, immediately utilize the resort's dedicated emergency van driven by a trained resort driver to meet the incoming provincial ambulance en route to Catarman.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Water rescue initiated within $20\text{ seconds}$ of distress observation.
* [ ] CPR commenced within $60\text{ seconds}$ of extraction.
* [ ] AED pads applied correctly following voice guidance.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ staff execution speed on emergency resuscitation protocols.

## SOP-SS-09: Missing Guest / Person Procedure

* **SOP Title:** Missing Guest / Person Procedure
* **Department:** Front Office, Security & Management
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Front Office Supervisor:** Receives missing report, verifies registration, logs details.
* **Security Lead:** Directs physical search sweep of resort grounds and CCTV review.
* **General Manager:** Escalates case to Philippine National Police (PNP Gamay) and family liaisons.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Information Gathering & Verification:**
   * Receive report from family or companion. Note missing person's name, age, physical description, clothing, room number, medical conditions, last seen time, and phone number.
   * Attempt phone call / messaging to missing guest's cellular phone immediately.

4. **Police & Family Coordination:**
   * Provide PNP Gamay with registered ID copy, recent photograph (if available from family), CCTV clips, and search timeline.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Coastal Beach & Marine Risk:** Because Gamay borders coastal waters, search sweeps must immediately prioritize checking the beach shoreline and pool waters during the first 10 minutes of a missing person report.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] Internal resort physical search completed within $30\text{ minutes}$.
* [ ] CCTV log reviewed back to last seen timestamp within $20\text{ minutes}$.
* [ ] Immediate police notification for missing minors ($< 30\text{ minutes}$).

## SOP-SS-10: Suspicious Person / Security Incident

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure

     * Lock resort lobby glass doors to secure guests inside.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Provincial Hospitality Balance:** Rural provincial culture values warmth and openness. Security officers must balance vigilant access control with polite provincial hospitality so genuine local visitors or walk-in diners feel welcomed rather than intimidated.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles

```
PAGASA Signal ──► Resort Action Stage
TCWS #1 (39-61 km/h)   ──► STAGE BLUE (Heightened Awareness)
TCWS #2 (62-88 km/h)   ──► STAGE YELLOW (Active Property Preparation)
TCWS #3 (89-117 km/h)  ──► STAGE ORANGE (Full Lockdown & Guest In-Room Safety)
TCWS #4/5 (>118 km/h)  ──► STAGE RED (Maximum Emergency Defense & Survival Mode)
```

   * **STAGE RED (TCWS #4/5):** Complete lockdown of all glass doors, assemble non-secure room guests into reinforced Function Hall concrete zone, monitor structural integrity continuously.

### 4. Localized Context
* **Northern Samar Typhoon Highway:** Gamay lies directly along the Eastern Visayas typhoon belt. Weather conditions can deteriorate rapidly from Signal #1 to Signal #3 within $12\text{ hours}$. Staff must execute preparation tasks early without waiting for local rainfall to begin.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] PAGASA bulletins logged every 3 hours during active weather systems.
* [ ] Resort Stage transitions declared promptly upon official PAGASA signal issuance.
* [ ] Guest weather advisories printed and distributed at Stage Yellow.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ completion of stage preparations prior to storm landfall.

## SOP-SS-18: Power Outage / Brownout Procedure

* **SOP Code:** SOP-SS-18
* **SOP Title:** Power Outage / Brownout Procedure
* **Department:** Facilities, Engineering & Front Office
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Revision Number:** 1.0

### 1. Objective & Scope
To manage main grid power loss, ensure automatic standby generator transfer within $15\text{ seconds}$, monitor fuel consumption, protect sensitive electronic gear, and maintain guest room power continuously.

### 2. Responsible Staff Roles
* **Duty Maintenance Technician:** Monitors ATS transfer, generator load, engine oil, and fuel levels.
* **Front Desk Agent:** Reassures guests, monitors elevator status (if applicable), logs outage duration.

### 3. Step-by-Step Execution Procedure
1. **Automatic Grid Failure Response ($0 - 15\text{ Seconds}$):**
   * Main grid power fails (NORSAMELCO outage).
   * Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) senses voltage loss, triggers diesel generator start, and transfers resort load within $15\text{ seconds}$.
   * Emergency lighting in corridors and public areas illuminates instantly.
2. **Technician Generator Verification ($1 - 5\text{ Minutes}$):**
   * Technician checks generator panel: Voltage ($220\text{V} \pm 5\%$), Frequency ($60\text{ Hz}$), Running Amperage, Oil Pressure, and Engine Temperature ($80^\circ\text{C} - 90^\circ\text{C}$).
   * Verify Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) stabilizes load.
3. **Extended Outage Fuel Management (Outages $> 4\text{ Hours}$):**
   * Check fuel gauge every 2 hours. Log fuel consumption ($\text{Liters/Hour}$).
   * Refill main base tank from reserve diesel drums when fuel drops to $50\%$ capacity.
4. **Grid Power Restoration Procedure:**
   * Grid power restored and stabilized for minimum $5\text{ continuous minutes}$.
   * ATS automatically transfers load back to grid. Generator executes $3\text{-minute}$ cool-down cycle before shutting down.

### 4. Localized Context
* **NORSAMELCO Grid Instability:** Unscheduled brownouts occur frequently in Northern Samar. The resort's ATS and generator system must remain on standby 24/7. Critical servers, PMS, and CCTV must run behind UPS battery backups so data is never lost during the 15-second generator transfer window.

### 5. Quality Assurance Checklist & Metrics
* [ ] ATS transfer completed in $< 15\text{ seconds}$ of grid failure.
* [ ] Generator parameters ($220\text{V} / 60\text{ Hz}$) verified stable post-transfer.
* [ ] Minimum $500\text{L}$ diesel reserve maintained in storage at all times.
* **Target Metric:** $100\%$ emergency power transition success rate; zero server crashes.
