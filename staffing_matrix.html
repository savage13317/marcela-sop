Staffing Matrix & Organization Chart

Authoritative map of every operational position at Marcela's Resort Hotel under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model: 11 Regular Core Employees (POS-01 to POS-11), a 17–19 person On-Call Flexible Pool (FLX-01 to FLX-03), and Contracted / Outsourced Services (FLX-04 and retainers). Positions are grouped into four department clusters plus Admin/Finance, following the Owner → General Manager → Cluster Lead → Associate hierarchy. Every core employee performs secondary cross-functional duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management.

Quick Jump to Position:

POS-01 General Manager / Resort Manager

Objective & Scope

Single internal authority for the entire resort. Drives commercial sales, GOP targets, operational SOP compliance, and overall resort performance under the lean staffing framework. Top node of the organization chart; holds final approval over hiring, discipline, emergency authorization, and inter-department grievances.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: Owner / Proprietor (external authority).
  • Directly supervises: POS-02 (Ops & FO Supervisor), POS-05 (HK & Laundry Lead), POS-08 (Cook Lead), POS-10 (Maintenance Tech), POS-11 (Admin/Finance Officer).
  • Final-appeal authority: unresolved grievances escalate to the GM after the immediate supervisor and cluster-lead steps.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Commercial & Financial Governance:
    • Oversees corporate/government lead conversions, contract negotiations, event proposals, and function hall bookings (SOP-FH-01, SOP-FH-03, SOP-FH-04).
    • Monitors monthly P&L, authorizes operational expenses, reviews daily revenue reports (SOP-AD-09, SOP-AD-10).
  2. SOP Auditing & Operational Governance:
    • Conducts daily walk-through inspections across Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B, Facilities, and Pool areas (SOP-FM-01, SOP-HK-05, SOP-FB-12).
    • Oversees recruitment, performance evaluations, disciplinary actions, and staff scheduling across core and on-call rosters (SOP-HR-01, SOP-HR-06, SOP-HR-11).
  3. Disaster Incident Command:
    • Acts as Incident Commander during typhoon alerts, severe weather, power grid failures, and property emergencies (SOP-SS-14, SOP-SS-15, SOP-SS-21).
  4. Secondary / Cross-Functional:
    • Intervenes personally during escalated guest complaints to secure service recovery (SOP-FO-11).
    • Supports function hall registration, VIP reception, and front desk surges during major group arrivals (SOP-FH-09, SOP-FO-20).
    • Coordinates with local government units (LGUs) and key vendors during contract renewals or license audits (SOP-AD-04, SOP-AD-12).

Localized Context

At Gamay's coastal provincial site, the GM keeps a physical emergency binder (arrivals, departures, in-house lists, typhoon response) so decisions remain possible during SAMELCO grid-power outages or internet lag. Designated point of contact for SAMELCO load-shedding and Starlink/4G network failures.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Gross Operating Profit (GOP): monthly and annual revenue/profit targets realized.
  • Guest satisfaction rating ≥ 90% positive across online and on-site reviews.
  • Labor & operational cost ratio within budget allocations.
  • SOP and safety checklist compliance pass rate ≥ 95%.
POS-02 Operations & Front Office Supervisor

Objective & Scope

Directs daily Front Office operations, manages staff shift scheduling, and coordinates cross-departmental guest service delivery and flexible on-call roster activations under the resort's lean operational framework. Operational bridge between the GM and the Front Office associates.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: General Manager (POS-01).
  • Directly supervises: Guest Services Officer (POS-03), Night Auditor (POS-04), and the Flexible On-Call Pool (FLX-01 to FLX-03).
  • Peer cluster leads: POS-05 (HK), POS-08 (F&B) — coordinate on lost & found, guest transfers, and event surges.

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Front Office Operational Management:
    • Oversees daily front desk workflows, guest check-in/check-out procedures, and reservation accuracy (SOP-FO-01, SOP-FO-04, SOP-FO-07, SOP-FO-14).
    • Handles Level-2 guest complaints, complex service recoveries, and authorized room transfers (SOP-FO-11, SOP-FO-12).
  2. Shift Scheduling & Roster Activation:
    • Prepares weekly staff shift schedules and monitors occupancy/event calendars to trigger on-call personnel (SOP-HR-05, SOP-HR-06).
  3. Cashiering & Shift Reconciliation:
    • Conducts daily shift handovers, supervises front desk cashiering, and reviews end-of-shift revenue balancing (SOP-FO-15, SOP-FO-21, SOP-FO-22).
  4. Secondary / Cross-Functional:
    • Assists with event guest reception and client coordination during function hall events (SOP-FH-06, SOP-FH-09).
    • Covers Night Audit duties (POS-04) during scheduled rest days, emergency leaves, or surge periods (SOP-FO-22).
    • Conducts routine walk-throughs of lobby and pool areas when the Housekeeping lead is off-site (SOP-HK-13, SOP-FM-17).

Localized Context

Confirms grid-power (SAMELCO I) and ATS/generator standby each shift, and keeps printed arrival/departure lists in the emergency binder for provincial load-shedding or internet lag. Verifies primary and backup satellite/LTE connections before opening the desk.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Check-in efficiency: average guest check-in under 3 minutes.
  • Flexible roster precision: 100% accuracy triggering on-call staffing relative to occupancy and event thresholds.
  • Zero unhandled or escalated front office guest complaints.
  • 100% compliance with weekly scheduling and timekeeping submissions.

    • Processes guest payments, cash drops, credit card transactions, and maintains shift balance accuracy (SOP-FO-15, SOP-FO-16, SOP-FO-17).

    • Logs initial event inquiries and walk-in function hall leads, transferring details to the GM (SOP-FH-02).
    • Assists in lobby cleanliness inspection and reports immediate maintenance/housekeeping issues when supervisors are off-site (SOP-HK-13, SOP-FM-01).

Localized Context

OTA and digital-channel inquiries (SOP-DS-10) run through Starlink primary with 4G LTE backup; during outages, printed rate sheets and manual booking forms keep the desk operational. Walk-in function hall leads are logged and routed to the GM for contract negotiation.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Check-in speed: average under 3 minutes per party.
  • Reservation entry precision: 100% accuracy in PMS details, room rates, and guest notes.
  • Cash balance accuracy: zero drawer discrepancies or missing charge slips.
  • Front desk guest satisfaction exceeding 90% positive.
POS-04 Night Auditor / Guest Operations

Objective & Scope

Executes daily system audits, revenue roll-over, and midnight guest check-in/out procedures while ensuring overnight security monitoring and early-morning F&B staging. Closes the 24/7 night-audit gap in the lean staffing baseline.

Position Relationships

  • Reports to: Operations & Front Office Supervisor (POS-02).
  • Supervises: FLX-04 contracted night security personnel (external guard agency).
  • Relief: POS-02 covers its audit duties during rest days, emergency leaves, or surge periods (SOP-FO-22).

Core Duties & Responsibilities

  1. Night Audit & Revenue Reconciliation:
    • Performs end-of-day PMS audit, verifies room charges, balances restaurant/bar POS bills, reconciles card and cash transactions, and runs the daily system rollover (SOP-FO-22, SOP-AD-09).
  2. Overnight Guest Operations:
    • Manages late-night guest check-ins, early check-outs, key issuances, and emergency guest inquiries during the night shift (SOP-FO-04, SOP-FO-13, SOP-FO-14, SOP-FO-24).
  3. Perimeter Security & Reporting:
    • Supervises contracted night security guards, conducts property walk-throughs, and monitors CCTV systems (SOP-SS-10, SOP-SS-12).
    • Prepares and distributes daily financial summaries, room-status audit reports, and night incident logs to the GM and Finance Officer (SOP-FO-22, SOP-AD-14).
  4. Secondary / Cross-Functional:
    • Assists kitchen operations by unlocking dining areas, turning on essential equipment, and staging early breakfast prep (SOP-FB-01).
    • Serves as primary on-duty emergency liaison for power outages, guest medical emergencies, or severe-weather alarms (SOP-SS-01, SOP-SS-06, SOP-SS-18).
    • Maintains cleanliness of the main lobby, front entrance, and public rest areas during off-peak overnight hours (SOP-HK-13).

Localized Context

Night audit closing reports must be delivered prior to 06:00 AM daily. During SAMELCO grid outages or typhoon alarms, the Night Auditor is the on-duty emergency liaison coordinating the GM's incident command and the outsourced guard agency.

QA Checklist & Key Metrics

  • Audit balance accuracy: 100% precision in daily revenue reconciliation, zero unposted PMS room charges.
  • Reporting timeliness: night audit closing reports delivered prior to 06:00 AM.
  • Security & incident compliance: zero unrecorded breaches, night-shift incidents, or unauthorized facility access.

Typhoon Contingency — SOP-SS-15 Stay-In Activation (Signal #2)

When PAGASA raises Signal No. 2, a four-person stay-in team is activated to keep the resort operational through the storm window:

  • 1 Management Lead — General Manager (POS-01) or Operations Supervisor (POS-02) as Incident Commander (SOP-SS-14, SOP-SS-15, SOP-SS-21).
  • 1 Maintenance Technician — POS-10 running generator readiness and property fortification (SOP-FM-08, SOP-SS-16).
  • 1 Housekeeping Utility — POS-06/POS-07 or FLX-03 relief handling ingress, spills, and boarding (SOP-SS-19).
  • 1 Night Audit / Front Office — POS-04 (or POS-02 relief) holding guest accounting and communications (SOP-SS-22).
