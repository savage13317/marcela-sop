Module 08: Digital Systems, Records & Maintenance

SOPs for digital systems management, IT operations, cybersecurity, guest records, CCTV, Wi-Fi monitoring, and record maintenance

DS-01 Hotel Digital Systems Overview

Objective & Scope

To provide a comprehensive operational framework for all hardware, software, network infrastructure, and digital tools utilized across Marcela's Hotel & Resort. Applies to all resort departments and personnel utilizing digital touchpoints.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • General Manager (GM)
  • IT Officer / System Administrator
  • Department Heads, Front Desk Staff, Cashiers.

Execution Procedure

  1. System Architecture Identification: Maintain a master directory of core systems: Property Management System (PMS): Front Desk, Reservations, Room Inventory; Point of Sale (POS): Restaurant, Bar, Room Service, Function Hall Billing; Network Infrastructure: Dual-WAN Router (Starlink Primary + Local 4G LTE Backup), Unifi Access Points, UPS units; CCTV System: 16-channel / 32-channel NVR with localized storage; Payment Gateways: Maya Terminal, GCash Merchant QR Ph, Bank Transfer Portals.
  2. Daily Startup Audit: Verify online status of core server/router at 06:00 AM daily. Ensure Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) battery levels read 100%. Perform quick connectivity check across Front Desk and POS terminals.
  3. Operational Readiness Check: Confirm synchronization between PMS and channel manager/OTAs (Agoda, Booking.com). Verify POS system printer connectivity in Kitchen and Restaurant cashier stations.
  4. System Outage Escalation: Immediately log connectivity drops in the Digital Systems Logbook. Switch manually to offline failover protocols if downtime exceeds 5 minutes (refer to SOP-DS-02/03).

Localized Context

Gamay, Northern Samar experiences sudden power interruptions and heavy rain cell-site degradations. All core network equipment must remain plugged directly into dedicated online UPS systems backed up by the resort generator.

DS-02 PMS / Reservation System Operation

Objective & Scope

To standardize daily operation, guest booking management, check-in/check-out processing, and offline synchronization within the Property Management System (PMS).

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Front Office Manager (FOM)
  • Front Desk Agents (FDA), Reservations Staff.

Execution Procedure

  1. Shift Login & Initialization: Log in using individual assigned credentials (never share accounts). Review the daily dashboard: arrivals, departures, stay-overs, out-of-order rooms, and pending payments.
  2. Processing Reservations & Check-Ins: Cross-reference guest booking details (OTA voucher, walk-in registration, or direct booking). Assign room based on clean/inspected room status received from Housekeeping. Collect cash, GCash, or card deposit and record transaction immediately under guest folio.
  3. Handling System Downtime (Offline Protocol): Print physical Daily Arrival & Departure Manifests at the start of every shift (06:00, 14:00, 22:00). If PMS goes offline during power/internet outage, record check-ins/check-outs manually on the Manual Guest Ledger. Re-enter manual ledger records into the PMS within 1 hour after connectivity restoration.
  4. End-of-Day Reconciliation: Perform night audit balance run; ensure all posted charges align with cash drawer, GCash logs, and card slips.

Localized Context

Northern Samar weather can affect cloud-PMS connections. Printing shift manifests on paper ensures check-in delays do not occur during sudden fiber or satellite connection drops.

DS-03 POS System Operation

Objective & Scope

To regulate order entry, table management, charge posting to guest folios, and daily sales closing across F&B, Pool, and Function Hall outlets.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Restaurant Manager
  • F&B Cashiers, Waitstaff, Bartenders.

Execution Procedure

  1. POS Opening Routine: Power on POS terminals and receipt printers at 06:00 AM. Log in using individual cashier PIN code. Verify opening float cash balance against physical cash drawer.
  2. Order Entry & Processing: Select table number/outlet zone; enter items accurately based on guest request and special dietary notes. Send order digitally to Kitchen Display / Kitchen Slip Printer. For room charges: Search guest name/room number in POS; verify room active status on PMS before posting. Require guest signature on printed bill slip.
  3. Offline POS Mode (Power Failure / Network Loss): Switch POS terminal to local offline mode or use carbon-copy Order Tickets (OTs) and Official Receipts (ORs). Calculate taxes (VAT 12%) and service charge manually using local rate charts. Post offline receipts to the digital POS system immediately upon network recovery.
  4. POS Closing Routine: Generate End-of-Day (Z-Reading) report. Reconcile total cash, GCash transfers, card slips, and room charges against system report.

Localized Context

Ensure thermal paper rolls are stored in airtight plastic containers to prevent moisture damage from high provincial humidity in Gamay.
