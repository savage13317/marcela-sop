### CULINARY & FOOD SERVICE SPECIALIST (`POS-COMB-03`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Banquet Kitchen Helpers & On-Call Kitchen Staff (`FLX-02`)
- **Role Summary:** Operates as a single-point kitchen and dining lead responsible for menu preparation, kitchen sanitation, inventory receiving, dishwashing, and low-occupancy F&B dining service.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Culinary Preparation & Cooking:** Execute menu items, food plating, and portion control in compliance with quality standards (`SOP-FB-07`, `SOP-FB-08`).
2. **Sanitation & Stewarding:** Perform kitchen station dishwashing, equipment cleaning, and waste management (`SOP-FB-12`, `SOP-FB-19`).
3. **Receiving & Cold Chain Management:** Inspect incoming food deliveries, maintain FIFO storage standards, and complete daily temperature logs for cold storage (`SOP-FB-14`, `SOP-FB-15`, `SOP-FB-16`, `SOP-FB-17`).
4. **Kitchen & Dining Setup:** Manage restaurant opening and closing procedures (`SOP-FB-01`, `SOP-FB-02`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Dining & Billing Execution:** Greet guests, take food orders, serve meals, and process F&B billing during off-peak windows or solo shift periods (`SOP-FB-04`, `SOP-FB-05`, `SOP-FB-09`, `SOP-FB-22`).
2. **Event Banquet Production:** Prepare banquet food production for Function Hall catered events (`SOP-FB-25`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Kitchen Ticket Time:** Food kitchen ticket time maintained under 20 minutes.
- **Temperature Log Adherence:** 100% compliance on daily cold-storage temperature logging.
- **Food Safety Pass Rate:** Zero food safety contamination incidents and kitchen hygiene pass rate >95%.
