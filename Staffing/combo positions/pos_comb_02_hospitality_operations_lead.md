### HOSPITALITY OPERATIONS LEAD & ATTENDANT (`POS-COMB-02`)

- **Reporting Line:** Reports to General Manager (`POS-01`) | Supervises Housekeeping & Laundry Relief Pool (`FLX-03`)
- **Role Summary:** Directs and executes complete room turnovers, public area sanitation, and internal laundry workflows while maintaining room quality standards across the property.
- **Mandatory Clause:** *"Under the resort's Lean Cross-Functional Model, employees are required to perform secondary operational duties during shift overlaps, low-occupancy periods, or peak event surges as assigned by management."*

#### Primary Duties
1. **Room Turnover & Inspection:** Execute daily guest room cleaning, bed making, deep cleaning, and room inspection releases (`SOP-HK-02`, `SOP-HK-03`, `SOP-HK-04`, `SOP-HK-05`, `SOP-HK-08`, `SOP-HK-12`).
2. **Sanitation & Amenities Control:** Sanitize bathrooms, replenish amenities, and manage minibar/in-room inventories (`SOP-HK-07`, `SOP-HK-10`, `SOP-HK-11`).
3. **Laundry & Chemical Operations:** Wash, dry, iron, fold, and store guest/linen inventory while strictly following chemical safety guidelines (`SOP-HK-16`, `SOP-HK-17`, `SOP-HK-18`, `SOP-HK-22`).
4. **Public Area & Deck Upkeep:** Clean public areas, restaurant spaces, and pool decks (`SOP-HK-13`, `SOP-HK-14`, `SOP-HK-15`).

#### Secondary / Cross-Functional Duties
1. **Function Hall Reset:** Reset and clean the Function Hall following event completions (`SOP-FH-19`).
2. **Maintenance Defect Reporting:** Report minor engineering/maintenance defects observed during room cleanings directly to maintenance (`SOP-FM-03`).

#### KPI Metrics
- **Room Turnaround Time:** Room turnaround time of 25–30 minutes per room.
- **Hygiene & Quality Pass Rate:** 100% pass rate on room quality and hygiene inspection checklists.
- **Linen & Chemical Control:** Zero laundry damage or linen loss incidents.
