SOPs for recruitment, onboarding, attendance, performance management, training, and administrative procedures

HR-01 Recruitment & Hiring

Objective & Scope

To establish a structured, transparent, non-discriminatory, and merit-based recruitment process compliant with Philippine Labor Code standards to source, interview, and select qualified personnel for Marcela's Resort Hotel.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • Department Head: Submits Personnel Requisition Form (PRF) with justified operational headcount needs.
  • HR Officer: Manages job postings, candidate screening, interview scheduling, and background checks.
  • General Manager (GM): Conducts final interview for supervisory/head roles and approves all hiring offers.

Execution Procedure

  1. Personnel Requisition: Department Head completes PRF detailing position title, job level, salary grade, and operational justification. PRF requires dual approval from HR Officer and GM prior to posting.
  2. Sourcing & Job Posting: Post vacancies internally (notice boards) and externally (local job portals, PESO Gamay / Northern Samar, social media pages, local university job boards in Catarman/Tacloban). Minimum posting window: 7 calendar days.
  3. Screening & Initial Interview: HR Officer screens CVs against minimum position standards (SOP-HR-04). Conduct initial structured interview (in-person or video call for non-local applicants) evaluating basic qualifications, communication skills, and service orientation.
  4. Technical / Practical Assessment & Department Interview: Shortlisted candidates undergo position-specific practical tests (e.g., mock table setup for F&B, bed-making drill for HK, practical cooking exam for Kitchen). Department Head conducts technical interview using standardized scoring rubrics (1-5 scale).
  5. Final Interview & Background Check: GM interviews top 2 candidates. HR verifies work references from prior employers (at least 2 professional references verified via phone/email).
  6. Job Offer & Appointment: HR issues formal Job Offer Letter detailing salary, employment status (probationary/contractual), benefits, start date, and reporting structure. Candidate must sign within 3 business days.

Localized Context

Community First Hiring: Priority sourcing is given to qualified residents of Gamay and neighboring municipalities in Northern Samar to foster local employment and community integration. DOLE Compliance: All recruitment activities must strictly comply with Republic Act No. 10911 (Anti-Age Discrimination in Employment Act) and DOLE regulations prohibiting unauthorized recruitment fees.

HR-02 Employee Pre-employment Requirements

Objective & Scope

To ensure all newly hired personnel complete mandatory Philippine statutory registrations, background verifications, and medical fitness assessments prior to their first official working shift.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • New Employee: Submits all required legal, medical, and statutory documents within the stipulated onboarding timeframe.
  • HR Officer: Validates authenticity of documents, creates physical/digital 201 Files, and registers employee in statutory portals.

Execution Procedure

  1. Issuance of Checklist: Upon signing the Job Offer, HR hands the candidate the Pre-employment Requirements Checklist.
  2. Mandatory Document Submission: Statutory Identification Numbers: SSS (Social Security System) Number, PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) MID Number, BIR Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) / Form 1902 or 1905. Clearances & Certifications: Valid NBI Clearance (Original), Barangay Clearance from resident barangay in Samar, PSA Birth Certificate (Copy) & Marriage Certificate (if applicable), Academic Records (Transcript of Records / Diploma / TESDA NC II Certificate), Certificate of Employment (COE) from previous employers.
  3. Medical & Health Clearances: Pre-employment Medical Examination (PEME) from an accredited clinic/hospital (CBC, Chest X-Ray, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Drug Test). Sanitary Permit / Health Card issued by the Gamay Rural Health Unit (RHU), mandatory for all F&B, Kitchen, and Housekeeping personnel.
  4. 201 File Creation: HR verifies submitted documents against originals, stamps "COPY VERIFIED WITH ORIGINAL," and files them in the employee's physical 201 File and digital HRIS directory.
  5. Statutory Enrollment: HR registers employee in SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and BIR online portals prior to first payroll run.

Localized Context

Samar RHU Coordination: Local HR staff coordinates with Gamay Rural Health Unit for sanitary permit issuance. All local hires can complete medical clearance within 2 business days at the local clinic to ensure rapid onboarding.

HR-04 Job Description & Position Standards

Objective & Scope

To define clear, standardized job descriptions, performance expectations, reporting relationships, and key performance indicators (KPIs) for every operational role at Marcela's Resort Hotel.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • HR Officer: Maintains master job description database, updates position templates, and audits role alignment.
  • Department Head: Drafts technical duties, updates operational KPIs, and reviews job duties annually.
  • Employee: Executes duties as outlined in signed Job Description.

Execution Procedure

  1. Job Description Structure: Every official Job Description must contain: Position Title, Department, Job Grade level, and Direct Supervisor. Core Purpose / Job Summary. Key Responsibilities & Daily/Weekly/Monthly Tasks. Minimum Qualifications (Education, Experience, TESDA Certifications, Physical requirements). Measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
  2. Issuance & Signing: Issued to candidate alongside employment contract on Day 1. HR and candidate review every duty clause. Candidate signs two original copies (1 copy for employee, 1 copy for 201 file).
  3. Annual Review & Updating: Reviewed annually every October during budget and operational strategy planning. Modifications require mutual discussion between Department Head, HR, and GM.
  4. Flexibility Clause Standard: All job descriptions must include the standard multi-skilling clause: "Perform other operational tasks and cross-departmental support duties assigned by management to ensure seamless resort operations, especially during peak event dates or severe weather emergencies."

Localized Context

Cross-Functional Resort Roles: Due to the 30-room boutique scale in a provincial location, staff are cross-trained (e.g., Housekeeping assisting F&B during large Function Hall events, Maintenance assisting Security during typhoon setup).

HR-05 Employee Attendance & Timekeeping

Objective & Scope

To establish accurate, fraud-proof timekeeping standards using biometric registration, manual backup logs, and strict attendance monitoring to ensure fair payroll processing and operational coverage.

Responsible Staff Roles

  • All Staff: Clock in and clock out accurately using biometric scanners for all shifts and meal breaks.
  • HR Officer / Timekeeper: Audits daily biometric logs, verifies discrepancies, and prepares payroll timecards.
  • Department Head: Approves shift logs, grace period exemptions, and official business trips.

Execution Procedure

  1. Clocking In/Out Mandate: Staff must clock in at the employee entrance biometric terminal in full uniform no earlier than 15 minutes prior to scheduled shift start. Clock out required for: Lunch/Meal break start, Lunch/Meal break end, and Shift end. Biometric Failure Protocol: If fingerprint scanner fails due to wet hands or technical outage, staff must sign the Manual Time Register co-signed by the Duty Supervisor within 1 hour.
  2. Grace Period & Tardiness Definitions: Scheduled Shift Start = T0. T0 + 1 min to 5 mins: On-time / Grace window (not penalized, but tracked). T0 + 6 mins to 15 mins: LATE (Deducted per minute from salary). T0 > 15 mins: SEVERE LATE (Deducted + subject to verbal/written warning upon repeat). T0 > 2 hours without notice: UNEXCUSED ABSENCE / HALF-DAY ABSENT.
  3. Absence Notification Protocol: Employee must notify Supervisor via direct call/SMS at least 3 hours before shift start if unable to report due to sudden illness or emergency.
  4. Timecard Audit & Reconciliation: HR pulls biometric data weekly, cross-checks against approved shift schedules, and routes Weekly Time Verification Sheets to Department Heads for approval.

Localized Context

Power & Internet Resiliency: During Samar grid outages, the biometric clock runs on internal battery backup. If completely discharged, the manual hardcopy logbook managed by Security at the staff entrance serves as official legal timekeeping.
